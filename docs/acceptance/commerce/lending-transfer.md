# Receive a donated book, lend it and recover a missing copy

A church resource table passes a donated book to its lending desk. Record the donor’s sharing choice independently of the gift or acknowledgement. The librarian selects the title and edition, assigns accession L61, records home campus and condition, and acknowledges the physical handover. Repeat the receipt with the same source identifier: exactly one copy becomes available. A second physical copy of the same edition receives a different accession.

An authorized guest borrows L61 for 14 days; another reader joins the title’s waiting list. The permitted reminder goes to the borrower’s approved destination. If the destination is shared, follow the configured confidentiality policy. Under this fixture a ready waiting list blocks renewal. The borrower reports the copy lost. Keep the loan history, mark the copy missing and keep it unavailable even if the external office reports that a replacement charge has been settled.

The borrower returns the original copy after the loss decision. Inspect its accession and condition, close the physical return once and offer the usable copy to the waiting reader for the configured pickup window. A pending reversal of a replacement charge goes to the responsible provider under its original reference. Its outcome cannot undo the physical return or create another accession. A damaged copy goes to repair and the waiting reader stays queued.

Test simultaneous claim attempts during the waiting reader’s pickup window: one current loan may be issued for this copy and only to the eligible claimant. A second campus must see the appropriate availability and transfer route without seeing private borrower or donor details. Verify a reader’s self-service account exposes their own loan and reminders only.
