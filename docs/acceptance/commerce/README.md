# Commerce and operations acceptance

Run each rehearsal in a fresh church and book. Trace the same physical unit, money and authorization across the desks involved. A matching grand total alone can hide a transaction posted to the wrong campus, owner or purpose.

| Rehearsal | Question for the implementer | Closing observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Purchase, receipt and late freight](purchase-to-counter.md) | How do purchasing, receiving, the counter and payables agree when a damaged delivery is invoiced in full? | Fifteen units cost 135.00, seven delivered units cost 63.00 and the cancelled purchase balance is zero. |
| [Rental inspection and deposit](facility-rental.md) | Who can apply a refundable deposit to approved damage after the space has been released? | A 25.00 assessment and a 125.00 repayment discharge the 150.00 deposit. The hire and the repair remain separately traceable. |
| [Publisher stock at an event](publisher-counter.md) | Can the church remit to the owner, accept a later return and settle the resulting balance? | The closing four publisher copies carry zero church inventory value. The publisher receives net 84.00 across two periods. |
| [Bookstore to lending desk](lending-transfer.md) | What happens when a retail copy becomes a lending copy and later goes missing? | The same physical copy carries one accession on recovery. Its return triggers a 20.00 repayment to the borrower under the agreed policy. |
| [Cartons, outreach parcels and returns](outreach-packs.md) | Can the steward reconcile individual handovers to the original pack units while protecting recipients? | Twenty-four cartons contain 144 packs. Eighty-seven reach recipients, three are written off and 54 remain held. |

For each rehearsal select a separate operator for every role named in the setup. Where a small office combines roles, exercise the configured compensating review as a separate action with an identifiable reviewer. Monetary examples use one reporting currency, two decimal places, documented fixture policies and synthetic identifiers. Dates and rates in the examples are local test inputs.

## Reading results

1. Retain the original source identity and business date at each desk. Export one row per source line and accepted action with actor, effective time, recorded time, organization, current state and authorized trace reference.
2. Compare the quantity observations at the named cutoff before comparing values. In-transit, third-party, quarantined and reserved units have different effects on availability and carrying value.
3. Reperform each named interruption from its stated checkpoint. Retry the same action with the same identity and compare the count and value of accepted downstream effects. Then try the specified distinct action that resembles the original.
4. Compare the views named in the rehearsal using separate accounts. A successful access restriction covers the search result, drill-down, attachment, export and notification for that actor.
5. Preserve the original and corrective documents. Rebuild the earlier cutoff from dated facts, then rebuild the closing cutoff including the correction.
