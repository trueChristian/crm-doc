# Hire a hall, inspect it and resolve its deposit

Use an approved Saturday hall booking from 10:00 to 12:00, with a 30-minute reset. The external provider reports receipt of the required 150 deposit against booking H12 in the church’s configured two-decimal currency. Church staff record the provider reference and the deposit requirement as satisfied. The facilities view displays only the operational state. At 09:50 the custodian issues key K3 to the named hirer and records the hall and equipment condition.

At 12:05 the hirer returns K3. One projector cable is damaged. Record the actual key return, open a work order with the item tag, and attach restricted inspection evidence. A staff member with the required authority releases the room after reset and makes the damaged item unavailable. A second booking at 12:20 overlaps reset; reject it. A 12:30 booking can proceed with safe equipment even while the external adjustment is pending.

An authorized reviewer accepts a 25 damage assessment. The provider confirms a 125 repayment and separately references the 25 retained. The CRM records the scoped outcomes against H12 for the responsible office. Twice importing the 125 notice leaves one acknowledgement and one request. A provider or accounting-system outage leaves the inspection, room availability and key return intact. If a notice refers to H12 at another church, reject the mapping without leaking the booking or changing its status.

The hirer can see the authorized inspection outcome and deposit status. A volunteer arranging the next event can see whether the room and projector are usable. Damage photographs, renter contacts, fee amounts and the reviewer's deliberations are restricted to the roles that need them.
