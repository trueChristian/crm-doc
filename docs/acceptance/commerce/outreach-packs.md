# Count and distribute outreach packs

A supplying partner sends 24 sealed cartons for a church relief day. Its dispatch says six packs per carton. The receiving steward counts all 24, verifies the unit conversion, and accepts 144 packs into project custody. A provider's shipment confirmation by itself cannot establish arrival. Record shortages or damaged arrivals against the specific dispatch and quarantine uncertain units before release.

| Movement in this rehearsal | Units | Evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Received from partner | 144 | Signed count and dispatch reference |
| Handed to verified or anonymously counted recipients | 87 | Completed receipt or authorized distribution tally for each nonoverlapping issue |
| Spoiled and approved for disposal | 3 | Condition report and independent witness required by this church’s policy |
| Held after return of unused field supplies | 54 | Field issue/return receipts and depot count |

A steward transfers 100 units to a field team. The field team distributes 87, identifies three spoiled units and returns ten usable units. The depot held 44 and now holds 54. Match issue and return at both desks: 87 + 3 + 54 = 144. The coordinator can report coverage in packs; a pack count gives no independent count of households or members.

Repeat a disconnected return message for ten units and a distribution receipt under the same identity. Depot custody must remain 54. A new unseen receipt with the same number of packs but conflicting source evidence needs review. If the field team counts nine usable returns, record one unit outstanding, identify the last signed handover and withhold full reconciliation. Keep the confirmed 87 distributed and three spoiled.

The supplier receives an agreed aggregate or anonymous acknowledgment. Recipient eligibility evidence and safe-contact information remain restricted to the relief team. Joining a future church activity requires its own invitation or consent.
