# From a church class or external attestation to a register review

Congregation A invites Ari to a local commissioning review. Congregation B operates a similar Sunday class and uses its roster only for attendance. A church can run either policy with its own staff, with or without a connected LMS.

## Arrange the fixture

| Fact | Starting value |
| --- | --- |
| Church A | One pending ceremony request and one preparation requirement. Sol can verify the requirement and confirm a local entry. Em leads the class and can mark attendance. Pat handles unresolved external identity mappings. |
| Church B | No open preparation review and no sharing agreement for A's subject. Its separate connection has a learner with the same external numeric key. |
| Issuer | A verified connection and subject mapping for A. The church authorized a single preparation attestation. The issuer sends a subject key, requirement, outcome, revision and applicability dates. |
| Formal register | Policy edition 2 requires one principal, an authorized officiant, a witness and occurrence evidence. The open book already contains entries through number 140. Ren is a witness without a directory account. |

## Exercise the desk and the integration

| Action | Observable result |
| --- | --- |
| Ari attends twice, once as a guest and once as a term participant. Em submits the second observation again after losing connectivity. | Two dated attendance facts and one term participation. Reconciliation shows two visits and one distinct person. The preparation requirement remains pending. |
| Staff record a paper issuer confirmation under A's approved manual procedure with the external service disabled. | Sol can review the document's provenance, subject, issuer, validity and purpose. A can complete its review at the desk. No external service, course roster or score is created in the CRM. Reset the requirement before exercising the electronic path. |
| The external service sends confirmed attestation X, revision 4, to A twice. | One accepted business outcome and one linked review of the pending requirement. A privacy-filtered operator acknowledgement identifies the outcome. The register is still at 140 and membership has not changed. |
| A message refers to B's namespace, an unknown person, a revoked grant or an unconfigured purpose. | Do not apply A's evidence to any person. A permitted operator can see the safe reason and resolve or reject it. B has zero new local rite reviews. |
| The issuer sends X revision 4 under the same message key with a different subject. It later redelivers revision 3. | Quarantine the conflict. The older notice cannot rewind the current decision. Escalate identity changes for direct confirmation. A roster search cannot discover this correspondence. |
| Sol verifies A's requirement. Em subsequently reschedules the ceremonial service because the room closes. | Show the dated preparation decision, reschedule the affected occurrence and bookings, and tell affected participants through their permitted routes. The service still requires independent evidence of performance. |
| The issuer withdraws X before the ceremony. | Reopen the affected review, notify Sol, and show the requirement as disputed until a fresh decision. A duplicate withdrawal opens no second review. For the next branch, obtain corrected source evidence and a fresh authorized local decision. |

## Ceremony, entry and issue

The ceremony eventually takes place. Ari is the principal, an approved guest officiates, and Ren witnesses. The officiant's confirmation is available to Sol. All dates are precise on this path. The same book will also receive another independent local entry.

| Record or command | Check before committing | Result to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare Ari's RiteRecord | Definition version 2 allows performed_here. The registrar has actual occurrence evidence, its place, date precision and the allowed participant roles. | One pending review referencing the local occurrence evidence. The named guest and witness can remain unlinked to a directory account. |
| Validate participant values | Only fields belonging to that definition version can be accepted. Required roles are present and the allowed value type and visibility of each configured field apply. | Ren appears as witness under the authorized register entry. Being a witness gives no access to a learning file, church directory or extract. |
| Confirm Ari and independently confirm the other local entry | Two clerks concurrently request entry into the same open book. Their request identities are distinct. | Current numbers are 141 and 142 in whichever allocation order wins. A clerk's retry returns its own original outcome. The two principals never share a number. |
| Issue Ari's authorized extract | Check disclosure authority for the requested recipient, current register status and approved template revision. | One CertificateIssue records the original name snapshot, issue number, recipient, authority and issued artifact. The external attestation remains identified by its issuer. |
| Correct Ren's name | Sol reviews documentary evidence, links corrected record and amendment, retains original wording and verifies book scope. | The original book number remains reserved and traceable. Issue a replacement extract only under disclosure authority and link its replaces_id to the prior issue. Narrow recipients see only the permitted wording. |
| Request an extract as the class leader | Class leadership alone supplies no disclosure authority for the register. | No new issue, artifact, witness information or revealing search result is produced. Record the access decision under the applicable policy. |
| Enter a year-only attestation from another church | The local definition permits attested_elsewhere. Confirm external source, year precision and evidence. | Historic record preserves its entered provenance. The local-performed count remains two. No synthetic ceremony day, service occurrence or local officiant is invented. |
| Record a second event with similar participant names | Evaluate definition repeat_rule and assigned reviewer. The local fixture requires review of a possible repeat. | Pending review may link a verified duplicate, accept a distinct occurrence or reject it with reason. A name match alone cannot invalidate either original. |

## Correct a mistaken attribution after issue

A later issuer correction identifies a different person. Hold affected pending decisions and send the registrar the minimum review notice. If the ceremony really involved Ari, the register keeper retains that evidence and records the resolution. If independent evidence shows a mistaken register entry, Sol authorizes an amendment, reviews the already-issued extracts and records their disposition. Processing the issuer notice itself cannot amend an entry or expose another person's identity.

Reconcile A's two local entries and separate external attestation. A class leader can inspect permitted attendance history and has no authority to issue an extract. B sees no record about Ari. Neither congregation admitted a member during this fixture.
