# Verify church audiences at each live destination

East and West hold services at 09:00. East has a public opening on the church site, a teaching item from 09:10 to 09:30 authorized for one public relay, and a testimony at 09:30 for people in the room only. West uses the same sermon title but permits only the members' church audience. Seed two independent occurrences, approved service orders, rights decisions, speakers and recipients. Leave a third destination disabled. The church registers the authorized destinations and audience, and the streaming provider operates each stream.

| Action | Church desk and audience evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Publish the public calendar and waiting page at 08:40. Start the provider's private source test. | The approved East title and start time appear without publishing the test. Search and public calendar feeds cannot reveal West's private details. The producer sees separate service identities. |
| Submit West's start notification using East's external stream identifier. | Record an unresolved provider mismatch under the correct church connection. No audience permission, actual start or other service is altered. |
| Start East on the church website at 09:00. Enable its public relay at 09:10 according to the service cue. | Record a per-destination acknowledgement and observation. A public visitor can see only the approved item. An authorized member can access West separately. The third destination receives no request. |
| Disconnect East's public relay at 09:18 while East's church player and West's members-only destination continue. | The operator sees one failed destination and its last confirmed state. A retry uses the original service and destination keys. Restarting East's relay cannot publish West. |
| Withdraw the public relay permission at 09:25. The provider is temporarily unavailable. | Stop the church-controlled route and invoke the agreed provider stop or manual cutoff. Restrict the local listing immediately. Show downstream withdrawal pending until independently confirmed. A previous “live” delivery acknowledgement cannot clear the withdrawal. |
| Start East's private testimony at 09:30. | The public and church-web audiences must be excluded from this item. If exclusion cannot be confirmed, stop sending that source or delay the restricted item. A church-approved holding slide or ending message may be shown under its own authorization. |
| End both services and deliver a duplicate end notification after a delayed start observation. | Each occurrence has its own final status, interruption and unresolved exceptions. No duplicate start, archive release, attendance or follow-up is created. |

Keep enough evidence for the church to identify service, audience, approved use, provider connection, binding revision, times and unresolved action. Keep keys and protected source locations out of roster exports and ordinary account history. Anonymous view totals from a provider are observations of devices or sessions. Reconcile them separately from named church attendance.
