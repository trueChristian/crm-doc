# Offering and donor confidence

Use a single church and currency unless a case changes them. Roles include a counting team, giving clerk, reviewer, courier, donor and ministry leader. The local policy admits verified cash and cheque gifts on the received date and reflects a later return at its effective date. Each case starts independently.

## 1. Mixed custody and an unresolved difference

1. Record the church, service and sealed bag S10. Count three envelopes. A contains 120 cash split 90 General and 30 Youth. B contains an 80 Missions cheque. Unidentified C declares 50 but contains 47 cash with no designation. The church's approved default for unmarked gifts is General. Enter two independent count rounds with those same items.
2. Verify that the supervisor sees two rounds, declared total 250 and counted total 247. Selecting the verified round once produces cash 167 and cheque 80. The discrepancy of 3 retains the envelope, reviewer and explanation. Posting an offline sheet again using its original identity leaves the selected round and totals unchanged.
3. Hand those tenders to the receiver. The seal is damaged and the receiver reports only 164 cash plus the cheque. Expect a custody difference of 3, tied to the transfer, alongside the earlier envelope difference of 3. A clerk can see that 247 was verified, 244 was acknowledged by the recipient and the transfer awaits disposition. A second count or delivery confirmation cannot erase the original evidence.
4. Filter to Youth. Its authorized leader sees only the disclosure-approved 30 in the summary. Check the same leader's access to A by identifier, name search, export and application read. None exposes A's identity, envelope, correspondence or donor-level activity. Restrict combinations of small-group, service or campus filters where these would identify a confidential donor under the local disclosure policy.

## 2. Truly anonymous, unassigned and private giving

Record an anonymous loose gift of 10. Record an envelope of 20 with a donor code the clerk cannot yet resolve. Record a third gift of 30 from a verified donor who requests public anonymity, permits private correspondence and declines fundraising contact. The approved aggregate is 60. Public output carries no personal names. Only the identified 30 can appear on a named statement at this point.

Two people claim the 20. The clerk opens a private review and does not expose either person's other gifts. Verify that no statement includes this 20 pending resolution. A reviewer then confirms one claimant with adequate evidence under the church's procedure. The current statement for the verified claimant gains 20 and the change is traceable. The truly anonymous 10 still has no named recipient. Totals remain 60 and replaying the approved identification creates no fourth gift. A fundraising export excludes the donor who declined solicitation even though the giving desk can deliver that donor's statement.

## 3. Joint and personal giving at one address

Set up adults J and K at the same address with separate portal identities. J gives 40 individually, K gives 30 individually and both authorize a joint 50 to Youth. The joint reporting instruction covers only the 50 and designates both as permitted recipients. A 20 from an organization carries J as signatory and the organization as donor.

| Query | Expected result |
| --- | --- |
| J's individual statement | 40. The organization and joint amounts are separately attributed. |
| K's individual statement | 30. |
| Joint statement under the mandate | 50 once for the joint identity. Each authorized recipient can receive that same edition. |
| Organization correspondence | 20, delivered only to a contact specifically authorized for organization correspondence. |
| Giving total across all four gifts | 140. Adding the two physical copies of the joint statement contributes no money. |

End joint delivery authority for future gifts and record J giving a further individual 15. J's individual history becomes 55. K sees no portion of the later 15. Access to an earlier joint document follows the applicable authority to retrieve that edition. Shared address, former access or signatory status alone cannot grant current access.

## 4. Return, remaining purpose and prior statements

Verify reference G80 for 80 Missions, produce dated statement D1 and record delivery. Import a confirmed return for all 80 with source R80 the following day. Preserve original gift, count and D1. Current Missions total from this gift becomes zero. A current statement or correction under the fixture's policy shows the original and linked adjustment or their disclosed net. The corrected edition identifies its predecessor. Resending D1 records delivery of D1. An explicit correction produces a new edition.

Import R80 again, then a fee-only notice for 5. The history still has one gift, one economic return and current gift total zero. The fee does not change D1, the donor's giving or the restriction. If the return instead references a pledge installment fulfilled by the gift, the unfulfilled installment increases by the reversed amount previously applied to it. A duplicate return cannot reopen it a second time.
