# Commission a church app, inbound service and MCP client together

The church secretary, ministry leader and app integrator rehearse this run with synthetic data. Record source revisions, identities of each acting principal, disposition, domain effects and visible fields. Inspect results through each participating client and an independent authorized staff view. The operation names below describe business actions. The installed system publishes the corresponding API operations and MCP tool names.

## Fixture

- Church North and Church South each use external person number 014. They have separate organizations and namespaces. North has one published retreat occurrence R with one unallocated seat. Only North has an upcoming unpublished service U.
- Jo is an adult with one North member account. Jo can register North child Sam for R until 10:00 UTC on 20 April 2027, exclusive. Jo has no grant for another child Kim. South and North both contain “Sam Lee”.
- North's app grants authenticated registration and self-service. A dedicated website intake connection can submit contact proposals. The welcome steward can resolve them. Only the membership clerk can decide formal admission. North's MCP client initially has scoped read and propose access for leader Anne. In this fixture an outstanding proposal requires the client’s propose grant to remain valid through approval.
- The attendance subscriber is online. The public calendar subscriber is one event behind. A public visitor may see R's published fields only. North's learning and accounting connections are optional and start disabled.

## Exercise the interfaces

| Step | Submit or observe | Expected result and independent check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Anonymous directory search and change feed request all services at North. | R appears with its published edition. U contributes no label, private room, attendee, total or pagination hint. A user cannot obtain a wider result by requesting another field projection or switching to MCP. |
| 2 | Jo requests available participants for R before the expiry. The same caller requests Kim by known ID and tries South's 014. | The picker includes the permitted Sam in North. Neither direct lookup yields protected fields or existence clues for Kim or South's record. |
| 3 | At 09:40 Jo submits one seat request for Sam under key A7. The app loses connectivity after server commit. In a race, a second eligible account requests the remaining seat. | Configure the fixture so A7 commits first. Sam holds the single confirmed seat. The other participant receives the configured waiting-list or capacity refusal. The church can identify both outcomes. |
| 4 | Jo reconnects at 09:42 and retries A7 with the same content. Then retry A7 with another participant while Jo still has a valid grant for the original request. | The original outcome is returned for the identical authorized retry. Reuse with changed content produces a conflict or a permission-safe refusal according to current authority. One seat and one attendance-roster change exist. No duplicate confirmation is sent. |
| 5 | At exactly 10:00, try a new guardian write and read the child's private booking details from an earlier link. | The expired representation grant no longer suffices for either operation. The staff roster retains the historical confirmed seat. Jo's independent personal account still works. The app clears or conceals now-inaccessible cached details. |
| 6 | North's intake connection submits a proposed address change under I9 for its mapped person 014. Immediately retry. A different sender also uses I9 and has its own authorized namespace. | The first connection receives one pending steward decision and cannot publish the change. The second connection's independently submitted request cannot bind to the first receipt. No update appears on South's 014. |
| 7 | Before the steward approves, the person corrects the address in the member app. The steward opens the older proposal, checks revisions and declines the superseded value. The intake service polls. | The member's newer revision survives. The sender can see the disposition of its submission, with only information permitted for that connection. A new proposal can cite the current authorized revision. The audit identifies sender, member and reviewer separately. |
| 8 | As Anne, discover MCP operations and view the permitted roster. Inspect what a change would do, then submit a scoped roster proposal under operation M2. Replay M2 through the API under the same effective actor and client identity. | Discovery exposes only permitted operations. Inspection has zero effects. One proposal and one receipt exist across both transports. Required independent approval stays pending. Unrelated contact details, safety notes and private services stay outside the response. |
| 9 | Revoke the client's propose grant after preview and before a fresh execution. Attempt the remembered tool call directly and subscribe to its protected result. Then ask an independently authorized reviewer to approve M2. | Fresh execution is denied. A revoked grant cannot deliver protected results. The custodian rechecks M2's current authority and exact target edition. The missing grant invalidates the outstanding proposal in this fixture. No roster change commits. The recorder keeps the original requester distinct from the reviewer. |
| 10 | Publish an approved venue change for R. The calendar consumer retries after a timeout. Repeat while the accounting and LMS connections remain disabled. | The public service retains one current venue and the calendar applies one change. The private roster subscriber need not receive the calendar payload. Each subscription keeps its own checkpoint. Jo's earlier booking and independent church services remain available. |

## Failure variations

| Variation from a fresh fixture | Evidence required |
| --- | --- |
| An external app sends a registration together with an unapproved membership-status field. | Reject the forbidden field with the contract's documented atomicity. In an atomic registration command no seat is consumed. Where batch items are independent, a separate valid item keeps its receipt. There is no membership change in either case. |
| Two trusted systems independently submit “Sam Lee” without a verified crosswalk or authority to create a member. | Each receives a scoped intake receipt if intake is permitted. One steward checks the candidate set. A name match alone cannot grant access, merge records, add a household adult or disclose the candidates to either sender. |
| Accounting or LMS stays offline through the retreat. | Compare R, seats, attendance and public calendar with the online baseline. A configured outbound handoff remains pending under its custodian. No course result, ledger acknowledgement or donor statement is inferred from the outage. |
| A service sends an unknown contract edition, then the same key under a supported edition with materially different content. | Record the unsupported message and reason according to the connection contract. Preserve its evidence. Correct under the authorized supersession procedure. Never overwrite an existing authoritative result by silently changing an operation's meaning. |
| Revoke an event subscription during retry and resume later after explicit reauthorization. | Its backlog stops while a separately entitled subscription proceeds. Reevaluate permitted fields at delivery. Resume only the authorized interval or establish a fresh scoped baseline if its replay window expired. |
| Private content returned by authorized search includes a request to send a roster externally. | Viewing the content confers no authority to send it. No delivery is recorded. A separate approved communication must identify recipients, purpose and current permissions. |

Pass evidence includes one successful church app journey, one inbound proposal and its terminal outcome, and an MCP request trace showing client, actor, domain authority, target edition and result. Compare record counts, current revisions, audiences, suppressed deliveries, pending handoffs and foreign organization visibility against the expected states. Keep private source values out of commissioning logs.
