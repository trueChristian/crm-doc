# A memorial booking, corrected identity and burial capacity

An office receives a request to hold a memorial for a former attendee. Two people share the recorded name. The caretaker has reserved space for a possible interment. The registrar, keeper of the burial ground, membership clerk and editor each establish their own facts.

## Initial facts

The office receives request Q9 on 3 March. ID P12 belongs to one living member and P21 to the former attendee. They have identical display names, separate households and distinct verified local identifiers. The family permits a private callback and a closed gathering. On the evidence supplied at intake, staff cannot distinguish the subject by identifier. The church's example register policy requires actual occurrence and independent verification before entry. A site visit, payment and booking provide no such evidence.

Burial space B3 has capacity three units. One unit is already occupied. One exclusive right BR6 reserves exactly one additional unit and has not been exercised. There are no other reservations. Starting availability is one uncommitted unit. A memorial may occur without an interment.

## Failure and correction branches

1. **Ceremony cancelled before it occurs.** Reset to M02, cancel with the actual time and release the related event resources under the agreement. Entries for rite, interment and certificate remain zero. Releasing a hall booking has no effect on BR6. Never allocate register number 19 for the cancellation.
2. **Simultaneous independent grave reservations.** Reset to M01. Two applicants each seek one additional unit of B3. One authorized grant succeeds against the last free unit and the other receives unavailable on reread. The totals are one occupied and two unused reserved, leaving zero free. The longstanding holder of BR6 still has one reserved unit.
3. **Wrong subject entered in a draft.** Reset to M03 with a draft ceremony subject incorrectly bound to P12. The reviewer corrects the association to P21 and records why. Check pending mail, public drafts, tasks and any external sends by reference. A draft link alone must have produced zero death decisions, death notices and registration outputs for P12.
4. **Wrong subject on an already issued document.** An erroneous issued certificate undergoes authorized withdrawal/correction and replacement under the institution's issue policy. Link the replacement to the prior issue and preserve its old wording and original source reference under restricted access. Notify only previously authorized recipients using verified safe routes. Never backdate the correction or substitute a new artifact under the old issue identifier.
5. **Cemetery location entered incorrectly.** The authority shows that the actual interment occurred at B4. B4 has capacity and the custodian separately verifies where the right may be exercised. Record the approved amendment and reconcile each affected space against the validated physical location and rights. Do not infer movement of remains from a clerical correction or transfer BR6 to B4 merely because B4 appears in the amendment.
6. **Directory search following verification.** Evaluate P21's visibility under the church's directory and life-state policy. P12's legitimate appearance, existing memberships and subscriptions remain tied to P12. An ordinary directory user cannot retrieve P21's private callback, burial authorization or a living person's name from a restricted adjacent book entry.
