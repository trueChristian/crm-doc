# People, care and governance handover tests

Use these exercises when two departments can individually pass their own rules while collectively reporting the wrong recipient, person, authority, headcount or status. Every date, authority, scope and threshold in the examples is fictional. Each church can exercise its own adopted rules by recording separate expected results. Use synthetic people and attachments.

| Exercise | Starting question | Evidence to compare after the final step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [One physical handover across two desks](collection-and-reunification.md) | Can a current protective restriction defeat an obsolete list during a continuity exercise? | One release and nine present from an initial ten, with the restricted reasoning visible only to its owner. |
| [Pastoral cover, referral delivery and staff return](pastoral-cover-and-referral.md) | Can a substitute perform agreed work without inheriting the coordinator's entire caseload? | The scoped assignment, two distinct visit outcomes, one acknowledged referral and the withdrawal time. |
| [Petition endorsement after a membership correction](petition-and-certified-roll.md) | Can a reviewer resolve a historical class error after the petition closed? | Six submissions, three accepted rights and one proposed agenda item, with both membership evidence editions recoverable. |
| [Adopted policy, staff acknowledgement and coverage](policy-change-and-duty-coverage.md) | Which workers actually satisfy a new duty rule for the complete shift? | Two eligible required workers at 18:00 as known then, with the later evidence correction separately attributable. |
| [A memorial booking, corrected identity and burial capacity](memorial-and-register.md) | What does the office know before a name is linked or an interment occurs? | One memorial entry, one independently authorized use of one reserved unit, and no changed rights for a living namesake. |
| [Independent intake, evidence custody and overlapping holds](protected-evidence-and-holds.md) | Can the alternate receive a case, release a redacted copy and demonstrate why the source was retained? | The independent acknowledgment, original and working identities, one external acknowledgment, custody trail and two separately released holds. |

Run the main script first and reset to the stated starting point for each counterfactual. Record observations from both owning departments and one deliberately unauthorized operator. A successful job or notification alone cannot substantiate a receipt, physical handover, review decision or record of attendance. For individual policy boundaries and many more examples, use the [people decision cases](../../decisions/people/README.md) and [governance decision cases](../../decisions/governance/README.md).
