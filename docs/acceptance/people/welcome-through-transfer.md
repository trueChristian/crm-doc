# A returning visitor, two interests and an external transfer

Exercise the front desk, welcome team, registrar, communications operator and receiving church separately. Use fictitious records. This church requires an approved admission and a verified destination acknowledgment before its clerk authorizes a later outgoing transfer. The transfer is effective on the date approved by the clerk. The church has one formal member in the starting roll, Jo. Jo and guest Ren share a telephone. Jo allows general notices and Ren asks to receive information about a service by email. No participant has authorized a home visit or automatic directory publication.

| Step | Action | Expected records and visible result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Ren brings a child to a gathering. The door team records the party in the total attendance and the welcome worker accepts one card from Ren. Ren asks about the next service and helping at a meal. | The welcome worker records one named adult encounter and two OutreachInterest records. The attendance owner keeps its count under the actual counting method. The card contributes no additional attendance, named child, or permission concerning the child. Formal roll total stays one. |
| 2 | A connector times out after submitting the card and retries the same source and intake reference. A volunteer claims the meal task while a second volunteer tries to claim the same work. | The connector receives its original result. One encounter and two interests remain. One worker owns the meal request. A changed body under the same key goes to conflict resolution without changing Ren or Jo. |
| 3 | The welcome worker answers the service question using Ren’s approved route. Ren asks the worker to introduce them to the meal coordinator but supplies no authority for general announcements. | The service interest can close as fulfilled. The meal coordinator receives the agreed limited referral. Ticket delivery and owner acceptance are separately recorded. No new subscription or recurring campaign audience results. |
| 4 | The receiving coordinator declines the handoff because they are on leave. Ren then withdraws the meal request at the desk before a reminder goes out. | Before withdrawal the welcome team could reoffer the referral to an authorized alternate. After withdrawal there is no unowned open promise and the queued reminder is suppressed. The service reply stays in the completed history. Jo’s independent subscriptions and the shared number remain unchanged. |
| 5 | Ren returns six weeks later and asks about membership. A clerk locates the verified identity and creates an individual application. Two clerks try to complete the approved decision. | There are two encounters on different dates for one person. Starting another request never lifts the withdrawn purpose. Exactly one membership and first period result from admission. The formal roll now counts Jo and Ren. The child has no membership decision. |
| 6 | Ren requests transfer to an independent church. The clerk checks authority, prepares an approved extract and sends it. The receiving church’s email server acknowledges delivery. | The local clerk sees an issued transfer awaiting organizational acknowledgment. The destination receives only the approved member and transfer facts. Household roster, contact preferences, giving, pastoral notes, protected information and church accounts are excluded. The local formal count is still two. |
| 7 | The receiving clerk acknowledges for the correct transfer edition and church. The source clerk verifies authority and approves the exit effective 1 September. A retry and a duplicate paper acknowledgment follow. | There is one transfer and one effective exit decision. Local roll counts two on 31 August and one on 1 September with this knowledge. Only the destination can make its own admission decision. The same person’s prior attendance remains visible to its authorized owner. |

## Branches to run from the indicated step

* **Different person at intake.** Repeat step 2 with a separate card signed by Jo giving the shared telephone and a different source reference. The steward can compare records within scope. One shared endpoint cannot merge the identities, transfer Ren's permissions or combine their interests.
* **Missing acknowledgment.** After step 3 a provider accepts a message but no ministry worker accepts the referral before its deadline. The sending owner still has an overdue handoff and a safe way to resolve it. A report may count one completed response and one unanswered referral.
* **Change after dispatch.** After step 6 Ren requests cancellation before local approval. The clerk marks the transfer cancelled, tries to recall or supersede the issued extract and logs the destination’s response. A late acknowledgment of that edition is retained for investigation and cannot close a membership period.
* **Further disclosure requested.** After step 6 the recipient asks where Ren's child lives. The transfer authority supplies no basis for that request. Keep the local transfer under review if needed and log any separately assessed request outside the transfer package. An external identifier is never a directory or account grant.
* **Ownership ends.** After step 4 the volunteer leaves the welcome team. Their task list, mobile lookup and later export cannot retrieve Ren's work. A retained printed sheet follows the church's custody and disposal policy. A case-specific entitlement independently granted for another role is examined under that role's purpose.
