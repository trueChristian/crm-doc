# Adopted policy, staff acknowledgement and coverage at the door

A governing body changes the training standard for a two-person welcome shift. The clerk can demonstrate adoption, the administrator can demonstrate when the control became effective, and the duty lead can demonstrate who was eligible to serve the whole shift. Each record has a different owner.

## Example adoption

| Boundary | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| 5 May | A confirmed resolution adopts edition P2 of the volunteer induction policy. P1 remains effective through 31 May. P2 takes effect on 1 June and applies to welcome leads at both campuses. |
| 15 May | Under the governing body's rule, all existing welcome leads are assigned an affirmative acknowledgement of P2. A material change needs a fresh acknowledgement even from people who signed P1. |
| 25 May | An authorized administrator approves the implementing configuration, scoped to the two campuses, effective on 1 June at 00:00 local. Under the example policy the duty is eligible only with both P2 acknowledgement and current training covering the entire shift. |
| 1 June | A shift runs from 18:00 to 20:00 at the north campus. It requires two confirmed workers. A standby counts only after an authorized move into a required slot. No qualification waivers are enabled. |

The clerk records adoption in [GovernancePolicyEdition](../../implementation/governance/GovernancePolicyEdition.md), linked to the resolution and predecessor P1. The administrator records the separately authorized [SettingEdition](../../implementation/platform/SettingEdition.md). The duty owner applies the local [PositionRequirement](../../implementation/ministry/PositionRequirement.md). If the approved control is absent or fails to resolve at the effective boundary, route the affected decision to the designated owner. Do not silently certify compliance or fall back to the old requirement.

## Individual results at the 18:00 decision

| Worker | Acknowledgement and credential | Duty decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| J | Signed P1 only. Training through 30 June. | Ineligible for the required shift until P2 is acknowledged. P1's historical acknowledgement remains accessible. |
| K | P2 witnessed on paper and recorded against K. Training ends 1 June at 19:00. | Ineligible for the full 18:00–20:00 shift. Past attendance cannot extend training. A split duty would require separately approved shifts and qualified cover for each. |
| L | Acknowledged P2. Training valid for the entire duty. Accepted a required slot. | Count one. The readiness view may show verified status and validity for the shift. Access to the restricted certificate stays with the credential owner. |
| M | Acknowledged P2. Training valid for the entire duty. Accepted standby. | Available as standby. Initially contributes zero to required coverage. |
| N | Holds verified training and reports opening the P2 document. It requires affirmation and N supplied none. | Zero toward coverage. Viewing a document does not populate the required affirmation time or evidence. |

## Execute through the normal desks

1. An accepting officer records K's paper [PolicyAcknowledgement](../../implementation/governance/PolicyAcknowledgement.md). A later assisted entry for the same person and edition finds the existing acknowledgement. One person-edition binding remains. Preserve the actual evidence and the attributable intake.
2. Before 1 June, historical readiness checks for May continue to resolve P1. A later re-run with the May event time and original knowledge reproduces the saved decision, even though today's roster shows P2.
3. At 17:45, one of two required places has eligible confirmed coverage and one eligible standby exists. The lead sees a shortage of one. A printout of people assigned to the team or a list filtered solely by P2 acknowledgment cannot attest that the shift is ready.
4. At 17:50, two dispatchers try to move M into the same required duty from different stale views. Apply one slot change to M's [VolunteerAssignment](../../implementation/ministry/VolunteerAssignment.md), or return the already-applied result for a retry. The second dispatcher re-reads. Two eligible required workers and zero standby remain. No person counts twice.
5. At 17:55, M withdraws. Required coverage returns to one. J then affirms P2 using an approved assisted route and accepts the newly offered required slot. J's [VolunteerCredential](../../implementation/ministry/VolunteerCredential.md) covers the duty. Only after the acknowledgement and accepted assignment does the count return to two.
6. At 18:05, K produces a renewed certificate with a new validity period beginning 2 June. The credential reviewer may accept the document for future duties. The 2 June start does not repair the 1 June coverage gap in K's original qualification. No change to the two counted workers follows.
7. A reviewer learns that J's P2 acknowledgement was attributed to the wrong person. Enter the authorized correction with the evidence and original attribution retained. Reassess eligibility for the affected duty. Flag the mismatch against what the lead knew at 18:00 and require a human determination of actual service. Do not rewrite who physically attended or backfill a signature for the other person.

## Failure isolation

- A policy administrator can read P2 and its acknowledgment counts but cannot inspect training evidence through those counts. A credential reviewer cannot amend P2's adoption date by reissuing someone's credential.
- A volunteer at the south campus later leaves that position while retaining a separate unrelated appointment. Stop reminders for the departed role after its effective end. Re-evaluate any remaining in-scope role before stopping that person's P2 obligations.
- A typo in P2's public summary can be corrected by the authorized publisher. A substantive change to the affirmation is a new policy decision and edition with its own affected population. The earlier signed wording and digest remain recoverable.
