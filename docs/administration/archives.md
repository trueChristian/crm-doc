# Documents, archives, certificates and custody

A church may hold a signed minute book, land deed, printed register, insurance policy, marriage record or photograph whose use is limited. Separate the fact in the owning register from the physical original, authorized digital copy and extract issued to a particular recipient. A preserved record can have an intentionally limited description in public finding aids.

A digital ArchiveCopy binds one immutable [DocumentRevision](../platform/configuration-content.md) to its preservation and custody record. A rite-specific [CertificateIssue](../people/rites-and-milestones.md) uses the same issue serial and one DocumentIssue for that release; a minute-specific [OfficialExtract](../governance/minutes-and-resolutions.md) does the same. Generating a new printable rendition of an already issued copy never allocates another serial. DocumentIssue records issue and revocation; the rite and minute records own the wording and authority.

**Access and certification.** A member requesting baptism evidence goes through the register custodian. The custodian verifies the requester or delegate, selects the verified rite occurrence, produces an extract and records its serial and recipient. Correction of the original register entry can revoke the affected issue and produce a replacement. A public verification screen must not offer name lookup, family discovery or links to the original register. A photo cleared for an internal anniversary exhibition still requires a separate publication decision before appearing online.

**Reports and acceptance.** List overdue loans, items with uncertain custody, preservation checks due, records under hold, and issued copies affected by a correction. Two concurrent loan requests for the same bound volume result in one accepted loan. A document locked by a hold remains available to its authorized custodian beyond the normal disposal date. A former board member cannot use an old digital pack link to read currently restricted minutes. A clerk can identify which physical volume and page support a certificate without searching all household records.
