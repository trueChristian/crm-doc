# Dated rolls, denominational returns and annual reports

A clerk prepares a return for a defined reporting perimeter and date. Leaders need to know whether “120 members” means 120 unique persons at year-end, 120 concurrent enrolments, or an estimated headcount. Store the meaning of every line and the data available when it was signed. A network can receive certified totals from cooperating organizations under their exchange agreements.

**Worked reconciliation.** Start the year with 80 members. Five admissions and two transfers in add seven. Three transfers out and one death remove four. Expected closing roll is 83. If the extracted roll is 82, inspect effective dates, duplicate merges and unclassified movements. Show the variance of one until resolved or explicitly explained. A child in a member household is counted in a household measure and enters the membership roll only if they independently qualify. Attendance across twelve services is not a substitute for the closing roll.

**Roll-ups.** Two campuses with 30 and 22 unique participants and five shared people yield 47 distinct people across those campuses when the same authorized organization can deduplicate. Two independent churches exchanging totals can report 52 campus participations; if identity linkage is unauthorized they must mark the unique-person total unavailable. Treat a missing return differently from a submitted zero. Report response coverage with the total.

**Working sequence.** Freeze the definition, perimeter and cutoff; preview; investigate unusual variations; record explanations; submit for authorized review; publish the certified audience-specific report. Permit supplementary financial statements, ministry narratives and accessibility-friendly print versions. A late correction reopens a copy, recalculates affected lines and notifies previous recipients that an amended return is available.

**Acceptance.** At month close the secretary can reproduce the original roll and the corrected roll and explain the difference. A receiving district cannot drill into personal gifts from an aggregate report. A congregation that closed mid-year can still certify its final partial period. A zero denominator renders the rate unavailable instead of implying a zero rate.
