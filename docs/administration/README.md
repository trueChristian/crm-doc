The employing organization owns personnel decisions. The church registrar owns membership and rites, the minutes clerk owns approved decisions and the archivist owns custody of originals and issued copies. A single secretary may perform all three jobs in a small church. Keep the authority under which each action was taken and retain independent approval where needed.
