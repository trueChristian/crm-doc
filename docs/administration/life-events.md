# Weddings, funerals, memorials and cemetery administration

Enable the applicable services and the church's own terminology. A service enquiry can come from a nonmember, a family representative, another congregation or an officiant. Relationship assertions, a scheduled service, a completed religious rite and a civil registration are separate facts. Only authorized staff may record evidence of an external registration. Local policy determines eligibility, required preparation and the form of certificates.

**Clerk journey.** A relative requests a funeral and supplies a safe callback number. Staff reserve a provisional slot, confirm authority, schedule the officiant and hall, and agree which service details may be published. After the service the clerk records what occurred and issues any permitted extract. Cancellation releases room and staffing reservations, applies the agreed fee terms and withdraws scheduled public announcements.

**Acceptance.** Two operators attempting to grant the last available niche cannot both succeed. A family can book a memorial without a listed plot. Removing a tribute hides its public rendition while retaining the privately authorized historical record under policy. A member death can close an active membership through its own dated workflow, and another person who shares the deceased's name remains unaffected. A family can decline public announcements while still receiving private coordination updates.
