# Partners, grants delivered to the community and relief distribution

A food pantry, scholarship, mission support or disaster appeal may serve people who never join the church. Permit an applicant to use a confidential service identifier and a safe contact method. Registration for assistance must not subscribe someone to church publicity. Ministry owns the project; care owns confidential casework; finance owns the authorized expense or grant; operations records the actual goods or services delivered.

**Example.** A pantry authorizes two parcels for a guest household. One is collected on Tuesday, another by a verified delegate on Friday. Store two delivery records and two stock movements. Repeating Friday's offline upload changes neither quantity nor stock. The public dashboard may count one household, two parcels and two service visits. A sponsor receives aggregate results and the expenses finance approved. A person may later join the church through the ordinary membership process.

**Acceptance and exceptions.** A site that runs out after one parcel leaves the award partially fulfilled and offers an agreed appointment or approved substitute. A lost voucher can be cancelled and reissued without doubling the award. A rejected applicant can appeal using the safe response route. A beneficiary's change of congregation has no effect on an already approved grant. Counts and stock balance reconcile after returns or spoiled goods; an oversubscribed round visibly distinguishes waiting, approved and fulfilled requests.
