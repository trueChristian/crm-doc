# Staff, clergy and employment administration

One person can serve as a paid employee, a minister, a governing officer and an unpaid volunteer in different capacities. Employment controls access to personnel records. A ministerial appointment or volunteer assignment belongs to the authority that made it. A church can share approved time, dated appointment terms, absence and departure notices with its payroll office. Personnel evidence remains with its custodian.

## Roster, appointment and exchange

A church may appoint a member to a six-month, half-time role while retaining their separate trustee or volunteer duties. Record the role, legal employer, supervisory relationship, service dates, capacity, signed terms reference and authority. Limit access to the agreement and amendments. A change in the term requires a new dated edition and renewed authorization. A scheduling officer sees availability and cover. The payroll office controls rates, wage calculations, leave balances, payslips and remittances.

A worker submits hours against the engagement and service period. Record the local time zone for interpreting overnight or clock-change shifts, actual start and end instants, and excluded breaks. A supervisor resolves overlaps, assigns cover and approves a revision. Share only approved dates, units, work category, permitted cost-centre reference, source identity and revision with the payroll service. A previous acceptance makes a retry harmless. Where there is no connected service, a restricted signed extract or human acknowledgement can close the handoff. Correcting approved time creates a linked revision and leaves the original recoverable.

An absence request records the affected interval, private reason under separate permissions, available replacement and approval. Select cover for each affected duty. Changing a three-day absence to two restores availability on day three. A roster coordinator sees appointment conflicts and availability but cannot see a diagnosis, salary or sensitive personnel case. A payroll rejection leaves an exception with its owner while the approved cover remains effective.

On departure, reassign open cases, recover keys and equipment, end staff grants, notify payroll and check outstanding handoffs. A former treasurer may still receive ordinary member communications or volunteer for choir. Access to protected payroll and board material is evaluated against each independent authority.
