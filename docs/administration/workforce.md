# Staff, clergy and employment administration

One person can serve as a paid employee, a minister, a governing officer and an unpaid volunteer in different capacities. Employment controls access to personnel records. A ministerial appointment or volunteer assignment belongs to the authority that made it. Payroll receives approved time, compensation changes and leave adjustments; private personnel files stay with their custodian.

**From hire to final pay.** HR records the agreed engagement and approved rate. A supervisor accepts the time entries; payroll imports only rows bearing a unique approved source key. An unpaid volunteer covering a shift receives a serving assignment unless a new paid engagement was authorized. A corrected timesheet already paid generates a delta in the next eligible pay run. When staff leave, the responsible officers confirm the actual return of equipment and removal of role-specific permissions.

**Outputs and access.** Authorized HR staff can see an establishment list with vacancies, expiring contracts, credential renewal dates and leave liability inputs. A team lead sees availability and cover. The treasurer sees pay values appropriate to their function. Staff can inspect their own time, balances and approved payslips. Board summaries aggregate salary costs in groups large enough to preserve confidentiality where required.

**Acceptance.** A 40-hour schedule at half time has a 20-hour reference week before local leave rules. A term change on the 16th splits approved earnings at the configured boundary. Exporting twice preserves one earning input. A departing treasurer retains ordinary member access while losing payment approval at the effective instant. A payroll rejection leaves its hours visible as an unposted exception.
