# Advanced capabilities that a church can choose

Enable each capability independently. Every one must have an owner, a reversible trial, an ordinary manual route and a measurable benefit. Run sample cases without sending messages, changing assignments or posting entries. An uncertain suggestion appears as a suggestion to the person responsible.

| Capability | Inputs and user experience | Controls, measure and acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain a historical result | A clerk asks “why did this roll change by three?” Compare two dated roll revisions and group the authorized differences by admission, departure, correction and duplicate resolution. Let the clerk open permitted source records. | Never reveal an excluded person's name through a difference or a count. A test roll with two admissions, two transfers out and three reviewed corrections explains a net change of three in the specified direction. |
| Substitute and capacity rehearsal | A worship leader previews two alternative schedules using availability, eligible skills, room limits, travel time, promised breaks and recent assignments. Show unresolved duties and why a proposed assignment is eligible. | Do not publish automatically. The volunteer confirms the proposed change. A simulated third session must reveal double-booked people without changing the live roster. Show whether confirmed last-minute gaps actually decreased after adoption. |
| Care handover with safe contact | A worker going on leave chooses open follow-ups, appoints a permitted cover person, previews which instructions can travel and sets a return date. A cover person sees next action and safe contact window. | Case narrative remains under the original access rules. Show outstanding and acknowledged handovers, without ranking a person's faith, vulnerability or likelihood of giving. An unaccepted handover returns to the coordinator. |
| Service-day continuity pack | An authorized duty leader prepares a short-lived pack for one occurrence with an attendance sheet, room assignments and the current minimum instructions. The interface displays age, exit reconciliation and safe escalation options. | No stale pickup authorization can release a child. A drill with a revoked delegate and a disconnected station must end in supervised verification and an auditable result. Destroy expired copies according to custody procedure. |
| Multilingual bulletin from approved facts | Staff compose a dated bulletin from released announcements, current times and authorized public media. Provide reviewed translations, large-print, accessible text and an audio-friendly edition from the same revision. | Carry expiry, audience and reviewer into every rendition. Withdraw an event and verify that unpublished future bulletins and scheduled deliveries stop using it. Measure time to approved publication and correction frequency per language. |
| Explain a stewardship total | The church can trace a published appeal total to accepted gifts, adjustments, the designation and the report cutoff. An approved accounting summary supplies a separately dated fund balance where available. | A returned gift appears under its adjustment date and attribution. Pending transfers, volunteers’ recognition credit and promises are distinguishable from received gifts; a small aggregate must not identify a donor. |
| Accessible media discovery | List approved chapters, reviewed transcripts, languages, speaker and accessibility features. A member searches their permitted library, resumes a sermon privately and downloads an allowed edition for a defined interval. | Honour audience, rights territory if configured, entitlement expiry, withdrawal and age policies on every rendition. Personal listening history is opt-in and is excluded from pastoral scoring. |
| Member-controlled participation record | A person chooses which verified training or service milestones to show to a coordinator and can issue a time-limited, revocable extract. Display provenance, expiry and correction route. | Never expose care notes, gifts or safeguarding evidence. A withdrawn credential prevents new use even if an old extract still exists on paper; a verifier can see current status without browsing the register. |
| Practical question answering | A user asks when the next open-room meeting is or which approved minutes authorize repairs. Answers identify the permitted internal record, version and date, or say the record is unavailable. | Recheck permissions when retrieving and displaying. If no approved decision is found, offer an authorized search route. An assistant cannot decide membership, release a child, post money or publish minutes. |

## Recording a reversible trial

| Table | Fields | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CapabilityTrial | id; organization_id; feature_key; accountable_owner; participating_units; objective; baseline_measure_ref; success_measure_ref; start_at, review_at, stop_at: instants; rollback_plan_ref; approval_ref; state: proposed, active, suspended, adopted or concluded | Limit trial scope and permissions. Include affected users in accessibility and privacy evaluation. |
| TrialObservation | id; trial_id; observed_at; population_description; metric_key; numerator, denominator; quality; adverse_outcome_ref: optional restricted case; observer | Include skipped and failed outcomes. Give the church a way to capture a harmful suggestion without storing unnecessary personal data. |
| TrialDecision | id; trial_id; decided_at; reviewer; result: expand, amend, disable or continue; justification; configuration_revision; follow_up_due_on | Disabling removes new suggestions or optional capture immediately. The church preserves approved schedules, gift evidence and previously issued reports. |

Acceptance includes comparing a trial with its manual alternative for accuracy, privacy, accessibility, staff time and recovery after failure. A church can stop a trial, see the setting change take effect and complete ordinary work using the standard process.
