# Cross-department acceptance journeys

Use fictional records in an isolated organization. Each journey checks the user-visible result, the owner of each fact, who can see it, and what survives a failure. Repeat the action from a second session, deliver status updates twice and inspect the historical view after a correction. A green result for one department does not establish the cross-department outcome.

## 1. A mixed-membership family visits

Create two adults sharing a mailbox, a teenager who spends time in two households, and a visiting grandparent. Only one adult is a formal member. Register the others as guests or individuals with a household connection. Link one verified sign-in. Have each adult choose their own directory audience and the child's verified representative choose the permitted profile fields. A Sunday attendance and a household address change must not affect the formal roll. On the test date, the clerk can enumerate four distinct persons and one formal member. A grandparent listed as an emergency contact cannot collect the child without active pickup authority. See [people](../people/README.md) and [child supervision](../ministry/child-check-in.md).

## 2. A transfer, an election and a corrected roll

Take a certified roll on 31 March. A transfer out becomes effective on 1 April. The previous roll still contains the person. A board meeting using an eligibility cutoff on 2 April uses its charter's voting rule, not the current household listing. Record a vote, approve the minutes, then correct a misspelt name. Reproduce the originally confirmed minutes, the amended edition and the current roll; none of those actions reopens the ballot. A district receiving a permitted aggregate cannot retrieve the individual voting record. See [membership and rolls](../people/membership-and-rolls.md), [governance](../governance/README.md) and [annual returns](../administration/annual-returns.md).

## 3. Two seats, one donation and a partial refund

A family registers for an adult seat at 30 and a child seat at 10, and designates a separate voluntary gift of 5 in the same currency. A provider confirms one payment of 45 with those three components. Verify two confirmed places and one gift of 5. Repeat the callback twice. Cancel the child’s place and confirm the provider’s refund of 10. The adult place stays valid, the child seat is released once, giving remains 5 and the net provider amount is 35. A pending refund displays separately. A late payment after hold expiry cannot exceed room capacity. See [registration](../ministry/registration.md), [church desk handoffs](../commerce/README.md) and [accounting exchange](../finance/accounting-exchange.md).

## 4. Move a service across campuses

An organizer moves a future occurrence from a 60-seat hall to a 35-seat room after 42 attendees have registered. Preserve the original issued notice. Show the seven-place shortfall to the organizer before confirming or publishing arrangements; never quietly displace guests. Resolve capacity, equipment and accessible travel needs. Re-evaluate upcoming reminders. Invited people receive the approved correction on their selected routes, and the public Telegram channel receives only the approved public text. A withdrawn recipient or cancelled occurrence is excluded at send time. Readers can distinguish a delivery acknowledgement from proof the person read the notice. See [calendar](../ministry/calendar.md) and [communications](../communications/README.md).

## 5. From a board resolution to a repaired roof

A board approves roof repairs up to 8,000. Issue an authorized extract and open a facilities work order. The steward records the affected room, closure dates, temporary location and accepted repair. The treasurer sends the external purchasing contact the resolution, approved estimate of 7,500, currency and expiry. A proposed change to 8,200 needs fresh authority. The receiving system confirms the agreed amount and returns its own reference. The clerk attaches an approved summary to the board pack. Booking availability follows the site inspection; neither approval of the minutes nor reopening confirms payment. See [minutes](../governance/minutes-and-resolutions.md) and [facilities desk exercise](../handbook/administration/site-disruption.md).

## 6. A church withdraws a sermon recording

Publish a sermon under the speaker’s approved release, with a transcript and a defined audience. Revoke the rights for the recording while retaining an independently approved bulletin summary. The church removes affected links from search, the app and future bulletins; the host is asked to withdraw the identified edition. A host acknowledgement covers only that destination. The editor tracks outstanding copies and can show when each was recalled or fell outside recall. Publication history remains auditable. See [media](../commerce/media.md) and [access](../commerce/access.md).

## 7. Pastoral support leading to aid

A visitor requests a private callback and supplies a safe time. A pastor accepts the referral and asks permission to share the minimum information needed for aid. A coordinator authorizes two parcels or a vendor payment. Finance receives the amount, fund and approved payee reference; it does not receive the narrative. The public impact report receives approved aggregates. Reversing a duplicate stock movement corrects distribution totals while preserving the confidential case. A person who withdraws permission stops future optional sharing and is told which issued records the church must still handle under its configured policy. See [pastoral care](../people/pastoral-care.md), [benevolence](../people/benevolence-and-community-aid.md) and [community distribution](../administration/community-programs.md).

## 8. Staff leave with ordinary member access

A treasurer also sings in the choir. End their staff and treasurer assignments on Friday, transfer open work, revoke derived sessions and reassess ordinary access. On Saturday the former treasurer can read their own rota and permitted personal gift acknowledgement. An old bookmarked donor export and a previously queued MCP request for donor details are denied. Approved staff time remains available to the external payroll service through the authorized route. A repeated time export produces the same handoff. See [workforce](../administration/workforce.md) and [access](../platform/identity-access.md).

## 9. A restricted appeal closes with a balance

Record a gift of 100 for an appeal and its donor restriction. At cutoff the accountant supplies an approved report showing eligible expenditure of 70 and 30 still restricted. Link that release to the project without deriving spendable cash from the gift register. With no report the church can publish the authorized giving total and mark expenditure unavailable. A fundraiser cannot reassign the 30 or issue a second acknowledgement for recognition credit. See [fund stewardship](../finance/stewardship.md) and [development](../development/README.md).

## 10. Restore after an interruption

A giving handoff succeeds at the accounting service but connectivity fails before its acknowledgement arrives. After a restore, query the existing transfer or reconcile the export to a dated accountant confirmation, then record the result against the original key. Verify one accepted handoff and one gift. Recheck later consent withdrawals, expired pickup authority and staff grants before reopening queued actions. Record outstanding cases, recovery point, recovery time and who authorized restart. See [recovery](../platform/operations-recovery.md) and [accounting exchange](../finance/accounting-exchange.md).

## 11. A class runs while its learning provider is unavailable

Register a young attendee for a Saturday group, assign the room and two approved leaders, and record attendance at reception. With the learning connection offline, the group still meets. A new helper whose required evidence cannot be verified stays pending. Later the verification officer accepts a scoped attestation; the coordinator separately approves the helper for a future date. Neither paper check-in nor the incoming evidence alters membership, academic progress or the earlier rota. See [classes and learning](../ministry/learning.md).

## 12. A member app submits a change and an assistant follows it up

From a church app, a verified adult requests a correction for their own record. A retry after a lost response returns the same request. The office reviewer can see and resolve that request through an authorized MCP client. A volunteer using the same client cannot enumerate the household or retrieve the protected request. Revoke the reviewer's role while a second request is awaiting action; the next command is denied and another authorized reviewer can take over. See [app and MCP acceptance](../acceptance/platform/app-and-mcp.md).
