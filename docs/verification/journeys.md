# Cross-department acceptance journeys

Use fictional records in an isolated organization. Each journey checks the user-visible result, the owner of each fact, who can see it, and what survives a failure. Repeat the action from a second session, deliver status updates twice and inspect the historical view after a correction. A green result for one department does not establish the cross-department outcome.

## 1. A mixed-membership family visits

Create two adults sharing a mailbox, a teenager who spends time in two households, and a visiting grandparent. Only one adult is a formal member. Register the others as guests or individuals with a household connection. Link one verified sign-in. Have each adult choose their own directory audience and the child's verified representative choose the permitted profile fields. A Sunday attendance and a household address change must not affect the formal roll. On the test date, the clerk can enumerate four distinct persons and one formal member. A grandparent listed as an emergency contact cannot collect the child without active pickup authority. See [people](../people/README.md) and [child supervision](../ministry/child-check-in.md).

## 2. A transfer, an election and a corrected roll

Take a certified roll on 31 March. A transfer out becomes effective on 1 April. The previous roll still contains the person. A board meeting using an eligibility cutoff on 2 April uses its charter's voting rule, not the current household listing. Record a vote, approve the minutes, then correct a misspelt name. Reproduce the originally confirmed minutes, the amended edition and the current roll; none of those actions reopens the ballot. A district receiving a permitted aggregate cannot retrieve the individual voting record. See [membership and rolls](../people/membership-and-rolls.md), [governance](../governance/README.md) and [annual returns](../administration/annual-returns.md).

## 3. Two seats, one donation and a partial refund

A family orders an adult seat for 30, a child seat for 10 and a voluntary gift of 5 in one currency, with no tax or fees for this fixture. Allocate the 45 capture to three components. After one confirmed capture, reserve exactly two places and record one gift of 5. Repeat the callback twice. The order, gift, admission and journal counts do not change. Cancel the child's place and confirm a refund of 10. The adult place remains valid, the child seat is released exactly once, giving remains 5 and net cash is 35. A pending refund displays separately from a confirmed money movement. A late capture after a hold expires must not exceed room capacity. See [registration](../ministry/registration.md), [checkout](../commerce/orders.md) and [receivables](../finance/receivables.md).

## 4. Move a service across campuses

An organizer moves a future occurrence from a 60-seat hall to a 35-seat room after 42 attendees have registered. Preserve the original issued notice. Show the seven-place shortfall to the organizer before confirming or publishing arrangements; never quietly displace guests. Resolve capacity, equipment and accessible travel needs. Re-evaluate upcoming reminders. Invited people receive the approved correction on their selected routes, and the public Telegram channel receives only the approved public text. A withdrawn recipient or cancelled occurrence is excluded at send time. Readers can distinguish a delivery acknowledgement from proof the person read the notice. See [calendar](../ministry/calendar.md) and [communications](../communications/README.md).

## 5. From a board resolution to a repaired roof

A board approves repairs up to 8,000. Issue the approved extract and assign the action. A requisition estimates 7,500; the purchasing officer raises a purchase order within authority. Record completed work, a matching invoice, independent payment approval, settlement and bank reconciliation. Cancellation before supply releases an encumbrance under the selected policy. An 8,200 change order must wait for a revised authority or a documented exception. Link the final financial report to the board pack while contracts, bank details and signatory evidence retain their access controls. See [minutes](../governance/minutes-and-resolutions.md), [payables](../finance/payables.md) and [budgets](../finance/planning.md).

## 6. A paid sermon is withdrawn

Publish a sermon with approved speaker permission, recording rights, accessible transcript and a paid offer. A successful payment entitles one intended account. Another household member cannot play it solely because they share a surname. A rights withdrawal stops playback for new requests and expires public previews and search results according to the approved policy. Notify affected purchasers through an appropriate service channel. A refund or replacement is a separate tracked commercial decision; withdrawal never erases the historical sale. See [media](../commerce/media.md), [access](../commerce/access.md) and [settlement](../commerce/settlement.md).

## 7. Pastoral support leading to aid

A visitor requests a private callback and supplies a safe time. A pastor accepts the referral and asks permission to share the minimum information needed for aid. A coordinator authorizes two parcels or a vendor payment. Finance receives the amount, fund and approved payee reference; it does not receive the narrative. The public impact report receives approved aggregates. Reversing a duplicate stock movement corrects distribution totals while preserving the confidential case. A person who withdraws permission stops future optional sharing and is told which issued records the church must still handle under its configured policy. See [pastoral care](../people/pastoral-care.md), [benevolence](../people/benevolence-and-community-aid.md) and [community distribution](../administration/community-programs.md).

## 8. Staff leave with ordinary member access

A treasurer also belongs to the choir. End the employment and treasurer assignments on Friday, transfer open approvals, revoke privileged sessions and reassess ordinary access. On Saturday the person can still see their own choir rota and permitted personal statement; they cannot export donations, approve payroll, retrieve a cached finance report or use a previously issued staff-only link. Approved time remains payable once. Evidence for the prior period remains available to an authorized auditor. See [workforce](../administration/workforce.md), [payroll](../finance/payroll.md) and [access](../platform/identity-access.md).

## 9. A restricted appeal closes with a balance

Record a donor restriction, an accepted gift for 100, eligible expenditure of 70, and a funding report. The remaining 30 stays under the applicable restriction until valid written authority permits a different use, return or continued holding. A volunteer writing the appeal cannot redirect the balance or issue a second receipt for recognition credit. Printed donor recognition, a family statement and public campaign totals are three separately authorized outputs. See [fund administration](../finance/stewardship.md) and [development](../development/README.md).

## 10. Restore after an interruption

A transfer succeeds at the provider and the church loses connectivity before receiving its acknowledgement. Recover from a tested backup, replay the external movement by its stable identifier and reconcile the journal, order and bank report. Verify that queued reminders obey any later withdrawal, that expired child pickup authority remains expired and that staff-only search and exported files recheck the current grant. Record unresolved cases, recovery point, recovery time and the person who accepted restoration. See [operational recovery](../platform/operations-recovery.md) and the [worked financial month](../finance/worked-month.md).
