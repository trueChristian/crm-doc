# Release the first bulletin

Use the six North identities from the [register workpaper](register.md). Church A has approved edition N1 of a public service bulletin. The text contains only the meeting time and venue. It contains no names, personalized links, roll classifications or giving amounts. All channels in this rehearsal terminate in a test receiver with a retrievable delivery history.

The church's fictional rule admits people on its current June roll and people separately enrolled in the public service bulletin. Everyone also needs current affirmative permission for the bulletin purpose and a verified deliverable endpoint. An address shared by several people can carry one copy of this public edition for an eligible recipient. This permission does not extend to a personalized notice, a private enclosure or another message purpose.

| Person | Basis at audience preview on 2 June | Purpose permission | Endpoint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Original June roll | Granted | E1, shared |
| P2 | Original June roll only | Granted | E1, shared |
| P3 | No selection basis | Unknown | None |
| P4 | Original June roll | Granted | E2 |
| P5 | Enrolled separately in the public bulletin | Granted, withdrawn at 09:30 on 3 June | E3 |
| P6 | Enrolled separately in the public bulletin | Never granted | E4 |

The preview shows five selected people, four people with permission and three distinct deliverable destinations. Identify P2 as a co-recipient on a shared endpoint only in the appropriately restricted preview. P6 is suppressed despite a usable address. The household can receive a public copy through a consenting member without establishing permission for anyone else.

At 09:00 on 3 June the clerk certifies the June amendment removing P2. At 09:30 the church records P5's withdrawal. Re-evaluate when dispatch starts at 10:00. Three previously selected people have no dispatch: P2 is outside the current audience, P5 has withdrawn, and P6 lacks permission. P3 was never selected. Two business deliveries remain, P1 through E1 and P4 through E2. If the design retains excluded preview candidates, distinguish those retained explanations from outbound jobs.

| Receiver observation | Result in delivery history | External effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 accepts D1 immediately | Store acceptance once for N1 and E1 | One public edition in the E1 test mailbox |
| E2 accepts D2 but its response is lost | Keep D2 unresolved and query by its stable dispatch reference | One edition in E2; the sender cannot yet assert acceptance |
| The receiver later returns its D2 acceptance twice | Match both observations to the original dispatch and reconcile one acceptance | E2 still contains one edition |
| An operator reruns the same edition and audience batch | Return the existing dispatch history and exclusions | No additional messages |

For this receiver and profile, the reconciled figures are two accepted destinations, two people associated with those deliveries, zero unresolved effects and no evidence of a read. Support may see the edition, delivery reference, time and permitted outcome. The public page and recipient-visible bulletin have no access to the suppressed names or reasons. If an edition includes a private statement for P1, the public shared-address rule above is inapplicable. Route it under the statement's purpose, verified recipient authority and permitted destination.
