# Recover and sign off the opening weekend

Obtain four independent pieces of evidence before creating the interruption. The registrar supplies the original roll and the dated correction evidence. The communication receiver supplies its delivery register. The payment service and bank supply settlement and refund observations. The storekeeper supplies the issue and inspected physical return. Each source is independently readable by its authorized reviewer in this rehearsal.

Take a recovery point after the original June roll, the bulletin preview and the three captures, before the June amendment, actual dispatch, settlement, return or refund. Keep test receivers active while the original application completes those later events. Record its result. Restore the earlier copy in isolation and with its outbound writers disabled. The objective is to learn whether already-issued mail, money movements and physical custody can be reconstructed from evidence. Restoring a database cannot reverse any of those external events.

## Deliberate discrepancy

Run a second recovery with the return receipt missing while the refund and debit evidence remain. The bank still increased by 87. The finance evidence establishes the 10 refund. Until the storekeeper confirms the physical return, the stock evidence supports a fall of two copies and 12 in value; create a discrepancy for one copy at 6. Block acceptance of the inventory reconciliation. Keep the refund intact and leave the physical movement pending. When the storekeeper supplies and verifies the original return receipt, apply it once and close the discrepancy. Re-run with the same receipt to show that it cannot add a second copy.

## Acceptance and custody

The clerk signs the as-of date, knowledge cutoff and identity sets. The communications reviewer signs the purpose edition, suppressions and confirmed receiver counts. The treasurer signs the bank statement movement, clearing and both fund balances. The storekeeper signs the physical evidence and units. The recovery lead records the recovery point, time to usable service, unresolved effects and who released each writer. In the clean rehearsal the variance for every stated control is zero. In the deliberate discrepancy exercise the inventory workpaper stays open until receipt evidence resolves it.
