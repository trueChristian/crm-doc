# Privacy, consent, disclosure and records custody

The organization defines which data it collects, why, who may use it and the local rules it has approved. Administrators record those decisions with an owner and effective date. A directory listing, an email newsletter, an emergency contact, a photograph release and use of a recording are separate decisions. Attendance, health needs, safeguarding, pastoral notes, donor identity and addresses with disclosure restrictions require individually scoped access.

## Decision and retention workflows

The current consent decision is resolved for the subject, purpose, option and scope at execution time. Distinguish unknown, granted, refused, withdrawn and expired. Refusal and withdrawal take effect throughout audience calculation and output generation; explicit refusal cannot be overridden by a group subscription. Resolve concurrent or contradictory equal-priority choices conservatively and put them in review. Cap the lifetime of any issued file or link. A viewer printing a directory can be told its issue date and confidentiality instructions. Record distribution and request return or destruction after a withdrawal when appropriate.

An officer receiving a request verifies the claimant and any valid representation, identifies records across departments and reviews exceptions with the designated custodian. Separate another person's identifying details from shared family and meeting records. Approve an export or reply with its scope, corrections and retained classes with reasons. Deliver through a verified channel and expire access. A correction appends to an approved minute or financial entry according to its domain rules. If a record must be retained, restrict subsequent processing and document the decision for review. Recovering from a backup must replay withdrawals and disposals before restored data becomes visible.

For disposal, dry-run candidates grouped by rule and owner; sample and review potentially conflicting cases. Live holds block disposal even after retention expiry. Release one hold only under its own authority. Select deletion, irreversible aggregation, de-identification subject to reidentification review, or archival transfer as configured. Unlink derived previews, search indexes, cached exports and offline replicas. Track physical copies separately. Audit disposal without storing the erased content as evidence.

Audit the creation of invitations, access to protected records, failed protected actions, permission changes, searches of restricted data, imports, exports, disclosures, impersonation, approvals and reversals. Readers cannot edit events about their own activity. Corrections add linked events. Monitor missing audit sequences and restricted access spikes. Separate diagnostic sampling and permitted audit retention. Access to audit detail is itself auditable.

## Example and acceptance

A parent allows a child to appear in a printed class register and refuses public photographs. A secretary can prepare the class list for its authorized audience. Media selection excludes the child from a public gallery. When the parent withdraws directory sharing after the newsletter has been queued, the send job recalculates eligible recipients and removes protected content before handoff. If disclosure has already occurred, preserve the original fact and start the correction or recall process.

- A person requests their data at a shared household address. Only the verified claimant's approved redacted package is downloadable. Third-party emails and private pastoral records receive an explicit reviewer decision.
- A staff member searches a protected address. Ordinary search, suggestions, recent-item lists and counts omit it. A permitted safeguarding reader sees it only for an allowed purpose, with an audit record.
- Two holds protect the same case. The first release leaves all evidence protected; the second triggers review of the still-current retention rule.
- An expired export and a delivery link whose permission was revoked both deny retrieval. Secure disposal of the package preserves its delivery ledger and reason within its approved retention.
