# Pastoral visiting districts and home contact

Some congregations ask elders, deacons or visiting teams to keep in touch with a particular set of people. An elder needs to know whom they have agreed to contact, when, and who will take over when their term ends. The office needs to see people who have asked for visits and still have no available visitor. A person may prefer a call, a visit at a neutral venue, a particular language, another visitor, or no visits at all.

## Establish the district and contact arrangements

| Decision | Church record and owner | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Establish or retire a visiting district | Ministry coordinator opens a restricted MinistryGroup and GroupTerm with defined congregation, purpose, review date and eligible leaders. A church can use geographic or language districts, or one visiting team. | The office can roster workers and plan coverage. Group membership and group leadership each have their own dated record. |
| Allocate pastoral responsibility | A coordinator records the person’s agreed participation in the group, dates and entry route. Use an authorized person link for each participant, including people who usually receive a joint visit. | A district list can include a nonmember who requested contact. Joining creates no formal church membership, directory listing or permission to contact another resident. |
| Agree contact | Welcome staff record the specific requested purpose, route and safe hours. A recurring personal visit requiring care coordination uses CareRequest, CareCase, dated CareAssignment and CarePlanAction. Decide with the person on interval, next review, scope and safe venue. | A private arrangement can be operated by designated staff even when including the person on an ordinary district roster would disclose their location or circumstances. |
| Arrange the next visit | The responsible worker schedules a CareContact under the agreement. A general calendar shows availability. Secure instructions are available only to the person assigned to that visit. | Two workers accompanying one another record one contact for the agreed visit. People actually visited are confirmed independently where person-level results are needed. |
| Delegate or hand back | Limit cover to the dates, subjects and actions needed. Review open and overdue commitments when a leader changes term. Record a successor’s acknowledgment and revoke former access at the effective time. | Unassigned work returns to a named duty queue. Temporary cover never inherits every case of an elder by joining the district. |
| End the arrangement | Record the person’s instruction, effective time, outstanding obligations and whether a single currently agreed task continues. Stop future reminders and restrict cached contact lists. | A resident may leave the visiting list while continuing attendance, church membership or an independently requested service. |

The district steward may use an address or language as a suggestion only where that information is available for this purpose. A protected household must have a confidential assignment route. A worker searching an ordinary district list cannot infer a hidden entry from a map pin, row count, downloaded route or list of exceptions. Moving campuses or sharing a household does not move anyone else's visiting arrangement. If two district leaders request the same visit, the coordinator identifies the accountable owner, any accompanying worker and whether there are genuinely different purposes. Reassignment preserves earlier contact history and authority.

## Requests triggered by absence

For a named service with expected participants, a coordinator may review an explicitly recorded absence under the church’s approved purpose. A cancelled service, headcount-only gathering, partial attendance sheet, unreturned paper roster or missing sign-in supplies no evidence of an individual's absence. Participation in a different gathering may be private. A clerk must be able to resolve an uncertain record without seeing where else the person worshipped. Repeated absences can place one item in a restricted human review queue when policy allows. They cannot change formal standing, mark someone homebound, notify relatives, or open a pastoral case automatically.

At review, consider whether there is already an active contact arrangement, an explicit request to pause, an unsafe route or a worker temporarily covering the next contact. Before calling or visiting, verify current permission, preferred timing and route. A shared telephone answered by another person can be recorded as an attempted contact without naming the individual or the reason for the call. When contact is declined, close the relevant action and prevent recurring reminders from repeatedly reopening it.

## Visit outcomes and district coverage

Use a review date and a cutoff instant when showing coverage. Report the number of people with a contact due in scope, those with a completed qualifying contact during their agreed window, people with attempts only, and people with no recorded attempt. Count distinct people, report visits separately, and name the person responsible for outstanding commitments. A superseded agreement is assessed under the edition in force during the window. Paused, withdrawn and out-of-scope arrangements have their own exclusion counts for authorized coordinators. A board-level summary withholds small or identifying groups according to the church’s reporting policy.

For example, five people independently agreed to a visit this month. A visiting team sees two people together and a third person separately. A call to a fourth person is unanswered and the fifth has no allocation. At cutoff, two completed visits reached three of five people, one had an attempt only and one needs assignment. An incoming worker claiming the fifth task changes assignment coverage immediately and completed-contact coverage only after the visit actually occurs. Correcting an earlier outcome creates dated evidence and a restated report.
