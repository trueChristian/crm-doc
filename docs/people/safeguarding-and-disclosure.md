# Safeguarding concerns and protected disclosure

A designated safeguarding lead needs a reliable way to receive a concern, acknowledge responsibility, record a factual chronology and initiate the organization's approved response. Store these records in a restricted case area. Configure local decision makers, escalation routes, alternate independent recipients, reporting windows and retention rules with competent local owners before the feature is activated.

## Intake, action and oversight

A confidential intake route accepts a minimal description, safe contact options and optional identity. A receipt gives locally approved instructions for urgent help and a safe alternative route; it must not disclose a reporter to a person implicated in the report. A human lead or alternate verifies acknowledgment. A dispatch to an unattended mailbox is not an acknowledgment. Escalate missed acknowledgments to a configured independent role and record the attempts.

Assign only authorized personnel, including an alternate if the usual approver, pastor, account owner or a relative is involved. A lead may introduce narrowly scoped protective measures, record external contact and schedule the next review. The event coordinator receives “ask the designated supervisor” or a release decision; they receive no allegation, diagnosis or reporter identity. Only designated safeguarding staff can associate a protective decision with the case narrative. Distinct purposes govern staff screening, pastoral care, police or agency referrals and membership discipline.

Record each read, search, print, download, disclosure, grant, amendment and destruction in a protected audit path. Keep identifying case text out of general search, event notifications, staff dashboards, calendars, telemetry, bulk export, public AI indexing and normal people timelines. Requests for access/correction go to an authorized reviewer who considers third-party protection, statutory duties, retention holds and permitted redaction under the applicable policy. Default automated summaries and cross-case suggestions off. A tool must never decide credibility, guilt, reportability or risk classification on its own.

## Verification

| Case | Expected result |
|---|---|
| A first-time guest reports anonymously and the ordinary recipient is implicated | Independent intake can accept and route the report, leave identity unknown, and prevent ordinary recipient access or notices. |
| A restricted person is also a recognized guardian | Collection or activity operation returns the current neutral decision. Directory, queue search and exported rosters cannot reveal the case. |
| An allegation is later corrected or found unsubstantiated | Correct chronology and reviewed outcome are retained under policy. Authorized staff specifically review protective measures and remove obsolete ones. |
| An employee with general administrator access attempts a search by case number | General search discloses no case existence; any exceptional access uses the independent, auditable process. |
| A review is due during a staff absence and a delivery fails | Escalation reaches the configured alternate and remains open until a human acknowledges it. |
| A retention date arrives while a hold is active | The case stays protected. A named reviewer documents release of the hold and later disposition; aggregate statistics cannot re-identify it. |
