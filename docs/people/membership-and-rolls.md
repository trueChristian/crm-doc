# Membership, transfers and certified rolls

The membership clerk needs to explain who belonged to which congregation on a given date, why a change was made and which names were eligible under the rules in force for a meeting. Weekly attendance, church website accounts, volunteer service and donations describe different facts. A visitor may attend for years without seeking formal membership.

## Decisions and reconciliation

Receive application, invite any required follow-up, collect the evidence the church asks for, and route to its designated decision maker. Admission creates approved MembershipDecision, Membership and first MembershipPeriod as one business operation. A failed approval produces none of those effects. Repeated submission of the same approved decision returns the prior result. A decline or withdrawal ends the application and its reminders, without erasing unrelated participation. Reviewers may request additional evidence without marking someone a member.

When a class or standing changes, close the earlier period and open the new period on its effective date. A clerk can propose a backdated correction with reason. Show affected statistics, voting snapshots, letters and integrations before approval. A new certified edition may be issued, but the edition actually used at a past meeting remains identifiable. Keep sensitive disciplinary or restoration proceedings outside general search. Authorized pastoral care or safeguarding decisions follow their own controls.

For an internal transfer, record consent, responsible clerks, issue date, acknowledgment and effective dates. An incoming congregation sees a minimal transfer package, never the member's private case notes. An outgoing transfer awaiting acknowledgment appears on a work queue. Prevent a cancelled request from applying a later acknowledgment. Where the local policy permits dual affiliation, state which congregation reports primary membership and how denominational totals deduplicate.

## Reports, controls and examples

* Opening balance plus admissions, incoming transfers and restorations, less resignations, outgoing transfers and deaths, equals closing formal membership for unchanged boundaries and definitions. Show class changes moving into/out of counted classes and removals made by correction as explicit adjustments. Report transfers between two included congregations separately from external movements.
* Publish printable membership lists and the count for congregation meetings at a specified cutoff. A visitor directory, an attendance list and a roster of eligible voters may contain different names. Each report names its population, rules and as-of date.
* Example: on 1 June Mara applies, is admitted effective 15 June by a decision recorded 20 June, and moves to associate membership on 1 October. A report using today's knowledge counts her under the old class on 30 June. A certified 18 June roll prepared before entry remains unchanged. A revised roll records its own publication date.
* A transfer to an unknown external church leaves the destination internal key empty and includes the external name. Access to that letter requires the applicable authority and expires under local retention rules.
* Two clerks approving the same application concurrently obtain one membership and one admission. A former member returning after resignation gets a new dated episode with a traceable link. A child of two formal members still has no formal membership until their own admission or other expressly configured, approved admission process.

## Exchange a transfer with an independent church

The source clerk verifies the applicant, authority to disclose, intended church and an independently confirmed recipient or office route. Show exactly which facts and issue edition will leave the congregation. An extract typically contains the conveying church, approved member name, attested membership facts, issue date, document validity where applicable, transfer reference and route for the receiving clerk to verify it. The member can collect a sealed document when an approved electronic route is unavailable. Record what was issued, to whom, how and when. A bulk directory, shared household, private reasons for departure and other people's identities fall outside this package.

An external church may reply by signed acknowledgement, approved API or verified paper correspondence. Match the issuer, recipient organization, transfer reference, issued edition, response kind and responder authority. Preserve the destination's own decision date when supplied. Keep sent, transport-delivered, acknowledged by a receiving clerk, and locally completed as separate facts. If a reply concerns an earlier edition, a namesake, a changed destination, or a transfer subsequently cancelled, send it to the clerk's exception queue. A gap in the receiving system can leave a local transfer issued with an overdue follow-up. An agreed policy that permits a unilateral exit requires its own dated decision and reason.

When the sending member changes their destination, withdraw the old issue under its authority, keep its receipt history and obtain authorization for the new church. An already delivered copy can require a correction or withdrawal notice. The source church can report only the disposition it knows about at the destination. A subsequent source correction calls for a fresh acknowledgment from the receiving authority if material. Test both the [external transfer](../acceptance/people/welcome-through-transfer.md) and the [historical roll](../handbook/people/certifying-the-roll.md).

## Review standing, appeal a decision and restore membership

A church defines which body may change formal standing, what review or appeal it offers, whether a pending appeal stays a decision, and which rights depend on a given standing. Churches can omit this workflow. Where it is used, a report, private pastoral conversation or outstanding contribution cannot itself change the register. Receive a concern through its authorized channel. The membership clerk sees only a direction issued by an authorized body and the scope necessary to carry it out. Protected reports retain independent ownership and access controls.

| Stage | Required decision and evidence | Effect visible outside the review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open review | Assign an authorized, conflict-checked reviewer. Preserve the governing policy version, safe notice route, agreed opportunity to respond and decision deadline under the church’s procedure. Limit access to individuals assigned to the review. | A pending review alone changes no roll, voting entitlement or church assignment. An independently authorized interim measure carries its own scope and expiry. |
| Issue decision | Record the deciding body or delegated actor, decision reference, affected congregation, status, effective date, review date if prescribed and who may receive the decision. Separate the restricted account of proceedings from the minimal standing code in the register. | Apply a class, standing or closure only through the appropriate dated MembershipDecision. When allowed by policy, voting eligibility can change without removing the person from the formal membership count. |
| Receive appeal | Log a timely request or an authorized extension, assign an independent reviewer and record precisely which part is challenged. Record who decided whether the effective change is stayed and for how long. | A queue status of “appeal received” neither reinstates nor suppresses rights by itself. Authorized roll readers see only the eligibility effective at their cutoff. |
| Affirm, vary or remit | Record the appeal decision and, if it alters the register, create a correcting or prospective MembershipDecision linked to its predecessor. The member receives the approved outcome by their safe route. | Notify only the affected owners. Flag issued notices, ballots or reports requiring review under the applicable procedure. The certified edition previously used remains reproducible. |
| Restore | Check the local restoration route, required evidence, authority and effective date. A continuing membership changes its period. Following a completed departure, a new episode can refer to the earlier one. | A restoration can restore a membership right. Each service appointment, safeguarding measure, portal account or personal contact preference keeps its own decision and review owner. |

**Worked decision.** Under one congregation's policy an individual remains a formal member during a temporary suspension of voting. The decision was entered on 4 June, effective 10 June. A meeting certified its electorate as of 8 June before the change took effect. An appeal on 11 June carries no automatic stay under this sample policy. On 18 June the independent reviewer orders eligibility restored effective 20 June. The 8 June edition still includes the person, a new roll as of 15 June excludes them from voting and a new roll as of 20 June includes them. All three dates count one formal member. The welcome desk and ordinary member directory cannot infer the proceedings from category filters, search results, notification previews or a change in someone else's access.

See [independent review and remedy](../handbook/people/independent-review-and-remedy.md) and [certification and corrections](../handbook/people/certifying-the-roll.md).

## Renewable and permanent membership classes

Each congregation chooses whether any of its membership classes renew. For a permanent class, disable renewal, leave the interval and rule empty and create no renewal tasks. For a renewable class, publish an approved renewal rule with the effective policy edition. Specify the cycle anchor (individual anniversary or common church date), the positive interval in months, local civil date and month-end convention, when submissions open, the last timely day, any inclusive grace days, and the clock and evidence used to determine receipt. State the counted standing and voting entitlement during grace, the consequence allowed after grace, who may decide it and what late evidence or restoration route is available. Record the edition and cycle with each work item. A later policy change takes effect under its own transition decision.

A member can renew on paper, through an authorized clerk or through the member app. Preserve the dated receipt and source or intake key, effective membership/class, cycle and evidence of the member’s request in the protected workflow. Record when the clerk entered a paper renewal separately from when the office received it. Duplicates for the same member and cycle return the existing receipt or are linked for review. Changed evidence or disputed dates remain visible to the decision maker. Renewal by one household member cannot renew another adult. A payment, gift, registration fee or recurrent giving instruction supplies no renewal or admission authority.

A configurable reminder uses a permitted contact route. Returned post or a failed delivery creates an office follow-up where appropriate. It cannot backdate a lapse or count as notice served. Where policy makes successful notice a condition of lapse, hold the case until the authority records the required notice evidence or a permitted exception. At grace expiry, identify pending receipts, disputed identity and open exceptions before submitting the decision to the designated authority. Record renewal with MembershipDecision kind `renew`, or a policy-authorized lapse as a dated standing change and reason. Preserve earlier periods and the distinction between an unapproved proposal and an effective decision. A lapse changes only the rights specified by the applicable policy. See the [worked renewal and voting cutoff](../handbook/people/certifying-the-roll.md#paper-renewal-after-certification).
