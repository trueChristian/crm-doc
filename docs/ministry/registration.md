# Registration, tickets and attendance

An invitation is an offer to respond. A registration requests places. A seat is a capacity claim. A ticket grants admission under stated conditions. Attendance records an observation. Keep those meanings distinct so guests who walk in, people who paid and did not attend, and anonymous headcounts are reported accurately.

## Priced places and order-unit settlement

An admission desk can report why a particular seat was confirmed or cancelled. The shop owns the agreed prices and refund decisions. The registration records below connect each shop action to exactly the affected place.

| Logical record | Fields | Cardinality and validation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AdmissionSaleUnit | `place_id → RegistrationPlace`, `order_line_id → OrderLine`, `unit_ordinal count`, `component_key code`, `parent_bundle_line_id → OrderLine?`, `handoff_id → OrderHandoff`, `currency code`, `list_amount money`, `discount_amount money`, `allocated_net money`, `allocated_tax money`, `amount_due money`, `financial_state code`, `seat_outcome code` | One seat-bearing unit has one active place. Unique order line, positive unit ordinal and admission component. An unnamed three-seat allocation creates three stable units before individual holders are known. A separately priced adult and child receive separate line identifiers. Historical allocations survive a transfer or cancellation. |
| AdmissionSettlementPart | `unit_id → AdmissionSaleUnit`, `tender_allocation_id → TenderAllocation`, `handoff_id → OrderHandoff`, `allocated_amount money`, `recorded_at instant` | Many-to-many between units and capture allocations permits split tenders and one line with several seats. Unique unit and tender allocation pair. Reconcile unit totals to the quoted settlement requirement and line totals to commerce acknowledgements. No local payment is posted. |
| AdmissionSaleAdjustment | `unit_id → AdmissionSaleUnit`, `amendment_id → OrderAmendment`, `refund_allocation_id → RefundAllocation?`, `handoff_id → OrderHandoff`, `kind code`, `net_delta money`, `tax_delta money`, `state code`, `applied_at instant?`, `reason code` | Many adjustments per unit, each tied to the approved amendment and, for a monetary return, the applicable commerce refund allocation. Unique unit, amendment, handoff generation and refund allocation leg. Unit credits across all legs cannot exceed the eligible remaining amount. Pending, applied, declined and failed are distinguishable. |

Allocate a family discount, bundled service price or rounding remainder once from the accepted quote using commerce's policy. Allocation shares, currency and tax must reconcile to the seat-bearing line or its priced parent bundle. A ticket included at zero incremental charge can still carry an allocated share and an entitlement. For a refunded bundle, apply commerce's approved component allocation to the actual seat, meal, goods or media entitlement affected. Delivery and gifts stay on their own lines. A registration edit cannot remove a gift, move a line to another currency or recalculate old discounts using today's prices.

A verified admissions handoff identifies the priced line, unit ordinal, action and generation. Replays return the recorded result. Confirm a place only when the required funding is established or an authorized waiver exists and capacity is still available. If capture arrives after the hold expires and another person has the seat, record funds received and seat unavailable, refer the affected unit for alternative fulfilment or refund, and leave all other family admissions intact. A cancellation retires the ticket and releases the place once. During refund processing present “place released” and “refund pending” as distinct facts. Record subsequent failure or settlement without reissuing the seat. An unpriced, freely admitted guest needs no sale unit.

**Worked family booking.** One adult ticket lists at 60 with a 10 discount, giving 50 net plus 5 tax. One child ticket lists at 30 with a 5 discount, giving 25 net plus 2.50 tax. The booker adds a separately designated gift of 12.50, in the same currency. Capture is 95.00. Only 55.00 and 27.50 map to the two admission units. The giving handoff handles 12.50. Deliver the provider's success message twice and retry the admissions callback twice. The result must remain two funded places, two tickets, one gift and one recognized capture of 95.00. When the child alone cancels, retire only that ticket and request a 25.00 net and 2.50 tax refund against its own unit. After settlement, 67.50 remains received comprising the adult admission and gift. A failed refund marks the same adjustment for review. The adult's ticket, the gift and the available-seat count must remain unchanged by refund retries.

## Admission and changes

1. A family selects three places. Check eligibility, consent where applicable, sales dates, per-person restrictions and all capacity pools together. A group stays together unless the booker explicitly allows a partial allocation. Unpaid reservations use an expiring hold whose time is shown to the booker.
2. For a paid place, commerce owns order and payment. Confirmation uses a verified settlement or authorized payment state selected by event policy. A fee waiver records its authorizer, purpose and fee amount. If a late payment arrives after expiry, try a fresh allocation. When capacity has filled, route the recorded payment for refund or authorized alternative. Do not displace the current holder.
3. A cancellation makes a confirmed place available, retires active tickets and requests a refund assessment under the accepted terms. Releasing capacity and paying a refund each have a separate status. Partial cancellations leave the rest of the family booking intact. Fees, discounts and tax are corrected in commerce and finance.
4. Offer released places to the waiting list. An offer holds exactly the offered units until expiry. On timeout, release them once, update the entry and offer the next eligible set according to the published policy. Staff can see households skipped because of the contiguous-seat requirement.
5. At entry the steward checks validity, version and prior redemption. A duplicate scan displays the original redemption time to authorized staff. Re-entry policy can record exit and re-entry without adding another attendee. A ticket transfer checks the new holder's eligibility and replaces the credential. Printed, low-connectivity and walk-in workflows share the same reconciliation rules.

An offline device records a unique observation and receipt time. Without a live seat authority it can admit only preallocated inventory under a written local decision. Duplicate redemption across offline devices is flagged for review on synchronization. Recorded actual attendance survives an admission dispute. A guest may remain anonymous where registration and safeguarding policies allow it. A visitor explicitly requesting follow-up may be linked to a person later with staff review and unchanged historical counts.

## Attendance reports and examples

Attendance rolls can show expected, checked in, absent and unmarked separately. Count adults, children, volunteers and online attenders under explicitly named measures. A streamed view device can represent several people. Show device views separately from individually confirmed participants. A leader gets their own event's roster, a ticket checker gets minimal admission details, and an analyst can receive totals with small groups suppressed.

| Acceptance case | Observable result |
| --- | --- |
| Capacity is 50. There are 46 confirmed and three held places | One place remains bookable. An expiring hold releases its three places once, even under retry. |
| A registration for three meets room capacity of two | It waits as a unit unless the booker previously allowed a split. A seat guaranteed to another family remains unavailable. |
| A cancelled ticket is scanned after transfer | Staff see that the credential is invalid. Only the replacement is eligible. Both versions remain traceable without disclosing the new holder to the old holder. |
| 60 named people attended and ushers counted 82 people in total | Display 60 named and 82 total. If counts are comparable the estimate of unnamed attendees is 22. Never report 142. |
| Two scanners observe the same named attendee | Both scan observations can be audited. The named attendee total increases by one. |
| A welcome visitor has no name or email and declines follow-up | An authorized anonymous counter increments. No fabricated person, communication recipient or formal membership is created. |
