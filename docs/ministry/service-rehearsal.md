# Rehearse one Sunday from plan to reconciliation

Use this as an acceptance fixture for a church with two campuses, a public worship service, a children's class, a visiting family, a paid afternoon workshop and a volunteer driver. It demonstrates what each role can find when events, facilities, credentials, admission, finance and the register disagree. All people and quantities are illustrative.

## Starting records

The adult service has one EventOccurrence, 70 public places and 68 confirmed individual places. The children's session has a separate occurrence, a 12-place supervised room, ten admitted children and two qualified staff present under a locally configured maximum of six children per present staff member. The afternoon music workshop has a separate occurrence, 15 places, 14 confirmed places and one waiting request for two adjacent places. Seat 14 is confirmed and seat 15 is free. A waiting-list policy allows a booker to accept separated places only after explicit consent. A driver has an accepted return shift and six passenger seats. Create one household with two confirmed adult members, a guest child and a second adult who lives elsewhere and holds current child-specific collection authority. Keep the guest child's formal affiliation unset.

| Time | Actor and action | Observable state and handoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saturday | Secretary publishes the three occurrences and final room assignments | Public sees only approved locations and times. Session leaders see scoped rosters. Personal itineraries retain distinct worship, supervision and workshop entries. |
| 08:20 | A new guest takes one place at worship | Worship reaches 69 confirmed. Group and church membership totals are unaffected. One welcome invitation is possible only if the guest selected follow-up. |
| 08:40 | Reception admits the visiting child to the children's session with verified consent | The child becomes the eleventh supervised child and one distinct named attendee. There is one active room stay and no inferred permission for adults sharing the household. |
| 08:55 | One of the two qualified child supervisors leaves unexpectedly | The current ratio exceeds the selected limit. Pause further admission, alert the duty lead and coordinate the approved response for the eleven children already present. No automatic removal, room transfer or change of custody occurs. |
| 09:00 | A suggested replacement has valid training, availability and a conflicting assignment at the second campus | The coordinator sees the travel conflict and can choose a different qualified volunteer. A software suggestion cannot count as staffed coverage. |
| 09:05 | A different approved replacement accepts, arrives and takes responsibility | Coverage is two qualified staff physically present, the duty lead rechecks the room, and admissions can resume. The original volunteer's history remains available. |
| 10:00 | Music coordinator makes an unscheduled substitution | The live order records actual music use. A pending archive-permission decision holds that portion in the publishing workflow; it does not change worship attendance. |
| 10:15 | The workshop attendee holding seat 14 cancels | Two workshop places are now open. Offer them to the waiting request once, with an expiry. The external payment provider reports the original booking’s refund state to the authorized reviewer. |
| 10:20 | Workshop holders accept both places within the offer window and complete the applicable payment | There are 15 confirmed attendees. Issue tickets and participant-only access details once. A duplicate settlement notice cannot create new places. |
| 10:25 | The collector living in another household arrives for the visiting child | Staff recheck RepresentationAuthority and restrictions for this child, this collector and the current time. Successful collection closes exactly one stay and one custody record. |
| 10:35 | The transport lead changes a passenger's drop-off point | Validate route, capacity segment, permitted handover and arrival arrangements. Present the driver with the new stop, keep the restricted underlying instruction private and record acknowledgement. |
| Monday | Teams reconcile counts, hours and exceptions | Report worship and workshop bookings, actual named attendance, anonymous counts, child custody closure, actual music use, fulfilled shifts and unsettled transactions under their own measures. A person attending two activities counts once in a distinct-person Sunday total. |

## Optional planning aids with verifiable limits

| Capability | Required inputs | Human decision and acceptance test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compare two proposed timetables | Published occurrence revisions, draft times, travel allowances, confirmed seats, held bookings and accessible-space requirements | A planner can compare “later start” and “change room,” see conflicting shifts, displaced registrations and unmet access needs, then discard either draft without changing live schedules. Recheck current records before approval. |
| Suggest rota candidates | Position requirements, valid credentials, recent workload, actual availability and declared preferences | Explain each public criterion to the coordinator, flag unknown eligibility for review and offer a manual selection. Similar-sounding names cannot substitute for identity. A suggestion creates no assignment or notification. |
| Detect pressure before Sunday | Time-bound capacity, actual staffing, confirmed and held places, resourcing decisions and their observation times | Show counts, shortage and timestamp, permit drill-down only in scope, and suppress protected person or group data in aggregate views. When the feed is stale, display uncertainty and ask for confirmation. |
| Prepare an accessible itinerary | Approved public or personal times, authorized locations, language and explicitly shared access preferences | A participant previews plain-language, large-print or low-bandwidth versions. Review private details before sharing a household copy. Never expose another attendee's care arrangement. |
| Summarize a post-event review | Authorized fact records, approved event history and reviewed outcome measures | Present a draft with a traceable record reference per material claim, label unresolved discrepancies, let the owner correct and approve it before distribution. Restricted pastoral, child or medical detail stays outside a general review. |

## Rehearsal completion

Repeat the fixture while connectivity is interrupted after ticket redemption and after arrival but before a room transfer acknowledgement. Reconcile staff observations, pending notifications and returned scanner records by their original keys. A safe recovery leaves one confirmed financial effect, one admission entitlement per place, one active or closed custody chain per child and a named owner for every unresolved discrepancy. Record which offline actions the church authorizes before the exercise.
