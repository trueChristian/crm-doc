# Church classes, mentoring and external learning

A Sunday school coordinator needs a room, a leader, a roster and a safe way to return each child to an authorized adult. A newcomer may attend an orientation meeting and request a conversation with an elder. A volunteer may supply evidence of a qualification from another institution. Each of these church activities works with a paper register or a church app and can be completed while an external learning service is unavailable.

## Which system can decide?

| Question | Authority | What the church operator sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who has a place in the Sunday class this term? | Group coordinator accepts GroupParticipation under the applicable GroupTerm, scope and capacity. | Effective roster, waiting requests and the authority to admit a visitor at a specific meeting. |
| Who taught or attended on Sunday? Who collected a child? | Ministry records a dated assignment and actual attendance. Child supervision records custody and release. | The assigned leader gets the scoped class roll. The collection steward sees current collection authority and the outstanding room count. |
| Who requested a mentor, a visit or membership preparation? | The church assigns the agreed follow-up, access, due date and owner. | The responsible worker sees the private next action. The rota and class directory exclude private conversation notes. |
| Who owns a course, enrolment, assessments, grades and awards? | The separate learning management system (LMS) issues and amends academic records. | If authorized for a church purpose, the reviewer can see an issuer's limited attestation, its source and whether it is current. |
| Is a person suitable to serve in this role or ready for a local ceremony review? | The clearance reviewer or ceremony coordinator applies the congregation's policy to the evidence. The registrar verifies an actual rite separately. | A decision about the requested role or preparation item, the reviewer, effective dates and any outstanding action. |

## Set up and run a church class

The coordinator establishes the group, dates, responsible leaders and meeting occurrences. A term can run across civil years or remain open. Use a GroupMeeting link to identify the term roster for each occurrence. A scheduled class occurrence records the room, local time and accessibility arrangements. Assign supervised rooms and approved helpers for the actual occurrence. A place on a regular roster creates neither a check-in nor a presumption that a parent can collect someone.

Invite or accept one identified person per participation spell. Use a verified representative for a dependent where required. A one-time visitor can take an available place without joining the term. An anonymous attendance estimate can support planning at an open meeting. A supervised child needs the identity, emergency arrangements and verified handover required by the supervision policy. A household spanning two campuses should have a single local person record per child, separate group participation where appropriate, and an independent custody trail per visit.

A teacher may take the class roll during a bounded assignment. Substitute leaders receive time-limited access to the relevant class. Close the term by resolving outstanding headcounts, custody issues, waiting requests and handovers of responsibility. Publish renewal invitations and contact people by their permitted route. The previous term's attendance remains attributable to its original meeting and recorder. See [groups](groups.md), [registration](registration.md), [volunteers](volunteers.md) and [supervision](child-check-in.md).

## Optional exchange with an LMS

| Direction and purpose | Minimum contract | Church-side effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Church to LMS, requested introduction or enrolment referral | Authorized subject or verified representative, recipient institution, scoped subject mapping, requested external offering reference, purpose, consent or other recorded authority, request key and contract edition. Share a permitted contact route only when needed. | Record referral delivery and subsequent acceptance, refusal or expiry. Offer a manual introduction if the person cannot use an app. The LMS decides admission to its offering. |
| LMS to church, requested eligibility evidence | Verified issuer/connection, recipient church, scoped subject mapping, external assertion key and ordered revision, designated requirement code, attested status, effective and optional expiry dates, and a correction or withdrawal reference when applicable. | Match to an authorized open church requirement. The reviewer may accept it, decline it or request clarification. No new member, rite, shift or child collection right follows from receipt. |
| Church to LMS, actual participation at an agreed meeting | Only if separately authorized, send the scoped subject, source occurrence key, observed attendance state, observation time, revision and correction link. | The LMS acknowledges receipt and applies its own academic policy. No inferred progress or assessment is sent. |
| Connection disabled or sharing permission withdrawn | Record effective time, revoke the connection or subject grant and stop queued optional disclosures. Give the coordinator a result for each outstanding request. | Existing church classes continue. A reviewer can inspect a permitted issuer document or obtain direct confirmation. Outstanding requirements stay pending until decided. |

Use a separate [external identity mapping](../implementation/platform/ExternalKey.md) per source and church scope. A matching name, shared family email or duplicate contact is insufficient to link a result. Validate permitted purpose, current authority, source identity, recipient, record revision and agreement on the requirement before applying a notice. Persist its [inbound receipt](../implementation/platform/InboundReceipt.md) even when its business disposition is pending or declined, within the approved retention period. Quarantine a conflicting duplicate and withhold the business action. A later suspension, expiry or corrected subject reopens the responsible church review and triggers a fresh check of future assignments. A documented local ceremony remains a fact until the registrar authorizes an amendment.

A church app may display the signed-in person's referrals, church class roster rights or review status using the same scoped API as staff. A reviewer may use an assisted interface to find an unresolved requirement. Each operation uses the principal's church and record permissions. A roster export, ministry chatbot or calendar feed must exclude academic scores, assessment answers, pastoral notes and private clearance evidence.
