# Banking, cash and currencies

A church may maintain several bank accounts, a mobile wallet, card clearing, cash drawers, foreign missions accounts and petty cash. Each instrument has an accountable owner. Access to bank identifiers and statement attachments differs from access to congregation-level financial totals.

## Month-end matching and control totals

Load a statement with opening 1,000, cash inflow 430 and payments of 100. Closing balance must be 1,330. The actual ledger contains a 200 late deposit still in transit on the bank statement and 50 outstanding issued payments. Adjusted bank is 1,480. If books show 1,490 before a bank fee of 10, debit fees 10 and credit bank 10. Approve only after both adjusted amounts equal 1,480 and each outstanding item has a source and expected clearing date. Carry unresolved timing items forward until matched or reversed. A second reviewer can reopen a reconciliation with reason; keep the originally signed snapshot.

A matching assistant may propose a 500 statement credit for two deposits of 300 and 200. It must show the evidence and confidence to the reviewer. An equal amount from a different currency or account is ineligible. A transfer between two bank accounts creates no income or expense apart from identified fees or FX. When the legs clear on different days, show transit and age it.

## Foreign-currency acceptance

Use base units per foreign unit. A supplier bill of 100 foreign at 10 base per unit debits expense 1,000 and credits AP 1,000. At period end, a closing rate of 11 raises the liability to 1,100: debit unrealized FX loss 100 and credit AP 100. Settle at 10.5 with a nonreversing valuation policy: debit AP 1,100, credit bank 1,050 and credit FX gain 50. Total net exchange loss over the life is 50. Under a reversing valuation policy, reverse the earlier valuation once before realizing the settlement difference. Reconcile transaction units, historical base, closing base, realized and unrealized movement.

Reject a negative or missing applicable rate. Store rounding residual to an approved account within a configurable tolerance with source reference; above tolerance demand review. A bank transfer fee in another currency keeps its own rate and posting. Do not aggregate raw amounts across currencies in donor reports. Show currency headings or a clearly labeled translated basis.
