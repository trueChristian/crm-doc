# Campaigns and specialized gifts

Churches may fund a building over several years, support a missionary through sponsorship, sell tickets at a benefit dinner, inherit property, receive publicly traded securities or manage an endowment. Operational fundraising totals and accounting balances serve different questions. Show separately money received, promises remaining, matching money confirmed, donated property, campaign costs, commitments and actual spendable resources.

## Examples

**Matching appeal.** A sponsor commits one matching unit for each two donated, capped at 400. Qualifying gifts of 600 earn a match of 300. If another 300 arrives during the eligible window, only 100 cap remains. The ordinary gift total is 900, earned sponsor match 400, uncollected match 400 until paid. When cash arrives for 400, the campaign can show 1,300 collected. No soft-credit record or reminder creates more cash. A returned qualifying gift triggers reevaluation of claimed or already paid match and sponsor communication.

**Gift of securities.** Suppose the church accepts ten shares, recognises them at 10 each and sells all ten for gross 106 with selling fees of 2. At receipt debit investments and credit contribution income 100 under the example policy. At sale debit cash/settlement receivable 104 and selling fees 2, credit investment carrying amount 100 and realized gain 6. If carrying value had already been remeasured, use the actual carrying value and the approved realization presentation. A donor acknowledgement can describe units and date while applying the church's permitted valuation wording.

**Endowment.** A policy-authorized spending rate of 4% against an eligible measured base of 25,000 gives a theoretical limit of 1,000. A protected principal floor or restricted instrument may reduce that amount further. If only 700 is approved and permitted, record that 700 draw separately from administration fees and market valuation. An authorized transfer to general operations moves resources internally and does not create an external gift. The governing report shows opening value, gifts, return, fees, approved distributions, closing value, protected amounts and amount available under the rule.

**Fundraising meal.** Under a locally approved allocation policy a ticket of 100 with a 40 benefit produces 40 exchange revenue and 60 contribution if those criteria are met. Charge ticket tax only under the applicable classification. The buyer, donor receipt, event attendance and settlement each refer to the same transaction without sharing confidential donor details with check-in volunteers.

## Acceptance

A donor pledges 2,000 for construction but pays 500 now. The public progress view states whether it displays 500 received or 2,000 committed and never silently adds them to 2,500. A notified bequest has no budget availability solely because its expected range is high. Declining a restricted gift records authorized refusal and return of funds where received. When a memorial gift names a deceased person, legal donor, private honoree reference and approved public dedication are distinct. Revocation of publicity consent removes future public naming without altering the signed accounting history.
