# Noncash, memorial, matching and planned gifts

The [donor service desk](../development/donor-service.md) owns gift offers, permission to publicize an acknowledgement, estate notices, tributes and follow-up. The [appeal register](../development/appeals.md) owns fundraising goals, match terms and progress. The giving register records what actually reached the church and the donor’s directions. Use the relevant shared identity when several officers work the same case.

| Situation | Church record and decisions | Information crossing to another service |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Donated goods or temporary use of space | Assess conditions, authority to accept, fiscal costs referred for advice, availability, condition, quantity and custodian. Record the quantity actually accepted, any declined portion and final ministry handover. A loan has a return date and custody owner. | Approved receipt description, date and terms may be sent to accounting or a property system. A returned valuation carries its issuer, basis and date. |
| Donated service | Specify offered work, access/clearance requirements, acceptance or refusal, hours actually delivered and supervisor confirmation. Follow ordinary volunteer and safeguarding checks. | Supply approved service evidence if requested. Courtesy correspondence can describe hours or work completed. |
| Memorial or tribute | Record the actual giving party, honoree, intended beneficiary and instructions for any notification separately. Confirm the person authorized to receive a family notice and permitted wording. | A public dedication, family notification and receipt each use separate authority. A partner or executor’s name and home address stay outside a routine accounting payload. |
| Matching offer | Keep signed terms, eligible purpose and dates, ratio, cap, rounding and allocation order with the appeal. Qualifying allocations reserve portions under the same agreement. An amendment affects the appropriate edition. | A sponsor’s actual payment gets a separate gift reference. Report earned, paid, denied and outstanding match separately. Return of a qualifying gift triggers an evidenced reassessment. |
| Bequest or property distribution | Log a confidential intention, notification, verification of representative, terms decision, custody and actual distributions. A revoked intention stops follow-up and predicted totals. | Transfer acceptance evidence, actual cash and noncash quantities separately. An accounting valuation, sale or estate adjustment remains identifiable as an external decision. |
| Benefit evening or sponsored activity | Distinguish registration/payer, benefit purchase, optional monetary gift, sponsor obligation and named donor. Record the specific parts supplied and accepted under the approved arrangement. | An authority supplies any approved receipt treatment or later return split. Only the contribution portion enters the giving register. A fee-only transaction has no gift. |

For a matching sponsor contributing one unit for each two qualifying units with a cap of 400, qualifying gifts of 600 and then 300 earn 300 and 100. Record 900 in verified gifts, 400 earned match and zero sponsor cash until the sponsor actually pays. If the sponsor then pays all 400 as one gift, the received total rises to 1,300. Two match links to that one sponsor gift do not increase it further. For a return after settlement, reopen the affected qualification under the agreed terms. Do not manufacture an outgoing transfer absent the sponsor’s decision and evidence of the actual transfer.

A church accepts eight of ten offered chairs and arranges collection by the hall steward. Store eight received units and the disposition of the other two. A donor letter can acknowledge those eight chairs before an accounting authority supplies a value. If a later sale is permitted, retain its external reference and any change in physical custody. The sale does not add a ninth chair, a second gift or a valuation to the original courtesy letter.
