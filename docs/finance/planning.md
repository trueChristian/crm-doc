# Budgeting, cost allocation and cash forecasting

A ministry proposes its coming year's activities and resource needs. The finance team aligns them to funds, donor restrictions, staffing, capital plans and expected income. The board or congregation approves a named version. Actuals are drawn from posted financial activity, while forecasts, open commitments and requests remain separately visible.

## Availability, controls and presentation

For a ministry expense line with approved 1,000, posted expense 300 and uncancelled open orders of 200, remaining authority is 500. A 100 bill against those orders moves 100 from commitment to actual: expenses become 400, commitments 100 and remaining authority stays 500. If its receipt already accrues an expense, replace the commitment at receipt so the subsequent invoice cannot consume the budget twice. A fresh 550 request is blocked or referred to a named override authority according to policy. A soft alert still records the decision and reason.

For income display actual minus budget as favorable when positive; for expense display budget minus actual as favorable when positive. State which convention applies and handle zero budget as an amount with percentage unavailable. Show actual-to-date against phased budget to date, full-year approved, forecast outturn, commitments and restricted availability. If budget approval is pending at the year boundary, use the configured interim authority or hold new discretionary commitments. Prior-year multi-year commitments carry forward only by an approved rule.

Allocate a shared utility charge of 101 by room area in weights 2:1. At two decimal places, one target receives 67.33 and the other 33.67 under a largest-remainder rule. Debit target expense dimensions and credit the shared expense pool to leave total church expense 101. In a balanced-by-fund book, supply reciprocal fund balancing lines when appropriate. Permission to enter forecast assumptions does not permit approval of a transfer or release of donor restrictions.

## Acceptance and decision support

- The annual budget report can reproduce the version known to the congregation on its approval date. A later transfer changes comparative columns labelled revised without overwriting the baseline.
- A ministry leader sees only their remit, upcoming renewals and uncommitted authority. A treasurer can compare several ministries, see held restrictions and explain a variance down to authorized documents.
- A finance team tests a scenario in which donations decline 15% and payroll remains unchanged. The cash forecast shows the first projected cash shortage, how the assumption affected the result and which balances are spendable. Publishing the scenario changes no journal or standing payment.
- A cancelled event releases future commitments, preserves sunk costs and shows the resulting forecast variance. A zero-denominator cost driver leaves the allocation draft unposted and identifies which evidence is missing.
