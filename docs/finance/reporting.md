## Outputs and reconciliations

| Output | Required controls and drill-down |
| --- | --- |
| General ledger and trial balance | Opening debit/credit balance, activity, closing balance per account and fund; drill to journal, rule and original document. Total debits equal total credits. An alternate-basis report names its adjustments. |
| Statement of financial position | At one date assets equal liabilities plus net assets. Show current/noncurrent classification where applicable, cash, donor receivables net of allowances, inventory, fixed assets net of depreciation, payables, deferrals and borrowing. |
| Statement of activities and changes in net assets | Opening net assets plus recognized income, less expenses, plus/minus other movements equals closing net assets. Show restricted/unrestricted, releases, internal transfers and comparatives without double-counting movements in combined totals. |
| Cash flow statement | Identify cash equivalents, opening and closing cash, operating, investing and financing flows under selected policy, effect of FX, internal transfers excluded, noncash events disclosed. Direct method uses classified cash transaction portions. Indirect method reconciles result to operating cash via noncash and working-capital adjustments. Both reconcile to the same cash movement. |
| Functional expenditure and fund roll-forward | Split expense by nature and approved ministry/program, administration or fundraising function. Reconcile pool allocations. Show opening resources, additions, releases, transfers, expenses and closing resources by fund. |
| Subledger controls | Tie AP/AR aging, grant and pledge balances, payroll liabilities, gift batches, bank deposits, merchant clearing, inventory valuation, fixed assets and tax return balances to the corresponding ledger controls at the same cutoff. Explain authorized reconciling items. |
| Board, member and sponsor packs | Summarize budget against actual, liquidity, obligations, grants and outstanding actions. A publish step removes donor and employee detail, bank identifiers and sensitive ministry locations. Use accessible numeric tables, labeled charts and period comparatives. |
| Audit and regulatory pack | Trial balance, journal detail, bank reconciliations, source index, approved accounting policies and revisions, allocation evidence, funds, restrictions, grants and related parties, retained submissions/receipts and adjustment schedules, scoped to the authorized reviewer. |

## Close procedure

1. Establish cutoff in local fiscal date and restrict regular posting. List unmatched sources, unposted approved batches, pending fees/refunds and unusual manual journals.
2. Reconcile bank, transit and cash; clear or explain aged items. Tie receivables, pledges, payables, inventory, payroll and other control accounts to supporting registers. Account for late bank/provider information using approved policy.
3. Record authorized accruals, prepayments, deferred income, allowances, depreciation, stock adjustments, FX valuation, grant recognition/releases and investment changes. Review unspent restricted balances, related-party activity and significant commitments.
4. Run a balanced trial balance and all statement equations; investigate unexpected balances, missing chart mappings and any major budget variances. The reviewer signs the exception register and statement pack.
5. Close the period and issue versioned snapshots. At year end, carry forward balance-sheet and fund balances. Either roll nominal balances into the appropriate net asset classes or derive them through a controlled virtual close. Opening net assets and prior-year closing net assets must agree exactly. A year close must not repeat the activity as current-year income.
6. For approved post-close adjustment, determine subsequent-period entry versus reopening or restatement. Record approval, adjustment, revised snapshots, recipients and reissued filings where applicable. An unchanged as-issued view remains accessible.

## Consolidated example and acceptance

Entity A sends 1,000 to Entity B as internal support. A reports transfer expense and B transfer income. An approved group elimination debits internal income 1,000 and credits internal expense 1,000. If unpaid at year-end, eliminate matching inter-entity receivable and payable 1,000 separately. Exclude the internal cash movement from group cash flows. Both local books retain their original transactions. If B recorded 980 due to a bank fee, match the gross event, identify the 20 external fee and settle the currency/timing explanation before sign-off.

Map local charts to one group chart, align reporting dates/bases, translate component reports using approved rate classes, and disclose translation effects. A reviewer can drill from a group number to local submitted amounts, consolidation adjustments and counterparty pairs. Permission to review consolidated totals does not confer local access to donor records. Test that missing member submissions, unmatched intercompany balances, failed cash reconciliation and unmapped material accounts prevent an unqualified final pack under the configured close policy.
