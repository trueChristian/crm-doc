# Long-lived assets, borrowing and leases

The facilities register tracks custody, safety, physical location and maintenance. The finance asset register tracks ownership, value, funding, measurement and accounting. One building can have components with different service lives. Donated, purchased, constructed, leased and borrowed items can appear operationally; finance recognizes only the appropriate rights and obligations under the church's approved policy.

## Worked asset lifecycle

Buy an AV system for 1,200, commissioned at the start of a month, zero residual and 24 full months of straight-line use. Debit equipment and credit bank or payable 1,200. A monthly debit depreciation expense and credit accumulated depreciation of 50 gives 300 accumulated depreciation after six months and a carrying value of 900. Dispose immediately after the sixth charge for cash of 750. Debit bank 750, accumulated depreciation 300 and disposal loss 150; credit equipment cost 1,200. The closing carrying value is zero. Disable subsequent depreciation. For a sale already booked to a receivable, clear disposal proceeds through the linked sale without duplicating the bank receipt.

A loan draw of 5,000 debits bank and credits borrowing 5,000. A payment with principal 400 and interest 50 debits loan liability 400 and interest expense 50 and credits bank 450, leaving principal 4,600. Where interest was already accrued, debit interest payable instead. A financing agreement paid directly to the supplier may be noncash at inception and belongs in the noncash disclosure, not fabricated bank movement.

## Acceptance

The fixed-asset roll-forward shows opening cost and accumulated depreciation, additions, transfers, disposals and closing values tied to the ledger by class and fund. If a 100 repair is capitalized in error, an approved correction can reclassify the cost and reverse excess depreciation while preserving the invoice and asset history. Physical stocktake can flag an unlocated item for investigation without writing off its carrying value automatically. A restricted-funded instrument may not be disposed until the remaining donor conditions are recorded. A new variable loan rate produces a dated schedule revision and future reminders while retaining already paid interest.
