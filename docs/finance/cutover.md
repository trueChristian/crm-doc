# Financial onboarding, cutover and handover

A treasurer taking over a spreadsheet or another accounting system must be able to establish an opening position, collect current giving while migration is reviewed, and explain historical figures later. Select a cutover day, legacy closing book and historical scope. A balance-only import is sufficient for historic general ledger totals only when the required operational open items and traceability are separately supplied. A full-history import brings dated documents, adjustments and reconciliations with a frozen legacy evidence index.

## Opening reconciliation

1. Freeze a reproducible extract and record totals, source identities, permissions, legal entity and calendar. Confirm signed prior close, available bank statements, last issued numbers and who may approve cutover.
2. Map currencies, chart, people, suppliers, funds, restrictions, dimensions, bank accounts and periods. Keep a distinction between anonymous and unresolved identities; a donor's corrected contact must not create another gift.
3. Validate opening A/R by customer/invoice and A/P by supplier/bill, including unpaid installments, credits, unapplied cash and overpayments. Confirm restrictions, advances, deferred income, deposits in transit, uncleared cheques and refundable merchant reserves. Reconstruct unpaid tax/payroll liabilities by authority and due date. Bring inventory with quantities and costs, and fixed assets with original cost, in-service dates, depreciation to date and remaining life.
4. Load into a rehearsal book. Compare the historic signed trial balance to the new opening trial balance and every control account to accepted open items. Confirm cashbook balances reconcile to the last statement with timing items preserved. Verify first post-cutover reconciliation can match an imported outstanding item without generating fresh expense/revenue.
5. Freeze accepted mappings and approve. Record a single documented cutover boundary for legacy and new event sources. Route late legacy items through controlled adjustment. Keep the historical read-only pack, signoffs, source-to-target IDs and an exceptions register.
6. Recover failed imports by replaying approved rows idempotently in isolated batches with control totals. Before accepting production records, an unapproved rehearsal can be discarded. After accepted postings, correct through linked entries or an authorized formal restatement. Derive previous- and current-period reporting from explicitly covered history.

## Worked opening

A signed opening trial balance shows bank 500, customer receivables 120, inventory 80, supplier payables 100, restricted net assets 150 and general opening net assets 450. Debits and credits both total 700. Two customer invoices outstanding for 70 and 50 support AR 120; one supplier bill supports AP 100. If operational open items create the AR/AP controls, exclude those identical balances from the direct trial balance loader and use an approved balanced opening-clearing mechanism that closes to zero. A subsequent collection of 70 debits bank and credits AR, leaving customer aging 50 with no new sales income.

## Acceptance

A trial migration sharing a supplier invoice number between two different legal entities keeps both correctly scoped. Opening value 80 for ten units yields an average of 8 under the fixture policy. A 20 cheque already deducted from ledger bank 500 but outstanding at cutoff can clear on the next statement without another payment entry; with no other timing items, the opening statement is 520. The treasurer can export all controls, approval evidence and outstanding items to an independently readable handover pack. Removing the outgoing officer's signing and application access does not remove their historic approvals.
