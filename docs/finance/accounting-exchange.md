# Accounting exchange and source ownership

A church can connect a familiar accounting package, a separately deployed bookkeeping application or a reviewed file exchange. Giving, worship, membership, events and donor correspondence can operate with the accounting connection disabled. This chapter defines the facts the church can exchange, who can decide them, and how to resume safely after an interruption. It requires no particular vendor, ledger design, API route or Joomla component layout.

## Ownership by transaction

| Fact | Authority | Church action and permitted result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member, visitor, giving identity, accepted donor instruction and verified allocation | Church register or authorized originating giving service as agreed for the specific channel | Record verified evidence and a private donor relationship. Resolve the origin before exporting so a gift imported from the accountant cannot be echoed back as a new gift. |
| Online capture, return, chargeback or reinstatement | Payment service or authenticated intake authority for that account | Match on source account and transaction/adjustment identity. Capture, settlement and fee events have different types. Hold unmatched or ambiguous corrections. |
| Physical count, custody and shortage | Approved counters, custodians and church reviewer | Keep an independent count and discrepancy history. Releasing a bag and receiving an accounting acknowledgement each have their own time and evidence. |
| Paid registration, optional gift and refund split | Registration/payment owner and approved allocation authority | The CRM can provide registered participant, payer link where permitted, fee/gift portions and service reference. Return to registration only the amount/status needed to decide admission or cancellation. |
| Approved benevolence, grant or reimbursement request | An authorized church officer approves the request. The payment or accounting owner executes it. | Send a restricted beneficiary or external payee reference, approved amount, currency, purpose, authorization and due date. The response may say awaiting review, declined, paid, partially paid or reversed. Never include pastoral assessment in the ordinary payload. |
| Fee, invoice, bank match, ledger posting, budget availability, formal receipt eligibility, payroll or financial statement | External accounting, payment or designated receipt issuer as applicable | Keep the external reference, publisher, status and effective/as-of time when the church needs it. An application may expose an authorized summary and a private deep link, with independent access checked at the destination. |

Connections advertise which operations and directions they support, accepted currencies and dates, how they identify events, lookup and deduplication capability, atomic group support and acknowledgement statuses. An outbound-only receiver does not imply support for importing balances. A church that uses a bookkeeper’s file can reconcile the same manifest by item even if it lacks live status updates. A simple export recipient can receive a file and confirm delivery without certifying individual application. Show the observed level of confirmation.

## Connection setup and cutover

The liaison selects the receiving church/legal entity, system account, authorized direction, allowed data purpose, designated owner per channel, status authority and operating contacts. Approve a mapping from each CRM purpose, campus, event or support category used in the exchange to the destination’s scoped codes. Version and date each mapping. Validate exact decimal representation, original currency, permissible fraction, effective church date, timestamp with offset, external key case/leading zeros, supported adjustment types and privacy rules. Require distinct roles for approving mappings and releasing sensitive batches where church policy requires.

Choose effective start/end dates and an explicit historical range. Preview incoming and outgoing items, duplicates, conflicts, totals and unmapped rows using a nonposting dry run under the normal disclosure restrictions. The liaison and accountant agree on a last acknowledged source key or cutoff. Already delivered or partner-owned history receives its existing external references. Archive receipts and open exceptions before changing providers. Moving a connection to a new endpoint or new church account never authorizes sending the entire history automatically. On disconnect, revoke access, stop scheduling deliveries and preserve local church evidence. Register any queued events as retained, abandoned by authorized decision, or assigned to an explicitly approved replacement.

## A monetary gift at the boundary

The names below describe a logical versioned payload. JSON, a row group in a file or a connector-native request can represent it. Different providers may expose additional operations outside this contract.

| Field group | Gift verification event | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | Church, source account/namespace, immutable business event key, type, source gift key, source revision, optional causation key and original source portion. A destination connection is part of the release context. | Return the source key, revision, unique business response ID and scoped destination reference where assigned. |
| Measurement | Local received/effective date, currency, exact gross contribution before provider fees, stable line keys, mapped purpose/edition and exact amount of each allocation. Send associated benefit/fee details only if within the agreed capability. | Report accepted, rejected or unresolved item groups, reason codes, decision time, currency and amount where the recipient confirms them. A transport-only receipt carries no inference of business acceptance. |
| Privacy | Minimal church/campus and donor token only when required by the signed configuration. Accounting-only aggregate delivery may omit donor identity completely. Grouping preserves protected church-side source/line traceability. | Permit status and minimized evidence to licensed roles in the church. No raw unfiltered provider error messages in donor or volunteer interfaces. |
| Dependencies | A return references the original gift, line keys, approved currency amounts and any earlier adjustment. An amended designation references prior terms and new approved mapping. | Unknown prerequisite, stale revision, missing purpose code or mixed currency results in a recoverable held or rejected decision. Preserve the original released payload. |

Example in NAD. Input for G42 has source `giving-office/NORTH/042`, revision 1, two allocations of 90 General and 30 Youth, and a verified gross amount of 120. The receiving service can reply “received” immediately and “accepted” later with its reference R615. The two replies acknowledge one event. A later confirmed return of 20 against the Youth line is another business event with its own source identity, original source line, amount and date. The current giving report then shows 90 General and 10 Youth. An accounting operator's separate fee of 3 or a subsequent payout of 97 has its own meaning. Neither reduces the donor’s net 100.

## Inbound authority and privacy

Accept inbound facts from a church-authorized source principal, bound to an organization, connection, direction, operation and data scope. Use source-namespace mapping for member or donor references. A donor ID reused by another processor or church identifies a different party. A payment token, name, email, address or matching amount alone cannot merge profiles. Unknown donor identity, unrecognized codes, duplicate-looking business events or conflicts with accepted church instructions enter a protected work queue. On resolution record who approved the mapping, the business effective date and the original source reference.

Inbound gift verification must reconcile the typed amounts and complete designation group before local verification. An application-supplied fee or pledge is never imported as a gift on account of a generic “paid” flag. A partial payout cannot stand in for a full gift, an unresolved return cannot be booked against the latest equal gift, and a donor correction cannot republish a name or change a person’s membership or permissions. A finance service can return a posting reference, official receipt status or authorized availability snapshot. It cannot edit a confidential pastoral record or substitute a new donor purpose.

If the recipient reports a changed amount or classification, record the discrepancy and invite the source owner to authorize a correction. Preserve the committed local church event and original outbound result. Scope aggregate totals, mapping errors, drill-downs, exported files, logs and MCP operations to the same restrictions as their sources. No routine payload needs prayer text, counselling notes, family conflict details, safeguarding history, full account numbers, card credentials or portal passwords.

## Delivery, recovery and evidence

1. Commit the church decision and its deliverable event durably together. Assign a business key and canonical digest before any network call. Reuse the platform’s [event and exchange protections](../platform/exchange-events.md) for approvals, attempts, authenticated callbacks, idempotency and audit.
2. Include the complete event group. The receiving service must apply the whole gift and its allocations atomically or return an explicit partial/unconfirmed disposition. An adapter using separate calls must track individual effects and withhold the group’s complete status until each effect is reconciled. An operator must never create a second gross gift to fix a missing allocation.
3. Record transport acceptance separately from business application. Retry transient failures with the same event key, digest, mapping and revision. A repeated response or webhook yields the prior result. A conflicting payload under an existing identity, a stale release following a correction or an unprecedented backwards transition requires review.
4. If an acknowledgement is lost, look up the original source key at the destination. Replay only when the receiver guarantees idempotency or the operator obtains evidence of nonapplication under an agreed recovery procedure. A connector without these capabilities stays uncertain until an authorized liaison checks, documents and resolves the case. An unauthenticated support message cannot mark it accepted.
5. Keep a manifest with per-currency counts, event-type totals, cutoff, scoped source keys, success/rejection counts and outstanding items. A difference report lists the affected line or event, assigned person, earliest unresolved time, safe reason and last action. Compare like populations and effective dates, including later returns. Distinguish excluded, pending and confirmed amounts.
6. For file exchange, preview before release, send through the permitted link, retain manifest identity and digest and record who retrieved it. Import a line-level response or authorized manual evidence where available. A corrected row retains source lineage and uses a new explicit revision where a business fact or approved mapping changes. Never rerun the entire file as new local transactions just because one row failed.

The ERPNext reference distinguishes submitted gifts, actual payment identities and financial records. Dolibarr distinguishes a public pledge, member application and checkout reference. Odoo documents the financial system’s distinct payment, statement and reconciliation states. The church adapts these ideas by appointing explicit owners and exchanging scoped evidence. The external system chooses its own accounting policy and processing model.
