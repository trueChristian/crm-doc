# Purchasing, payables and reimbursements

A ministry requests goods against its budget. A budget holder approves the purpose, a buyer orders, a receiver confirms delivery, accounts payable checks the invoice and a payment authorizer releases funds. Each approval has a scoped delegation, expiry and amount limit. Small churches can assign an independent after-the-fact review where policy allows and record the exception. A changed supplier bank destination requires a separate verification before release.

## Recognition and exceptions

- A purchase request or order reserves 500 against the budget if encumbrance control is enabled. It has no operating expense under the example policy. After acceptance, move the reservation to actual or accrued receipt exactly once.
- Receive goods worth 300 before the supplier invoices. Where receipts are accrued, debit inventory or expense 300 and credit goods received awaiting invoice 300. At matched billing, debit the receipt clearing account 300 and credit trade payables 300. If the first invoice differs by 12, route the variance to a tolerance policy and appropriate price variance, inventory or expense account. The simpler invoice-recognition policy skips receipt accrual and posts expense or inventory at invoice.
- Pay 220 against an approved 300 bill. Debit payables 220, credit bank or payments clearing 220. The item's open balance is 80. An unapplied supplier deposit instead debits supplier advances; application clears it against a later bill. A bounced transfer reverses disbursement and reopens the application.
- Issue 100 travel cash as an advance, receive qualifying expenses of 85 and a cash return of 15. Debit advance receivable and credit cash 100; debit travel and credit advances 85; debit cash and credit advances 15. The advance has zero balance and the claimant receives no further payment.

## Acceptance

A 50-unit order can receive 40 and be billed for 40, leaving 10 committed. An invoice for 45 enters the variance queue until policy or evidence resolves the extra five. A supplier's 20 credit appears once in payables, payment selection and the reconciliation. A cashier cannot update a destination and approve the same release. Cancelling an unreceived order releases its commitment but preserves any accepted receipts and posted bills. The AP aging at cutoff includes credit balances, posted installments, disputed items and payments in transit separately and agrees to its ledger control account by book and currency.
