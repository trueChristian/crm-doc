# Inventory accounting and stock valuation

Commerce owns catalog, selling price, physical location, orders and fulfilment. Finance owns financial valuation and its tie to the inventory control account. A repository for sermon downloads may have many licensed access rights while carrying no physical units. A printed sermon booklet can have trackable units, a sales price, a carrying cost and a separate digital entitlement.

## Purchasing, distributing and counting

For perpetual accounting with first-in-first-out, receive 10 hymnals at 4 and 10 at 6. Inventory costs 100 for 20 units. Deliver 12. Debit cost of sales and credit inventory 52 (10 × 4 + 2 × 6). Eight remain valued at 48. For a separately configured moving weighted-average policy, the average before sale is 5, cost of sales is 60 and eight remain valued at 40. Both outcomes are valid for their stated fixture; record the selected method in reports. A return uses traceable original assigned cost where required. An unsalable return enters quarantine, then an approved write-down or disposal.

A warehouse transfer moves units and value between responsible locations; the church as a whole records no sale. An outreach giveaway reduces inventory and debits approved ministry expense, funded appropriately. Donated saleable stock can recognize contribution income and supported inventory value under policy; selling it later recognizes sale proceeds and releases its carrying cost. Periodic accounting instead captures opening inventory, eligible purchases and closing valued count, with cost-of-sales adjustment once per period; avoid posting perpetual costs in the same population.

For a consigned book sold for 50 under an illustrative agency agreement retaining 10 commission, debit bank or clearing 50 and credit payable to publisher 40 and commission income 10, assuming no tax in this fixture. Paying the publisher clears the 40 liability. A changed agreement that makes the church principal requires a separate assessment and mapping.

## Acceptance

- A February count made after closing January cannot silently reassign the January carrying value. The reviewer records effective date, cutoff evidence and impact before approving any adjustment.
- Two simultaneous orders cannot both consume the final specific unit or cost layer. An authorized backorder remains unfulfilled and carries no cost of delivery until actual transfer.
- A book bundled with access has separate physical cost, digital grant and price allocation. The finance and fulfillment quantities reconcile across a physical return or a digital-only refund.
- Export roll-forward by book, legal owner, warehouse, item, lot, fund and currency. Opening value + receipts + recognized landed cost ± adjustments − outgoing cost = closing value. Confirm ledger control and explain stock that is present but belongs to another owner.
