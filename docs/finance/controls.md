# Financial controls, localization and exchanges

## Role and action matrix

| Actor | Normal scope | Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offering counter | Assigned collection, tenders and custody | Independent witness or separate count as configured. Count corrections are append-only. |
| Donor-services officer | Authorized giving identities, allocations and statements | Cannot change posted amounts by editing a receipt. A public recognition choice is separate from accounting identity. |
| Ministry budget holder | Spending requests, remaining authority and own dimensions | Own claim, related-party transaction or above-limit approval routes to an independent officer. |
| Bookkeeper | Source entry, matching proposals, journals and exception work | Bank-destination changes, outgoing release and own manual postings are independently authorized under threshold policies. |
| Treasurer | Review and execute authorized payments, periods, reports | Sensitive exports and exceptional overrides include purpose and audit. A conflict or delegation expiry redirects approval. |
| Payroll officer | Compensation, approved runs, filings, employee statements | Sensitive fields and payment instructions are masked outside authorized duties. |
| Governing body or members | Approved version of permitted reports | Recipients receive only the intended snapshot and level of detail. A member's own donor statement is a distinct private document. |
| Auditor | Selected books, time window, evidence, postings and revisions | Read-only grant, recorded export scope, expiry and revocation. Confidential supporting material can require separately scoped authorization. |

## Policy and posting decisions

Configure statutory identity, fiscal year, language, denomination, independent document numbering, rounding, taxes, permitted receipts, statement disclosures, reporting basis, currency rates and filing calendars per legal entity. Rate or form changes have effective dates and human approval. Do not assume charitable activity makes all sales exempt, all offerings tax-deductible, or clergy compensation subject to one treatment. Tax, payroll and statutory packs show jurisdiction, valid policy and reporting period. A missing required rule blocks issuance or queues a reviewed exception under policy.

Under an example 10% inclusive price of 110, taxable base is 100 and output tax 10. A 55 supplier expense comprising base 50 and tax 5 with 60% recoverability posts expense 52, recoverable input tax 3 and supplier liability 55. Output tax 10 less recoverable input tax 3 yields a net 7 liability before other adjustments. A credit note reverses its original basis and rate; retain any filing-period adjustment. A zero-rated item, exempt transaction and out-of-scope gift can all have zero tax but need distinct classifications. Validate rounding at the approved line or document level for mixed rates and currencies.

A sales event must supply legal entity, jurisdiction/tax reference, original order/line, currency, net, discount, tax, captured tender and effective dates. A donation event adds giving identity scope, allocations and conditional status. A payroll event carries totals by approved earning/deduction/control and dimension plus a restricted link to detailed slips. Inventory sends cost and quantity movement for sold/returned goods. Duplicate deliveries, reorderings, partial failure and corrections use the original source and unique event identity. An accounting response includes status, journal reference, applied rule version and actionable exceptions.

## Audit, portability and acceptance

Export chart, fund and dimension mappings, journal headers/lines, open items, reconciliations, document versions and a manifest with record counts and totals. Support original currency and base amounts, decimal precision, time zone and cutoff. A limited auditor export redacts or separately grants source evidence. Restore a backup into an isolated scope and compare signed trial balance, subledger aging, donor receipt sequences and latest closed snapshot before accepting recovery. Configure retention, holds, access expiry, masked exports and destruction approvals per record class and jurisdiction. When an operational contact is corrected, keep issued financial documents legally attributable under the adopted retention policy.

Risk signals may prioritize duplicate supplier invoices, unexpected destination changes, repeated round amounts or separation exceptions, with explainable evidence and human disposition. A suggestion can never release a payment, rewrite a closed period or accuse an individual. Verify an expired approval cannot be reused, a vendor account edit invalidates prior payment approval, a rerun cannot post salaries twice, a late refund links to the original sale and report generation never exposes confidential names to an aggregate-only audience.
