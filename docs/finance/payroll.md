# Payroll, stipends and remittances

Finance pays authorized employees, clergy and other payees under their approved classification and jurisdiction. Employment, calendars and work assignments originate in personnel operations. A reimbursement, honorarium, allowance, benevolence award and regular wage each need their actual classification, recipient and evidence. A ministry title alone cannot establish payroll or tax treatment.

## Calculation and exception path

Prepare a pay calendar and versioned rates. Import approved inputs with a cut-off; flag missing approvals, new payees, destination changes, unusually large changes, duplicate engagements and negative net pay for human decision. Preview a register, component breakdown, payment control and journal totals. Independent authorization freezes the run. Post expense, employee liabilities and employer costs, then send separately authorized payment instructions. Track each result and settle remittances by due date. A failed payout leaves the employee's net wages payable open until successful reissue.

Gross wages of 1,000, employee withholding of 150, an employee pension contribution of 50 and employer pension cost of 60 produce a net transfer of 800. Debit wages 1,000 and employer benefits 60. Credit employee net wages payable 800, withholding payable 150 and pension payable 110. Pay the worker with debit wages payable and credit bank 800. Remit 150 and 110 to their respective payees. Payroll expense totals 1,060, net wages 800, cumulative outflow after remittance 1,060. These are illustrative amounts rather than a tax table.

When a timesheet is corrected after payroll release, retain the prior payslip and create a delta in a permitted supplemental or later run. An overpayment becomes a tracked recoverable amount under approved recovery terms; a payroll operator cannot silently debit the worker's bank. Final pay accounts for approved earnings, leave, deductions, advances, access expiry and undelivered statements. A worker paid from two grants appears on one slip, with confidential cost allocation detail available to authorized finance staff.

## Acceptance

- Two clerks importing the same timesheet cannot generate two earnings. Re-running an unapproved calculation gives the same result against the same inputs.
- A person receiving both salary and reimbursable mileage sees separate classifications and no duplicate reimbursement. A noncash benefit can increase taxable base without increasing cash due.
- For the worked run, gross-to-net, general ledger, payments and remittance liabilities reconcile. Isolating an employee bank rejection leaves other employees and liabilities unchanged.
- A ministry manager approving a timesheet cannot view employee banking, personal tax identifiers or coworkers' pay. An auditor can verify run totals and selected details with access logged.
- An expired rate or missing local tax configuration blocks release of the affected pay group with an actionable error.
