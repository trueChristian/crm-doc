# Receivables, sales and payment clearing

A shop, ticket desk, venue rental or media service supplies order, delivery, tax and refund events. Finance owns the posting, outstanding receivable and settlement. Keep order, invoice, payment authorization, capture, entitlement activation, revenue recognition, refund, chargeback and bank payout distinct. Their dates can differ. A donor statement must never present the full price of a purchase as a gift.

## Worked settlement and refund

Assume delivery and transfer of sermon access both satisfy the sales obligation, an illustrative 10% sales tax applies to the physical item only, and the processor keeps its fee. A buyer pays 110 for a book (100 revenue and 10 tax), 20 for sermon access and a separate 30 donation. Debit processor clearing 160, credit book sales 100, tax payable 10, media sales 20 and contribution income 30. For a perpetual inventory book with cost 40, debit cost of goods sold 40 and credit inventory 40 when delivered. Preserve source line IDs and allocation mappings. If delivery is pending at capture, credit customer deposits/deferred income as appropriate and recognize income when its actual trigger occurs.

The processor pays 156. Debit bank 156 and fees 4, credit processor clearing 160. The statement matches the 156 deposit. Later, an approved 20 refund for sermon access debits sales returns 20 and credits processor clearing 20. If collected from bank separately, debit processor clearing 20 and credit bank 20. The resulting bank receipts net to 136 and processor clearing returns to zero. The gift and tax on the book remain intact. The media service independently records the entitlement change under the sale policy. For a refunded physical item, reverse the applicable tax and recognize any recoverable stock at its actual returned condition and cost. A nonrefundable fee remains expensed.

## Other receivables

Invoice 600 for a six-month program payable in advance under an assumed evenly delivered service policy. Debit AR 600 and credit deferred revenue 600. Cash clears AR, and six approved monthly entries of 100 debit deferral and credit program revenue. Early cancellation recalculates remaining obligations and issues any approved credit or refund. An invoiced hall rental may include a separately refundable security deposit recognized as a liability until returned or validly applied with evidence.

A collector records aging by due date with current, overdue, disputed and credit balances. Recognized allowances and authorized write-offs use linked postings; a later recovery is its own receipt. Retain unapplied cash separately from income. A correction to an invoice after statement issuance creates a credit or debit adjustment document rather than replacing its amounts.

## Acceptance

The same capture may arrive before its invoice; hold it unapplied and link when the invoice appears. Duplicate settlement imports must leave the bank, revenue and receivables unchanged. A partial refund can't exceed the unrefunded amount of its original tender and line. A lost chargeback on a previously refunded line flags a potential double debit for investigation. An online buyer can view their own invoice and refund status; finance can follow the full payment chain; a sermon editor sees entitlement status without donor history or payment credentials.
