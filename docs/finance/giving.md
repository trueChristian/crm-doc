# Offerings, gifts and donor correspondence

A church receives gifts at services, by post, at an office, through a payment provider or from another authorized register. A pledge, envelope, deposit, gift, attribution and statement answer different questions about the same activity. Each carries its own authority and identity.

## Intake, verification and custody

| Stage | Officer’s decision | Durable result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receive | Establish church, source account, channel, evidence, received date, currency and actual amount. Use a stable receipt or capture reference. Assign a receipt reference to the physical envelope or loose offering once. | One pending gift for each actual contribution or anonymous collection portion. A payer may represent another person or organization. |
| Identify and designate | Resolve a giver only with sufficient evidence. Capture original instructions, designation codes and permissions. Route ambiguous instructions, conflicting identities and closed designations for review. | One or more allocation lines per gift. A registration, order or family connection supplies a possible link only. |
| Count | Record each count round with its counter, witness, tender, date and denomination where used. Record anonymous loose offerings and preserve discrepancies. | One accepted round per batch. Select it independently of donor-identification progress, retaining every observation. |
| Verify | For each currency and tender, match actual kit, envelopes and loose intake to the selected count. For external captures, match to provider evidence for the precise source account and transaction. | Verified contributions have exact allocation totals. The giving officer can resolve one unidentified but otherwise admissible gift using the church’s protected unassigned-donor process. |
| Release | Apply local approval and segregation rules and release only the configured accounting handoff. Custodians independently sign dispatch and receipt of physical collections. | The gift, collection and destination handoffs remain individually traceable during a delay or outage. |
| Correct | Record an adjustment to the original allocation, effective date, cause, actor and evidence. Protect submitted editions and escalate ambiguity. | Net received, affected pledge progress and later donor correspondence reflect the approved correction exactly once. |

For a cash gift of 75 General and 25 Missions, the two allocations sum to 100 in one currency. Joining those allocations to the two independent count rounds still gives 100 received. A terminal fee of 3, an external settlement of 97 and a recurring promise to give 100 next month leave the verified contribution at 100. Keep each reporting population explicit. A weekly list of tender counts can differ in date or cutoff from a report of verified gifts. Obtain each population and exclusions before treating the difference as a physical shortage.

Separate unmarked money, missing donor identity, unresolved designation and unresolved count. An approved default purpose can apply to a truly unmarked gift. An explicit designation that cannot be honoured requires fresh instructions or an authorized return. A loose anonymous offering can contribute to a purpose total without a fictitious member or donor profile. An envelope with a donor code awaiting review remains excluded from named correspondence. A person giving privately may still receive their own authorized statement.

## Pledges and regular giving

A pledge records the donor's agreed schedule, currency, designation and conditions. An identified but unaccepted intention remains proposed. A confirmed gift can be matched to an installment when the purpose, currency and donor instructions agree. Show original promise, amendment, cash actually received, amount waived and remaining commitment separately. A change of designation or excess installment requires consent or explicit local handling. Preserve the instructions and prior edition. Closing an appeal or a donor’s death prompts authorized private review of future contacts and obligations.

For a commitment of 120 in four installments of 30, a receipt of 25 against the first leaves 5 of that installment outstanding. A confirmed return of 10 reduces the received money to 15 and reopens that amount. A second independent gift of 40 in another currency creates a separate gift. Linking it to the schedule requires an authorized conversion basis or explicit alternate instruction. Avoid deriving pledge progress from a live exchange rate.

The payment provider owns the charge and token. Record the consented amount, schedule, purpose, effective dates, provider reference and pause/revocation status where a donor has authorized recurring collection. A privilege to maintain a donor's address cannot authorize a new charge. A missed, pending, declined, late or retried collection has an operational follow-up and no received-gift amount until evidence confirms capture. A bank-originated standing order may be matched to repeated receipts without recording a mandate granting the church power to debit the donor.

## Acknowledgements, statements and corrections

A giving clerk checks the party, intended recipient authority, reporting period, gift currency, language, returned gifts and statement scope. A verified legal giver, a household, an honoree, a donor adviser and an authenticated payer may all be different. Each audience receives only its permitted information. Two adults at the same address may give personally, jointly under an express instruction, or for an organization. Create a single joint edition only for the covered gifts. Changing a representative or ending a household relationship requires an access review before re-delivery.

Choose a single issuer for each type of document and receipt series in the church. A CRM courtesy acknowledgement or giving-history statement can be issued independently. If accounting or another service issues official receipts, the CRM can deliver an authorized copy or store its scoped reference and correction status. While that issuer is unavailable, report the pending status and offer the permitted acknowledgement. No default tax status, deductible amount, filing rule or qualification follows from being a church. Freeze the issued content and retained source references. A delivery failure is retried against the same edition after checking current authority. An amount, identity or wording correction issues an identified replacement and preserves the original.

A private report may show gross receipts, confirmed returns, reinstatements, net gifts by designation, unassigned differences and unapplied commitments. Use the original currency and explicit local dates or a separately labelled external reporting basis. Public views require current recognition authority and disclosure-safe aggregation. An aggregate limited to one service, household or small group may reveal a donor even where all names are removed. Giving access remains distinct from pastoral care and benevolence access across staff, portal, reports, APIs and assisted tools.
