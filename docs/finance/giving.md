# Offerings, gifts and donor service

An officer can receive from a visitor, household, business, foundation, member or truly anonymous donor. Membership, household membership, the authenticated payer, legal donor and person receiving acknowledgement may differ. A partner in the same household gains no access to a private gift through the household relationship.

## Collection and attribution

Two counters count a sealed offering separately where policy requires. Envelope amounts and anonymous loose cash must reconcile to the selected count by currency and tender. Route differences for recount or documented resolution before posting. Keep bag, count, bank slip and cash-in-transit balance linked. A net deposit after an unauthorized petty cash deduction triggers review. A service leader can see approved totals while donor identities remain with donor services. Issue printable count sheets and support recording a witnessed offline batch once connectivity returns.

For a general cash gift of 75 and a restricted gift of 25, debit cash in transit for each fund's share and credit each fund's contribution income by the same amount. On deposit debit bank and credit transit 100, preserving the fund split when balanced by fund. The bank credit matched to this deposit is settlement evidence. On a returned gift, reverse or adjust its own allocation and eligibility, reinstate any satisfied pledge as appropriate and correct any issued statement. Post bank charges separately.

A recurring pledge of 600 payable as twelve installments of 50 can track commitment progress without posting future income. If policy recognizes an unconditional promise, debit pledges receivable and credit contribution income at its recognized measurement, applying any required discount or allowance. The collection then credits receivables. Support allowance, write-off approval and later recovery. A promise conditional on matching gifts remains pending until the qualifying evidence exists. A matching employer's separate payment has its own party, gift and acknowledgement.

## Acceptance

- A sealed collection counts 200. Identified envelopes total 150 and loose cash 50. A 195 slip creates a 5 discrepancy with an owner, and the system cannot mark the batch fully reconciled merely because a deposit exists.
- An identified donor requests anonymity in public. The congregation sees an anonymous gift, authorized finance staff retain the donor, and the donor receives the correct personal statement. A truly anonymous 50 creates no statement.
- A parent and an adult child share an address and give separately. Generating either statement excludes the other's entries. A permitted joint statement includes only allocations covered by explicit joint giving.
- A 60 purchase containing a documented benefit of 20 is split under the configured receipt rule. The receipt displays only the approved eligible amount, and the ledger treats the exchange portion according to its sale policy.
- Retrying a recurring charge or online callback produces one gift and one posting. A failed attempt remains visible in collection follow-up without increasing available funds.
- Finance accepts a donated instrument with an approved 500 carrying value. An asset link and donation journal agree; donor correspondence uses the local wording and valuation disclosure policy.
