# Prices, tax and delivery quotes

A quote is a reproducible offer to supply named items at a stated price, destination and instant. Changes that increase the payable total, change delivery promises or materially narrow access require renewed acceptance. A finance administrator approves the calculation policy by market. A church's status, a free-form billing address or a purchaser's membership alone cannot establish tax treatment.

## Arithmetic and policy

Determine channel, customer eligibility, fulfilment location, supply type, delivery destination and transaction date before tax selection. Resolve base tier, then eligible promotions in configured order. Apply a percentage to its named base only. Allocate document discounts across eligible lines in proportion to their eligible amounts. Round at the configured level, assign residual minor units by largest fractional remainder and a stable line identifier for ties. Cumulative discounts stop at zero. Gifts and excluded items cannot absorb discounts.

For a simple exclusive component, net 100 and rate 0.10 give tax 10 and gross 110. For an inclusive gross of 110 at the same rate the net is 100 and tax is 10. For stacked or compound taxes, use the approved calculation order and configured base for each component. Document totals equal the sum of their component amounts after rounding. The total due equals sale and delivery net plus charged tax plus separately designated gifts. A fixed donation suggested at checkout requires affirmative selection. Currency conversion, credit balances and tender belong to settlement or payment, outside this calculation.

## Operational checks

- Two eligible lines cost 10.01 and 10.00 in a currency with two minor decimals. A 1.00 order discount divides to 0.50 each under the stated proportional rule. Three equal lines sharing 0.01 assign that cent once to the first stable tie-break identifier.
- A purchaser changes from campus collection to delivery in another tax region. The system releases the obsolete quote, computes the new charge and delivery estimate, and requires the revised acceptance before capture.
- If address evidence conflicts at checkout or a jurisdiction lacks an approved rule, hold the quote for review or limit the available market. Display the issue to the purchaser without revealing internal region rules or exemption evidence.
- A promotional message fails but its coupon remains valid. A cancellation before payment releases the coupon exactly once. Pausing the promotion prevents new quotes and follows the agreed treatment of already accepted orders.
