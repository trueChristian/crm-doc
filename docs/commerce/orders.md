# Checkout, orders and collection

One checkout can contain merchandise, an access pass and a voluntary gift. A guest can buy a guide without creating a membership record. A private digital purchase must bind to an eligible recipient through [access and playback](access.md). Payment, fulfilment and posting progress independently. Staff must be able to see which step needs attention, who owns it and what the purchaser has been told.

## State and recovery

1. An active cart validates stock hold, current quote, delivery promise, access eligibility, accepted terms and applicable gift policy. Create an order and a collection request once per checkout. Retrying a frozen checkout retrieves its recorded state.
2. A redirect or receipt shown by a browser only brings the customer back to their order. A verified capture, accepted cash tender, confirmed bank receipt or approved credit terms determines eligibility for fulfilment. Display pending when the outcome is unknown. Inspect the existing attempt before initiating another collection.
3. Authorizations can expire or be voided. Capture an allowed portion once, reduce the remaining authorized amount and reauthorize separately if required. An order under credit terms can ship with an approved finance receivable and limit. Never mix that receivable with money received.
4. For mixed orders, assign the merchandise and gift before issuing confirmation. Send only confirmed gift allocation, designation, verified donor preferences and reversal references to giving. Receipt eligibility, conditional gifts and anonymous treatment are reviewed there. Digital entitlement or shipment follows each eligible line. A downstream outage queues the affected handoff and leaves other confirmed facts intact.
5. Fulfil partially, backorder with a promise, offer an approved substitution, or cancel the unfilled balance. Never silently increase the order total. Complete when all units are fulfilled or dispositioned, money has a recorded disposition, and outstanding exceptions have an owner.

## Acceptance examples

- A family orders a 50 guide and adds a 20 gift. The payment is captured once for 70. One order confirmation shows both purposes, and the giving register receives only 20. The person buying can decline donor identification. Another household adult sees neither order nor contribution by default.
- A payment succeeds during a connection loss. Reopening checkout and receiving two success messages produces one capture, one stock issue, one entitlement where eligible and one request for a donor acknowledgement.
- A payment of 30 in store credit and 40 by card settles 70. If the card declines, the partial balance remains explicit. Release or reinstate credit by policy if the buyer abandons the transaction. No line receives more than its amount due.
- A digital-only cart uses contact and recipient data required for access. Shipment address is absent. A work queue offers staff a masked lookup, and a guest must prove control of the receipt channel before seeing an order.
