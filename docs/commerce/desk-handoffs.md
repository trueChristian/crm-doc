# Resource desks and external payment handoffs

The registration desk answers whether a person can attend. A lending desk knows whether a physical copy is on loan. The giving steward records the donor’s intent and designation. A payment or accounting service can attest to the money received for each purpose. The church chooses which services to connect and designates one authority for each external outcome.

| Activity | Record that the church controls | Information exchanged when needed | Church-side evidence of completion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Event fee, family concession or sponsored place | Registration place, terms revision, hold expiry, authorized waiver and admission credential | Scoped place or group request reference, agreed fee by unit, currency, receiving provider and payment request identity. Return the verified status with its item allocation. | Confirm once while capacity and eligibility permit. Keep the waiver authority private and bind any refund request to the affected place. |
| Free publication or relief kit | Ministry project, available units in its custody, pickup or delivery observation | A supplying warehouse can give a dispatch, quantity and unit. A consenting recipient may give a collection code. | Record counted handover and any variance. Anonymous distribution can record quantities under policy without inventing people. |
| Loan of church property | Tagged equipment item or library accession, borrower or responsible team, due date, return and observed condition | Optional external evidence of an approved damage assessment, recovery or fee waiver | Release or quarantine the item on physical inspection. A paid charge alone leaves the loan or repair exception open. |
| Bookstall or partner sale | Church event, stall assignment, steward, external desk or provider reference, optional consented follow-up | Direct the visitor to the merchant. If pickup is the church’s responsibility accept a scoped pickup claim, allowed units, expiry and redemption status. | Attest only to units actually given out. A purchase creates no directory profile, gift or correspondence permission. |
| Room hire with an external deposit | Reservation, named responsible party, access issue, sign-back time, inspection and damage evidence | Scoped booking, deposit requirement, verified outcome and reference to any externally approved adjustment | Custodian records release of the room/keys and any follow-up work; the renter receives a separate deposit status from the responsible desk. |

## A family changes its retreat booking

An adult and two children request three places; capacity can hold all three for fifteen minutes under the event’s stated policy. The accepted fee statement allocates 50 to the adult, 20 to the first child and zero to a child with an approved concession, in one currency. An optional 10 gift has its own purpose and reference. Send the provider two chargeable place references and the separate gift reference. The event desk can see its own attributed amounts and waived place. Its view excludes the gift, the donor and the reason for the concession.

A verified provider response supplies 70 for the two charged places. Registration can confirm those two under the accepted policy; the third confirms against its waiver once eligible. A transport timeout after the request means the outcome is unknown. Look up the original request before opening another. A browser redirect, message from a booker, or notice for a different congregation cannot confirm a place. When a response comes after the hold expires, claim capacity again under the event’s queue rule. If the seats have gone, place the affected units in the resolution queue for alternative attendance or a referenced external refund. Do not take another person’s seat.

When the first child cancels, retire that child’s admission and release exactly one place. Track the 20 adjustment separately as requested, acknowledged or unresolved. A repeated callback cannot retire another child’s credential, cancel the household, or reduce giving. The family-facing view identifies the affected place and shows whether the financial owner has confirmed the adjustment.

## Follow a handoff from either desk

1. Capture organization, originating service, source operation and revision, action, currency and amount or unit and quantity, plus the permitted church reference. Keep the authorized actor, effective time and observation time for support. Exclude card data, raw bank information, names of children, private notes and the membership register.
2. Bind each external acknowledgment to its operation and authorized church. Reject an unexpected source or payload conflicting with the same action key. An unresolved identity, stale version or unexplained amount belongs to a named coordinator with a next review date. A timestamp alone cannot prove which operation supersedes another.
3. Record a physical observation, capacity change or printed issue at its own desk. A remote payment or delivery notice cannot substitute for a steward’s check-in, a returned book or a consent decision.
4. For a disconnected provider, retain the pending request and its correlation, show its current state and reconcile when service returns. Churches operating manually can record an authorized reviewer’s attestation from the external system with its source reference. API and app submissions use the same approvals. Send a single confirmed outcome to the requesting desk. Limit exports to the receiving role.
5. Match by scoped reference and unit before comparing totals. Two equal family fees across campuses remain two different obligations. Corrections identify the original action and reason and preserve its earlier effect in as-of reports.

The provider’s duties, including till sales, settlement, pricing and register closure, are documented in the supplied ERP references. A church can choose any compatible provider.
