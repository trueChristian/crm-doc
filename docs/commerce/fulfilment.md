# Counter sales and fulfilment

A book-table cashier can collect cash, a family can collect at a different campus, and one online order can produce multiple parcels or a combination of parcels and digital access. Each unit has exactly one current fulfilment disposition. Only the workers completing a delivery get the contact information necessary for that delivery.

## Counter shift

The operator opens with an approved float, selects the physical location, scans variants and confirms the basket. An authorized concession includes reason and approver. A cashier may complete an anonymous cash sale if permitted. Printed or electronic confirmation identifies the selling organization, date, document reference, currency, supplies, taxes, gift if any, tenders and way to request service. Electronic receipts require a destination for that transaction only. For cash of 100 against 73, record 100 in and 27 change or net 73 consistently. Mobile/offline sales must use bounded, preallocated stock, unique receipt identities and the supported tenders. An offline terminal may accept cash under policy; showing a card success requires independently confirmed collection.

The shift closes to new entries, the custodian counts cash, and a reviewer resolves any cash over/short. Under a gross tender/change convention, expected closing cash equals opening float plus tender and pay-ins less change, refunds, payouts and safe drops. Deposit bags and custody signatures link the safe drop to treasury. Configure thresholds for supervisor override, cash refunds and material variance. Preserve shift and transaction evidence after staff roles change.

## Fulfil, invoice and recover

Allocate stock, generate a packing list showing only assigned items, scan the actual lot and confirm a dispatch or handover. The order keeps the accepted delivery terms even if a carrier price changes. A clerk changing an undelivered address records authorization and rechecks destination restrictions. Lock that parcel's address at handover. Returned mail re-enters custody and triggers customer contact. A completed digital line points to its grant and delivery outcome. A failed email alone does not cancel a valid grant.

For sales on account, finance approves the buyer, credit limit, billing address, payment terms and due date. The finance invoice is authoritative for the open receivable, aging and write-off. A pro forma or packing slip is distinguishable from an issued invoice. Issued documents stay reproducible in the buyer's accepted language and currency, including any required address, identifiers, numbering and exemptions. A credit note references original supplies and tax components. Corrections follow the policy effective for that jurisdiction.

## Acceptance examples

1. Opening float 100, cash tender 150, change 20, safe drop 75 and recorded cash refund 5 yield expected close 150. Count 148 produces a reviewable shortage of 2, separate from sales and giving.
2. Two of three books dispatch in separate parcels. Each has one movement and tracking reference. The third remains outstanding. Replaying a carrier message never issues more stock or journals more revenue.
3. A pickup pass forwarded to another adult fails where policy requires named-collector proof. A documented substitute can collect after authorized change, and replay of the redeemed token fails.
4. One order includes one recording and one shipped guide. The buyer can use the active grant when the digital line completes. The financial report distinguishes the unfulfilled physical promise and issued invoice from cash received.
