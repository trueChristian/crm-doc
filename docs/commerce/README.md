# Church publications and resource desks

A worship team can publish a Sunday sermon, a volunteer can hand out relief parcels and a coordinator can reserve places at a retreat. Each outcome belongs to the church team that witnessed or authorized it. A congregation can run all three activities with an existing shop or payment provider, or run its free activities without a financial connection.

| Church task | Responsible desk | Where to start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approve a speaker’s public credit and a replay of the service | Worship producer, media librarian and rights reviewer | [Sermon library and publication](media.md) |
| Restrict a service to members or withdraw an identifying transcript from several channels | Publication owner and channel operator | [Audience and access](access.md) and [worked withdrawal](../handbook/commerce/publication-and-withdrawal.md) |
| Confirm a priced retreat place, accept a concession or cancel one family member’s place | Event coordinator | [Church desk handoffs](desk-handoffs.md) and [admission and attendance](../ministry/registration.md) |
| Give out a study guide, loan a book or refer a sale to a local bookstall | Resource steward or librarian | [Resource desk operations](desk-handoffs.md) and [front office and lending](../administration/office-services.md) |
| Hand over equipment or supplies, or record damage when a hirer returns keys | Facilities or outreach coordinator | [Acceptance rehearsals](../acceptance/commerce/README.md) |
| Verify state after an external callback, cancellation or failed withdrawal | Implementer or reviewer | [Decision cases](../decisions/commerce/README.md) and [opening-weekend exercise](../verification/opening-weekend/README.md) |

The [publication record catalogue](../implementation/commerce/README.md) locates the church-owned permissions, releases and media descriptions. Registration, pastoral follow-up, facilities, giving and outreach keep their own permissions and histories. A person can collect a parcel, borrow a book or listen to a public sermon without joining the membership register.
