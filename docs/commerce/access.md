# Audiences, discovery and withdrawal

The congregation chooses a release audience per service, edition, use and channel. An open sermon, an edited recording restricted to the congregation and a private archive can all originate from one service. A baptism testimony can permit live transmission to the service hall while withholding permission to publish the recording or name.

| Gate | Evidence evaluated at request time | Decision when evidence is missing or outdated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Release | Exact edition and rendition, active release revision and time interval | Withhold the item. A public service listing can still show independently approved service details. |
| Audience | Public clearance or current, scoped authority for a member, ministry group or invited account | Withhold restricted metadata and playback. Verify an invitation for its intended account and expiry. A shared household email cannot qualify its other users. |
| Use | Rights holder’s grant for live viewing, replay, preview, download, captions, translation or syndication | Permit only uses explicitly covered for that release. Recheck rights for each derivative and channel. |
| Current restriction | Withdrawal or hold covering the use, territory, revision or audience | Block new authorized links immediately at the gate. Recheck active sessions within the declared interval and track outstanding revocations. |
| Distribution | Channel supports the requested visibility, expiry and withdrawal controls | Keep sensitive material unpublished on a channel incapable of enforcing the chosen audience. Route to the publication owner for a different distribution choice. |

Apply the decision before returning titles, public contributor names, artwork, facets, transcripts, feed entries, CDN redirects or download URLs. A denial response must disclose only what its audience may see. A private service shared by link still needs its intended audience verified. A church app, embed, feed and staff-assisted preview apply the same rules. Reviewing a reported problem may require a short-lived support correlation, but individual viewing history needs its own approved purpose and retention.

Where another platform streams or archives the service, the CRM may hold only the approved local description, the scoped provider release identity and the audience policy. The owner selects who can publish, what the provider may receive, when to refresh access and where to report a failure. Accept a provider’s release confirmation only against the authorized church, exact requested revision and use. A general “stream ended” leaves the archive private pending its own release. Keep a failed withdrawal visible to the responsible operator and record local denial, remote acknowledgment, retry due time and independent confirmation of removal separately.

A public speaker credit can survive a church member’s request to leave the directory when the independently authorized credit still applies. Conversely a staff account with access to directory details cannot expose a private prayer request in program notes. If an edited public copy is cleared after withdrawal, publish the new revision with approved accessible alternatives. Verify the old transcript, artwork and search snippets independently before closing the withdrawal.
