# Subscriptions, grants and playback

Purchasing supplies a possible source of access. A current grant and the active publication and rights rules determine which edition, person and use are eligible. A church may choose free public teaching, a members' archive, scholarships, individual sales, expiring rentals or subscriptions to a series. Configure these independently at congregation, item and channel level. Eligibility for a free item never requires a zero-price card authorization.

## Decide access at request time

1. Resolve the requested edition, release and use without exposing private metadata. Check organization, active window, audience, rights and publication state first. A purchase cannot bypass a rights withdrawal, incident hold or restricted audience.
2. For public free material, allow the configured public uses. For restricted material, verify the viewer or named seat, then identify an eligible active grant or an expressly authorized group/membership rule. Evaluate all valid sources before denying access. Distinguish access to a listing from entitlement to full content.
3. For first-use rental, reserve the activation once. Activate on successful delivery of the eligible work as defined in the terms. Browsing a listing, failed playback, search previews and an unredeemed gift cannot consume rental time. Record the promised start and exact end at activation. Retrying activation must leave them unchanged.
4. Authorize only the named rendition and use. Generated playback or download links expire, cannot be reused outside their authorized scope, and must not reveal the archival master. Respect separately permitted offline delivery. Access revocation governs future controlled delivery only.
5. Deduplicate simultaneous starts by the same playback operation and enforce the paid seat/session limit across devices. Store individualized progress, private bookmarks and transcript navigation only if the user enables the feature. Provide deletion of personal progress while retaining permissible minimum transaction evidence.

## Renewal, support and reversals

A renewal uses accepted terms, current authorization and one renewal order for the service term. Send advance notifications when configured or required by the applicable rule pack. Price or scope changes requiring new acceptance pause automatic collection until confirmed. If a collection fails, follow the documented retry and grace schedule; stop access at the agreed end unless another independent grant applies. Cancellation distinguishes immediate termination from stopping the next renewal. Prorations, credits and partial refunds quote the effective dates and amounts for approval before applying.

A financial dispute can suspend access according to the terms. On a won dispute, reconsider eligibility and reinstate only if current rights and duration allow it. Full or partial refunds follow the original line allocation. Check whether another paid pass, subscription or free release independently authorizes use. Confidential scholarship approvals and amounts stay out of public directories and normal media engagement reports. Support staff can see reason categories and expired terms without inspecting pastoral information.

## Acceptance examples

- A visitor purchases a 48-hour rental activated on first playback. Two devices start the same authorized playback at 09:00; only one activation is recorded, ending at 09:00 two days later by elapsed-hour policy. The second device is admitted or refused by the plan limit. A failed prior delivery did not start the clock.
- A monthly subscriber cancels renewal in the middle of a paid month. Access remains through the named end, no next order is charged and retrying cancellation returns the original decision. If a refund is separately approved it has its own posting and grant action.
- A member receives a gift for an existing paid series and a mistaken purchase is refunded. Revoking that purchase leaves the separate valid gift available. A rights withdrawal still takes precedence over both.
- A payer purchases three named seats. A fourth simultaneous assignment is refused. Removing a seat ends future access for that seat only, preserves the order and records who changed it. A buyer cannot query the recipients' private playback sessions.
- A low-bandwidth user switches to audio-only mid-sermon. Both renditions use the same authorized edition and time window. No extra charge or entitlement activation occurs.
