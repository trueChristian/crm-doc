# Inventory and custody

A campus steward can distinguish stock physically present from stock available to sell. Finance owns cost recognition and procurement commitments. A receipt records what arrived, including short, excess, damaged and substituted goods. The receiving clerk sends those discrepancies to purchasing and links accepted quantities to the purchasing document.

## Balances and custody

For a variant and location, on-hand equals posted inflows less posted outflows at the as-of instant. Available to promise equals eligible on-hand less open reservations and configured safety stock. Display incoming purchases and backorders separately. A withdrawal, hold or pick checks the latest balance atomically. Multiple storefronts drawing from the same bin share that balance. A barcode scan alone makes no accounting or custody change until the authorized transaction confirms. Expiry recovery closes a hold only while it still belongs to the cart. If payment completes after expiry, allocate again or route the order to the agreed shortage remedy.

At dispatch, link movement to the shipment line and cost basis. Financial valuation follows the approved cost method consistently per inventory class, with traceable adjustments and reconciliation to the inventory control account. Stock held on consignment is physically tracked and valued under its specific ownership agreement. Loanable ministry equipment remains in the resource register unless explicitly reclassified. An item delivered back for a refund first enters inspection. Record restock, repair, quarantine or write-off with evidence and actor. A gift of donated goods that enters trading inventory carries a link to its separately approved valuation.

## Acceptance and controls

- One bin has 12 units, three reserved and two safety units. Seven can be newly promised. Two concurrent requests for five cannot both succeed. Failed requests leave zero partial holds unless partial fulfilment was explicitly accepted.
- Ten transit out of Campus A. Campus B receives eight. Both local balances and the two in transit are traceable until a further receipt or approved loss closes the transfer.
- A counter enters zero for a variant while leaving another uncounted. Only the counted variance may be approved. A delivery arriving after the cutoff is excluded from the count comparison and appears in today's current balance.
- A receipt of nine against ten ordered contains two damaged. Seven become sellable, two enter quarantine, one remains undelivered. Supplier matching sees the discrepancy and finance receives the accepted receipt facts only as configured.
- A lot recall lists affected shipments, open orders and quantities in each branch. Counter staff can see the hold and replacement process, while purchaser contact details remain with the authorized follow-up worker.
