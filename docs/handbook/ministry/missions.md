# Reconcile a mission trip and community distribution

A mission project brings together a partner agreement, funding, transport, entrusted goods and community outcomes. Link their identifiers while preserving separate authority. A donor can receive an approved outcome figure but does not thereby gain a passenger manifest or list of beneficiaries. A transport lead receives instructions for the people assigned to their actual journey.

## Plan one trip by leg and occupied segment

Choose the vehicle and effective seat layout. Count passenger seats separately from driver, and record configured accessibility spaces and their impact on ordinary seating. Give each stop an order and estimated instant, translated into the relevant local zone for the traveler. Each passenger claims an ordered pair of stops, boarding then leaving. Require both stops to belong to the same journey and disallow a zero-length or reversed journey. Evaluate vehicle availability, driver eligibility for the whole journey including return where relevant and local approval requirements.

At every stop first release seats for those leaving, then consume seats for those boarding. Count the interval until the next stop. Include the appropriate companion's seat and accessible space where the layout requires it. A passenger with no confirmed seat remains waitlisted; an oversubscribed request cannot be treated as approved just because another seat opens later in the day. A trip may carry more distinct people over its whole route than it has simultaneous passenger seats.

**Manifest fixture.** Use a vehicle with five ordinary passenger seats, plus its driver. Stops are A, B, C, D in that order. Two passengers ride A to C, two ride A to B, three ride B to D, and one rides C to D.

| Segment | Passengers aboard after stop changes | Ordinary seats free |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| A to B | 4 | 1 |
| B to C | 5 | 0 |
| C to D | 4 | 1 |

Eight distinct passengers can ride. One more from A to D fails on B to C. One additional passenger from C to D can fit. If a reconfigured vehicle has only three ordinary seats alongside an accessible bay, recalculate every segment by seat type and accompanying person before promising a replacement. A site coordinator cannot drop the accessible passenger from the manifest simply to make an alternate layout fit.

Before departure, reconcile confirmed riders and persons actually boarded. Assign an owner to a missing passenger or changed collection arrangement. A child or supported adult's handover follows their action-specific representation authority. A driver sees only their stop list and practical instructions during the duty interval. An unreachable passenger who never boarded may still attend the church event by other transport. Do not generate an absence from that transport observation.

## Plan a distribution and reconcile quantities

Define a distribution session, site, supplies, units, named project steward, eligibility decisions, maximum entitlement and privacy mode. The warehouse owns movements and finance controls the value and any restrictions attached to the goods. Confirm transfers at both ends. Keep central stock in transit until acknowledged locally; issuing a community parcel is a different movement from moving it from a warehouse.

A unit conversion such as 1 crate = 12 sealed parcels requires approval for this item; weight cannot be converted to parcels without an item-specific rule. Record opening, receipts, actual issues, returns to central, spoilage and physical closing with their times. Hold unconfirmed promises separate from actual units distributed. A correction links the original handover; never silently change what the storekeeper remembers issuing.

**Stock fixture.** A site opens with 12 parcels, receives five crates of 12 parcels, issues 41 parcels, returns six to central stores and records three damaged parcels approved for write-off. Expected closing usable on-site is 12 + 60 − 41 − 6 − 3 = 22. A physical count of 21 creates an unresolved shortage of one. Central stores must own the receipt of the six returned parcels; that six is not part of on-site usable stock. If two remaining households each have an unfulfilled promise of two parcels, report 21 counted on-site and four promised. Seventeen counted usable units remain uncommitted pending review of the shortage.

## Respect the assistance decision

A product handover may use a protected award token, identified case, household or permitted anonymous route. Verify the purpose, valid issue window, remaining entitlement and actual goods at confirmation. A repeat of the same issue identity returns its prior receipt without reducing stock or entitlement a second time. A suspicious overlap in names is a review signal; do not deny assistance automatically or expose details about another household. When stock is short, maintain original queue time and the documented allocation priority. A family can decline unrelated communications or church membership without affecting an otherwise approved issue.

Report assisted visits, named unique households where authorized, anonymous issues, total parcels and estimated reach separately. A distribution issuing 41 parcels to 18 identified households plus four anonymous contacts cannot claim exactly 22 unique households without further authorized evidence. Publish restricted-gift outcomes at the permitted aggregation and allow the project officer to explain uncertain reach.

## Close the project

Reconcile planned funding, confirmed receipts, financial expenditure, physical deliveries and outcome definitions. A gift restricted to one trip, commercial participant fees and mission support are distinct. Cancellation sends the appropriate review to finance for each source, preserves the donor's instructions, checks outstanding supplier commitments and offers revised travel or refunds to participants under their accepted terms. Return keys and equipment, close custody or incident reviews, account for all travellers and review pending partner handoffs. A project may finish delivering services while a grant acquittal or stock shortage investigation remains outstanding, with named owner and due date.
