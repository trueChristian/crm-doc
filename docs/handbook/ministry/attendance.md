# Produce attendance figures a secretary can explain

Before a report runs, declare its population, time period, included occurrences, location, eligible participation states, counting unit, identity-resolution cut-off, time zone and observation cut-off. A registration register, a named roll, a click count and an usher's headcount answer different questions. Give each a separate column and a definition. A secretary should be able to explain every change between an earlier certified report and its corrected edition.

## Normalize observations for one occurrence

1. Record arrival as an observation with occurrence, person or approved guest reference, observer, actual observation time, entry time and source. Preserve scans, attendance roll, authorized offline entry and approved correction as evidence. Reject a duplicate operation without erasing the evidence of two distinct scanners.
2. Reduce repeated observations for a person and occurrence into one counted presence. Admission, transfer between rooms, re-entry from a break and check-out do not create second visits. Keep attendance state separate from “still in the building.” Explicitly marked absent, excused and unmarked are distinct. Do not mark a missing response absent without the roll owner's decision.
3. Apply a documented hierarchy for mutually inconsistent observations, such as pending review of a signed correction versus a contradictory late scan. Record which was accepted, by whom and why. An offline teacher who actually saw a learner may have stronger evidence than an unattended early registration.
4. Determine which count segments are known to be disjoint. A headcount of everybody in the hall already includes the people whose names were scanned there. A separate count in a child room can be added if both scopes and times are comparable and no transferred child was counted twice.
5. Record count quality as observed, estimated, partial or unknown and its scope. A stream device count without household confirmation remains device activity. A blended service may separately report individually confirmed online participants and physical attenders. Uniqueness across modes requires authorized identity evidence.

## Worked Sunday service pair

All fixture observations are final, comparable physical headcounts covering each entire service and include named attendees. Assume each attendee is counted once at each service.

| Occurrence | Named confirmed | Total observed | Derived unnamed visits |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| S1 morning | A, B, C | 5 | 2 |
| S2 evening | A, C, D | 4 | 1 |

Named person-visits equal six across both services. Distinct identified people equal four, because A and C attended both. Total person-visits equal nine. Three visits have no linked individual. From this evidence the number of distinct people including unnamed visitors is unknown. If a planning estimate explicitly needs bounds, at least five different people attended (S1 alone has five) and at most seven (four identified plus three unnamed visits). Announce neither end as the actual unique-person figure. If a count were an estimate or partial hall sample, even those bounds would require reconsideration.

On Monday one previously unnamed S1 visitor is privately confirmed as E. Update one linked observation, leaving S1 total five. The revised named visits are seven, distinct identified five, unnamed visits two and total visits still nine. Registering E with the church is a separate decision. Suppressing small cells in a public report must also prevent hidden counts from being reconstructed by subtracting visible campus totals.

**Counterexample.** The evening desk records four named people at 19:45 but the usher counted three people before they entered. Subtracting four from three would yield minus one. Show the time and scope mismatch and mark it for reconciliation. Do not silently clamp to zero, invent an unnamed visitor or overwrite the usher's observation.

## Groups, membership and population denominators

For a ministry participation rate, pick eligible people as of a stated date or define a person-time method. Example: a term begins with ten eligible choir members, one withdraws after week two and two join for week four. Nine original members attend at least one of the first four weeks and one new member attends in week four. The “initial cohort attended” count is nine out of ten. The “ever eligible cohort attended” count is ten out of twelve. Both are valid answers to different questions. Record the inclusion rule for transfers and archived or deceased people. A later household merge must never silently rewrite the membership snapshot used for a certified earlier return.

When a person attends two groups under one ministry, sum their distinct group-session visits when measuring workload, and deduplicate people across groups when measuring reach. For a combined programme attendance report declare whether distinct sessions or persons per programme are counted. A volunteer serving in the adult hall and helping teach at a later workshop may belong in both role breakdowns but is one distinct person in the period.

## Report revisions and inspection

Store report edition, approved event set, filters, methodology, data cut-off, identity resolution edition, reviewer and the counts or signed extract needed to reproduce it. An identity merge takes effect for authorized current recalculation. If absorbed and surviving identities appeared in the same occurrence, review the duplicate appearance, consolidate counted named presence and retain links to the original observations. If an earlier certified edition requires correction, publish a superseding edition with an explanatory difference. The historic edition remains retrievable under its original authorization.

A late observation with an actual Sunday date, entered on Tuesday, belongs to Sunday's current operational view. A copy certified Monday excludes it. A comparison between the two explains “one late named observation” independently of calendar changes or attendance definitions. Role changes after certification affect who can inspect the export today, even when the underlying frozen population does not change.

**Sign-off checks.** Every named visit maps to an occurrence and an accepted observation. Totals equal their mutually exclusive components. Derived anonymous visits carry count scope and quality. Counted roles and campus totals state whether they overlap. Restricted small groups are suppressed or combined. Ceremonies, funerals or streamed services with only anonymous counts can appear in total activity without inventing individual attendance or converting visitors into church members.
