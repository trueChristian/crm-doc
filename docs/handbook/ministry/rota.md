# Construct a rota fairly and keep it staffed

The coordinator first establishes whether a person can do the work, then chooses how to distribute invitations among eligible people. Publish the church's criteria to staff in plain language, including its treatment of declining, being on standby and taking a break. Volunteers can set availability or leave a role without disclosing private reasons to the rota team.

## Determine when a person can serve

For each DutyShift, use its actual preparation and handback interval, campus, required position, required number, qualifications, supervision pattern, travel requirements and whether it is a required or standby place. Compute effective availability in the volunteer's own named zone, including overnight windows and dated exceptions. A recurring Saturday night preference cannot implicitly authorize the whole of Sunday. Use the actual interval for the date in question.

Evaluate the person, active position appointment, role-specific skill and every mandatory credential across the entire duty interval. Apply valid-from inclusive and valid-until exclusive boundaries in the credential's declared locality after translating the source policy's meaning. A clearance valid through 30 June local day is usable until 1 July at local midnight. A shift beginning 30 June at 23:00 and ending 1 July at 01:00 needs renewed coverage or another eligible worker. Return eligible, ineligible or needs verification. Unknown evidence cannot satisfy a mandatory requirement.

Compare confirmed and held commitments. Two duties that end and begin at 10:00 can still conflict if a 20-minute journey lies between campuses. Check leave, availability, requested rest, team pairing rules and limits. A driver or nursery supervisor's candidate status may reveal only “clear for this role” or “needs reviewer.” Reference, identity or protection details belong to their authorized verifier. Exclude giving, household composition and pastoral case details from skill and fairness calculations.

## Example reproducible allocation

This church's example policy ranks eligible candidates by least used share of their personally declared service target over four completed weeks, then longest since last served, then a stable neutral ordering. The used share is fulfilled minutes divided by voluntary target minutes over the same period. An unreplied or declined invitation never adds fulfilled minutes. New people with a positive target and no prior service start at zero. A person declaring a zero target can be contacted only if they independently choose to make a particular shift available. A coordinator may change a soft ranking to support mentoring, recording the authorized rationale.

The current shift requires one qualified welcome steward from 09:00 to 10:15. Travel from the west campus takes 20 minutes.

| Candidate | Recent fulfilled / target minutes | Other evidence | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ava | 120 / 240 = 0.50 | Last served 12 days ago. Eligible and available. | Second under the tie breaker. |
| Bo | 60 / 120 = 0.50 | Last served 19 days ago. Eligible and available. | First invitation. |
| Cato | 0 / 180 = 0 | Credential ends at 10:00 before duty ends. | Requires renewal or a different duty. |
| Dani | 30 / 180 ≈ 0.17 | Confirmed west-campus duty ends at 08:55. Earliest arrival is 09:15. | Travel conflict. |
| Eden | 60 / 180 ≈ 0.33 | Declared unavailable that date. | Exclude privately. |

If Bo declines, send the scope-limited invitation to Ava and retain the decline without reducing Bo's future eligibility. If both decline, report one open place and the escalation deadline. A list of unconfirmed suggestions cannot satisfy promised coverage. The church can choose a different fairness policy but should be able to recreate an offer from the stored edition, candidate decisions, observation time and tie break.

## Build a whole rota

Handle shifts with few eligible workers first. Within that set prioritize earliest confirmation deadline, then start and stable shift identity. Reserve the chosen person's interval in the draft before evaluating the next shift. If a rare-role candidate also qualifies for a common role, a planning review can swap assignments to fill both roles. Example: Imani alone can interpret, while both Imani and Jo can usher. Fill the interpretation position first, assign Jo to welcome, and publish two offers. A greedy selection by name for the usher shift alone could fill only one role.

Show required, offered, accepted, present, standby and still missing as distinct counts. Before publication rerun constraints against current commitments and eligibility. Keep the threshold for “ready to publish” separate from the threshold for physical opening; confirmation weeks before Sunday does not meet a present-staff ratio. Record the exact proposal accepted by each volunteer, including time, campus, role, instructions and changed expectations. A material timing or location change invites reconfirmation rather than carrying an old acceptance forward without review.

## Substitute and restore coverage

On withdrawal, retain the original assignment and its end reason. If a replacement depends on approval, the original volunteer remains assigned only where they explicitly remain able and authorized to serve. Otherwise mark a real vacancy. Offer a reserve an expiring place, check qualifications, travel and hours again on acceptance, retire superseded pending offers and update affected duty leads. Two reserve acceptances for the last place produce one confirmed required assignment. The other receives an explanation and may consent to a standby place.

If a supervisor's qualification is withdrawn after publication, mark future required coverage unfilled where the withdrawal applies and open an action. A printed sheet shows issue time and a contact who can verify a change. At the door the duty lead records actual arrival and departure. A 09:00–12:00 attendance with a 15-minute break gives 165 fulfilled minutes; a person scheduled but absent has zero. Approved partial duty can count in workload without being represented as full coverage.

**Reconciliation.** For each period compare rostered minutes, offered intervals, accepted minutes, actual service and approved corrections. Show repeated urgent calls, concentration of duty among few people, unfilled specialist roles and long sequences without a break. Give the coordinator an explanation and a way to correct poor data. A volunteer's illness, disciplinary review or protected circumstance should never appear in a public fairness chart.
