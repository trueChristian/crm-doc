# Move a person through groups and formation

A learner can be a visitor, returning member or church worker. A group role, course enrolment, child supervision permission, denomination-specific rite and authorization to update somebody's profile have distinct owners. The example follows one learner through a change of class without losing prior evidence or granting rights to relatives.

## Build and close a term roster

1. The coordinator defines group, term dates, number of places, age assessment date, review and entry requirements, waiting rules and which leaders may see it. A term can begin mid-calendar year. A confidential cohort hides its existence from ordinary group search and public aggregates.
2. Determine age in whole years by comparing the birthday in the assessment year with the assessment date. Subtract one when the birthday has not occurred. For a 29 February birthday in a year without that day, store the church's chosen effective date for this administrative age band; use the rule of the relevant authority where a regulated decision requires one. A year-only or approximate birth date produces a range, never a fabricated first of January.
3. If all plausible ages satisfy the configured band, the age condition alone may clear where policy permits this precision. If plausible ages straddle a threshold, route for review. If none qualify, show the authorized alternative. A parent, volunteer or general attendee cannot read private birth details from a rejected request.
4. A transfer begins as a request against the destination offering and its capacity. While awaiting approval, retain the original class place if the policy promises continuity. On acceptance, end the source participation at its effective boundary and open the destination spell as one approved change. Prior attendance and teacher decisions retain their original occurrence and class.
5. When closing a term, list unresolved attendance, incomplete evaluations, proposed renewals and superseded roles. Rollover creates invitations or proposals, not unexamined access to the next term. Recalculate current age, eligibility, consent, accommodation requests and leadership access before final acceptance.

**Age fixture.** A class admits people aged 12 through 14 inclusive on 1 September 2027. A child born 1 September 2015 turns 12 that day and meets the age condition; a birth date of 2 September 2015 yields 11. A year-only 2014 gives age 12 or 13 and satisfies the age condition if this church permits a guaranteed range. A year-only 2015 gives 11 or 12 and needs review. A missing day is not a reason to expose the child's file to the whole planning team.

## Determine what counts as completion

Pin the curriculum version, required units, evidence types, attempt policy, equivalence decisions, required instructor approvals and any limits on prior learning when opening the offering. Retain relationships between units differently from route dependencies in a pathway. Inside a path, define alternative options within a requirement group, then require every mandatory group to have a satisfied option. Reject a prerequisite cycle and show the loop so the planner can correct it.

A teacher records facts about an actual attempt, date, outcome and source. An “attended” observation can satisfy an attendance-based unit; an assessed demonstration can require separate evidence. A nurse, choir leader or volunteer considering a role sees the approved completion status only when relevant, not private instructor comments. Publishing a subsequent curriculum version does not change the basis of previous certificates.

**Equivalence fixture.** Offering A has required units W (welcome), S (service), and C (community visit). Learner K completed W, attended S without the project evidence required for completion, and missed C before moving campus. Offering B uses new units W2, S2 and M. An approved equivalence maps A.W to B.W2 in full, requires a revised practical exercise for S2 and has no match for M. After transfer B must show one earned unit, two open units and the source evidence. It cannot turn the original S attendance into a full practical award or add a C pass.

K later demonstrates S2 and obtains a reviewer-approved exemption for M under this offering's policy. The report displays two earned units and one waived requirement, with dates and issuers. If certificates permit approved exemptions, one award can be issued from that completion. If this offering requires all three to be directly demonstrated, it must instead remain pending until M is completed. The governing rule edition is visible to the reviewer.

## Review mentoring and formal milestones

A mentor can record a learner-agreed meeting and the completion evidence needed for an agreed pathway. General programme staff should see the mentoring stage complete, pending or deferred. Private conversations, family circumstances and protection allegations have restricted owners and must not appear in a sign-up view or progress spreadsheet. A learner can withdraw from mentoring without causing a public group status change.

Suppose a preparation pathway requires a completed course plus either an approved mentor meeting or reviewed prior learning, followed by an authorized pastoral review. When K's course award and mentor confirmation are accepted, open one pastoral review action. A repeated award notification or nightly recalculation returns that same open action. Approval permits the designated officer to enter the applicable milestone in the register. A course instructor cannot create the rite or change church membership merely by marking an assignment complete. If the source award is withdrawn after review, alert the milestone record keeper with the corrected evidence; do not quietly erase a recorded church event.

**Reconciliation.** For each reporting date count distinct applicants, accepted people, active spells, paused learners, withdrawn learners and awards by edition. Count repeat attempts and course transfers independently from the unique learners measure. Compare physical session attendance to evidence of completion, invalid historical award serials to approved replacements, and expired instructor roles to actual access. A former teacher can retain attribution for last term without continuing access to children's current details.
