# Determine who has custody of a child

Before acting, the operator must be able to find the child, their current responsible team, any pending movement and the permitted next action. Access to a room roster expires with the assignment. Reception staff require an available, private route to the duty lead when the record is uncertain. The [register](../../people/households-and-authority.md) owns authority and restrictions. The [supervision register](../../ministry/child-check-in.md) owns custody and movements.

## Accept a child into supervision

Find the child by a private lookup. Confirm identity with the arrival adult and resolve ambiguous matches without displaying unrelated children. Determine the relevant occurrence, room, age rule, care instructions needed for this session, consent for attendance and applicable authority of the arriving adult. An emergency contact, a parent name on a family record, a paid place and a valid admission ticket answer different questions. Staff must resolve the action actually being taken.

For the same decision instant check that no active custody chain already exists for this child at an overlapping session, qualified staff are physically present, the room can accept one more child, practical care needs have been addressed and an eligible receiving worker is ready. Record receiver and arrival adult. Create custody, open the first stay, consume the room place and append a named attendance observation together. Printing the label may fail independently; reprint against the existing custody record with a numbered replacement. A failed printer must not create another child or attendance record.

On entry the assigned room team gets a display name, matching identifier and the approved practical care instruction. The arrival adult gets the receipt for the corresponding child and how to contact the lead. A sibling sharing the session has individual custody and release decisions. Two children attending a class together do not require two separate parent accounts.

## Acknowledge a transfer

A transfer request names the sending and receiving rooms, the child, the intended receiver and the request instant. Verify the child is still in the sending room and reserves one provisional place in the receiving room. If the receiver cannot accept because capacity, supervision or the care instruction has changed, abandon the request, release the provisional place and retain the sender's accountability. A request alone never transfers custody.

The receiving worker acknowledges physical handover. In one decision, end the sending stay and active seat, convert the provisional receiving place to an occupied place, open the receiving stay and record both workers. An acknowledgement replay returns the original confirmation. If the child physically moved during an outage, use the approved documented manual handover with named people at both ends. On restoration reconcile effective time, recording time, interim responsibility and competing reports. Do not backfill an invented receiver from the scheduled rota. The live view must label unconfirmed discrepancies and identify who is investigating.

## Decide a collection request

1. Identify the child and session. Confirm one active custody record, its room and any pending movement. Staff at a remote desk can request collection but cannot acknowledge physical release from another room by assumption.
2. Identify the claimant through authorized private means, inspect the effective child-specific collection authority at release time, and evaluate scope, date, named adult, decision effect and superseding restrictions. A global or scoped prohibition that applies to this action marks the request denied or referred according to policy. Ambiguous or conflicting records require review by the designated lead. Record which authority revision and decision time supported the response.
3. Check the matching child-specific credential. A photographed or shared code is only one input. A lost label triggers the church-approved alternate identity and authorization process; record verifier, second reviewer if needed and reason. Neither a familiar face nor possession of a matching code authorizes a restricted adult.
4. Re-read the custody revision at final handover. In one decision create the successful collection attempt, close exactly one stay and custody chain and retire its credentials. A failed or refused attempt records the minimum necessary details and leaves the child supervised. Send instructions only through the approved, safe contact route.
5. Where desk connectivity cannot prove current permissions, use an approved alternate channel to the designated verifier. If current authority remains uncertain, continue supervision. Any incident report stays with the protection or incident owner. The ordinary roster shows only the allowed operational action.

**Boundary fixture.** Adult K has permission to collect until 10:00 exclusive. A different authorized representative, L, lives elsewhere. K's request at 09:58 results in a pending decision, then a restriction takes effect at 09:59. A physical handover attempted at 10:01 requires a fresh evaluation and cannot be authorized by the 09:58 lookup. Record K's failed attempt privately. L presenting verified current authority at 10:05 can proceed under policy. The exercise must pass through both a live desk and a previously printed roster.

## Close, reconcile and disclose

Compute opening supervised plus admitted plus inward transfers less outward transfers and successful releases for each room. Compare to actual physical count, active stays, pending transfers and responsible personnel at that time. Record another team's visit or a transient assembly explicitly if it changes the count's population. Session closure requires each discrepancy and uncollected child to have an accountable owner; a session cannot be certified as fully reconciled while an active custody chain remains. Use a separate “closed for admissions” status if doors close while children remain.

After operational access expires, preserve the dated custody chain and decisions under the approved retention and evidence policies. An ordinary Sunday school teacher reviewing last year's attendance cannot retrieve disallowed claimant details or the contents of private instructions. Where a family asks what occurred, disclosure is decided by the responsible privacy and safeguarding owners and recorded.
