# Secretary, returning officer and communications desk

Use the procedural manual for the decision on your desk. Each fixture declares its own policy so that an implementation can reproduce the result and a different church can substitute its adopted rules. Names, identifiers, amounts and time windows in these examples are fictional. The [governance register](../../governance/README.md), [communications register](../../communications/README.md) and [administration register](../../administration/README.md) own the underlying records.

| Work to complete | Procedure | Evidence of completion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commission an isolated church from verified founding officers to its certified first annual return | [Church profile](commissioning-profile.md), [people and access](commissioning-access.md), [end-to-end rehearsal](commissioning-rehearsal.md) | One reconciled membership, governance, donor and financial evidence trail |
| Issue a call even when delivery or an agenda change threatens its validity | [Notice windows and changes](notice-control.md) | Issued wording, service results, deadline calculation and ruling |
| Seat delegates, substitute a proxy and determine item-level quorum | [Accreditation and quorum](roll-and-quorum.md) | Reconstructible entitlement and presence at any decision instant |
| Dispose of amendments and count a weighted decision | [Motions and vote arithmetic](motions-and-weights.md) | Exact wording, copies of counts, threshold and ruling |
| Certify an anonymous election under disagreement or a tie | [Secret elections and recounts](election-control.md) | Balanced paper account, independent tally and certification |
| Correct the record, review a dispute and track the consequence | [Minutes, extracts and review](minutes-and-review.md) | Signed edition, authorized change and downstream action |
| Decide who may receive a message and through which channel | [Routing and consent](contact-routing.md) | Specific eligibility result for every attempted destination |
| Reconcile provider timeouts, duplicate feedback and shared inbox replies | [Delivery incident desk](delivery-incident-desk.md) | One traceable submission intention per route and a documented outcome |
| Reschedule reminders and coordinate a multilingual emergency notice | [Urgent announcements and reminders](emergency-and-reminders.md) | Current, accessible instruction and closed or escalated exceptions |
| Compile, certify and correct year-end membership and financial papers | [Annual pack close](annual-pack.md) | Reproducible as-issued and restated returns |
| Lend a minute book, prepare an extract and recover a damaged digital master | [Archive custody](custody-and-release.md) | Traceable custody, controlled release and recovery evidence |

Run the examples with an authorized clerk account, a limited audience account and a simulated integration actor. Check the operational result, the view each actor may see, the historical snapshot before and after correction and repeat behavior. An issued notice, confirmed vote, circulated statement or transferred archive item has consequences outside the application; reversal records a new action against that history.
