# Year-end reporting and the annual meeting pack

The secretary assembles a packet from accountable records. People owns the membership history; finance owns the signed statements; ministry owns attendance; the secretary owns collection, reconciliation notes, presentation, attestation and distribution of the compiled return. The [return register](../../administration/annual-returns.md), [board packet](../../governance/annual-cycle-and-policy.md) and [exchange of approved accounting evidence](../../finance/accounting-exchange.md) keep their distinct authorities.

## Freeze the basis before requesting figures

Define the legal or pastoral perimeter, reporting period, zone, person or seat counting unit, membership status qualifying at a point in time, campus overlap handling, financial basis, currency, precision, exchange treatment, privacy suppression and the latest record-entry time for the as-issued return. A cutoff for membership effectiveness differs from the timestamp at which evidence arrived. A period with no authorized return is missing. A submitted return with no activity is a reported zero.

Assign each line an owner and a source version. A headcount is not unique people. Anonymous attendance cannot be deduplicated against named people without evidence. Document denominator, applicability, known exclusions and unit for each rate. Prefer supplied numerator and denominator when aggregating rates. Independent churches may return aggregate numbers without sharing names, in which case cross-church unique people can be unknowable. Store that uncertainty visibly.

## Walk a membership return

**Fixture A1.** Opening roll is 160 unique official members. Twelve first admissions and three incoming transfers add 15. Four departures, two outgoing transfers and one death remove 7. An otherwise routine correction reclassifies a visitor as a member effective next year and has no current-year effect. Expected closing roll is 168. The two members present at both campuses count once each across the organization. Print opening, each movement, expected closing, extracted closing and variance. If extracted closing is 167, retain variance −1 while investigating the source. Certification requires either resolution or the reporting policy's explicit exception. An applicant, household resident, regular visitor and signed-in user remain separately reportable populations.

Ten January records later show that an already counted outgoing transfer was effective on 2 January of the following year. A restatement of the prior year restores one member and makes expected closing 169. If the 168 report had been certified prior to discovering the evidence, its issued version stays at 168. Publish an approved revised version at 169 with the effective change, notify prior recipients and reconcile the next year's opening. If the original report had been 167 under an unresolved variance, restate its extracted and expected values separately rather than concealing the previous discrepancy.

**Fixture A2.** Campus East has 80 named participants and West has 55. Ten authorized matching persons appear in both, so the union is 125. There are also 18 unlinked anonymous visits. Publish 125 known unique people and 18 anonymous visits, or an applicable bounded measure with an explanation of unknown overlap. Two districts receiving only 80 and 55 totals cannot infer 125 without the authorized overlap figure.

## Resource and finance pack

Request an accountant-approved release for the agreed reporting perimeter and period. Record the issuing organization, period, basis, currency, approval, publication audience and source revision. A member pack can carry an authorized aggregate; supporting donor and payroll records stay restricted. Return a discrepancy to the accountant. A corrected release becomes a new attachment revision, and the clerk can reproduce the old issue.

If the accountant supplies budget and actual figures, retain the stated basis and comparison convention. An unavailable percentage must display as unavailable. If only nine of twelve ministry reports have arrived, label coverage 9/12 and distinguish missing submissions from suppressed lines.

## Issue, correct and reissue

1. Freeze return revision and source versions; run population, totals, privacy and period checks. Resolve discrepancy ownership before the deadline. A late committee submission is linked to the next packet revision, never substituted into a signed pack.
2. Bind attestations to the same figures, annex digests, audience scope and exception register. Material change requires fresh approval. Produce accessible numeric tables, searchable headings, plain-language definition notes and a controlled print copy.
3. Release copies by recipient class. The board pack and member edition may have different annexes. Keep commitment, dispatch, receipt and external acceptance as separate evidence; a sent attachment alone cannot establish filing approval.
4. Upon correction issue a new signed revision with effective account period, old reference, correction reason, authorized reviewers and distribution follow-up. A restored copy can reproduce both the earlier issue and the latest release.
5. At an annual meeting a delegate asks why published attendance differs from membership. The clerk can show both definitions and line sources without exposing underlying names, restricted payroll or individual voting eligibility.
