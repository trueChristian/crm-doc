# Urgent multilingual bulletins and rescheduled reminders

The coordinator works from a single authorized instruction with a revision, geographic scope, audience, issue time and expiry. The church determines which events qualify for emergency override and who may authorize it. A church notice does not establish that emergency services were contacted or that all people are safe. Use [community communications](../../communications/community-communication.md), [reminder records](../../communications/reminders-and-orchestration.md) and [continuity and reunification](../../assurance/continuity-and-reunification.md).

## From first report to all-clear

Verify the report and identify the actual incident lead. Draft an actionable concise instruction specifying who is affected, where to go, when it applies, when another update is expected and a trusted route for assistance. Distinguish public instructions from personally identifiable information or unverified claims. The approval binds its text, zone, approved channels, translations and audience. A vague “send to everyone” cannot turn a restricted case report into a public announcement.

Each language edition must preserve place names, critical times, accessibility instructions, urgency and the difference between a forecast and confirmed fact. An authorized reviewer compares each material change. If a translation is missing at deadline, use only the previously approved accessible fallback or send a deliberately minimal approved multilingual holding notice. Log the unserved language group and its follow-up. Screen readers and print should receive the full instruction in text. An image-only flyer, a color-coded status or an audio-only recording is insufficient as the sole route.

**Fixture U1.** At 08:00 the north campus hall closes until noon. Thirty intended personal recipients remain after a union of north-campus attendee and volunteer lists. French is preferred by 8, English by 22. Both editions are approved. Five people have phone-tree instructions only, 25 have an eligible digital route, and the incident lead approves an additional channel post. The dispatch has 30 intended people, 25 planned digital routes, five call assignments and one separate publication. At 08:12 a changed report moves the service to Hall C at 11:00. Stop unissued revision-one alerts, preserve originals already sent and issue a new revision to all still-eligible affected people, including those who received the earlier details. A 09:00 all-clear releases or supersedes each remaining pending instruction according to its content; a separate affected scene cannot be cleared by inference. Present the latest revision prominently and show effective time and previous issue status.

A caller who reaches only voicemail records “attempted, no confirmed person.” The incident lead sees unresolved confirmations and escalates according to the phone tree. Distribute only neutral wording where endpoints may be unsafe. Publishing to a general channel cannot substantiate that any one person saw it. If the church has a reunification operation, explicit verified handovers and welfare checks feed its own register.

## Reminder calendar procedure

- For an elapsed offset convert the occurrence to an unambiguous instant, subtract the requested minutes, then evaluate the recipient window. For a local calendar-day rule select the intended local date, intended wall-clock time, zone and ambiguity resolution, then resolve the instant. Persist both local intent and resolved instant.
- Always apply the specified calendar and zone for the deadline, the source anchor and recipient quiet hours; these can differ. For an overnight 21:00–07:00 quiet period the first permitted time is 07:00 in the recipient's zone. Define intervals as start-inclusive and end-exclusive. Intersect sender windows, quiet hours, service deadline and latest useful time. If no permitted instant remains, expire or escalate under the named rule.
- On source revision inspect the stable occurrence, current attendance or duty status, intended recipient and previously issued revision. Cancel obsolete pending work; an issued materially changed fact needs a separately authorized correction. Ignore an out-of-order earlier source revision. A volunteer swap transfers an upcoming duty to the new volunteer and suppresses outstanding personal reminders to the previous one.
- On outage, resume only current, eligible reminders within their catch-up and expiry limits. A repeated scheduler tick or changed wall-clock offset does not create another intended reminder. Display unresolved ambiguous source times for human resolution when no configured policy can choose an offset.

**Fixture U2.** A service at 08:30 local uses reminders 12 elapsed hours before, with quiet hours 20:00–07:00 in the same zone and latest useful time 08:00. The planned 20:30 the previous day is deferred to 07:00, then rechecked. If the service changes to 07:10 and the agreed latest useful time is 06:40, deferral to 07:00 exceeds the deadline and the automatic route expires. If it had already been sent, the granted authority determines whether to issue one correction. A member reassigned at 06:55 cannot receive the former duty's 07:00 reminder simply because they were on the earlier roster.

## Verification

The coordinator can reproduce affected population, accepted messages, confirmed acknowledgements, unresolved follow-ups and manual calls. An editor cannot issue a superseding official instruction without its required approvals. A phone-tree call outcome cannot silently become a medical or safeguarding record. A restoration performed after all-clear produces no stale evacuation alerts.
