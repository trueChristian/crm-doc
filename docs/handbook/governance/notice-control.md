# Issuing and amending a call to meet

**Outcome.** Before the chair opens the meeting, the clerk can identify the text each eligible class was entitled to receive, the latest service deadline, the route used, any unresolved exception and the body empowered to rule on it. Use the [meeting records](../../governance/meetings-and-notice.md) and [dispatch records](../../communications/campaigns-and-content.md). A reminder of an existing call and a changed formal call are distinct issues.

## Calculate the deadline

1. Choose the governing body, class, current charter edition, service calendar and governing time zone. Freeze these for the issued notice. State whether the relevant event is posting, dispatch, receipt or deemed service. The administrator must supply a rule for nonworking days and the exact local cutoff when service is time-limited.
2. For clear calendar days, let D be the local meeting date and N the required number. The latest effective service date is D minus (N + 1) calendar days. For clear business days enumerate preceding dates in reverse, excluding meeting date, weekend and holidays; retain N eligible clear dates and choose a service date strictly before the earliest. Roll to a permissible service day if required. An elapsed-hours rule instead subtracts its duration from the meeting instant.
3. Convert each route's dispatch to effective service using its own rule. A postal estimate is a scheduling aid unless the charter recognizes deemed service after a specified interval. When proof of receipt is required a posting certificate alone leaves a pending item. Keep date and evidence separate.
4. On an incomplete charter, withhold an automatic “valid” result. The chair sees which criterion needs a ruling, who may make that ruling and any deadlines. Corrections, waivers and adjournments require their respective authority.

**Fixture N1.** General meeting on Saturday 20 June 2026 at 10:00 local, five clear calendar days, effective service allowed every day. Service on 14 June leaves the 15th through the 19th clear and qualifies. Service on the 15th leaves four clear days. Under a separate five-clear-business-day rule with no holidays and service allowed on working days only, 15–19 June are the five days and Friday 12 June is the latest permissible working-day service. Under that same rule with Wednesday 17 June designated a holiday, the five dates are 12, 15, 16, 18 and 19 June, so service must occur by Thursday 11 June. Save the selected rule, holiday revision and eligible dates with the calculation. A changed holiday calendar cannot silently rewrite historical validity.

## Build and serve the issued pack

Construct the notice roll using its own cutoff. Include non-voting observers when entitled to notice; retain the voting roll separately. Prepare location, joining instructions, accessible alternatives, agenda and controlled papers. Public posters omit restricted joining information. The authorizer views the exact digest and a privacy-safe audience count before signing. Freeze the pack and queue a distinct permitted recipient operation for each intended recipient. Staff can record service by hand, postal dispatch, formal public display or individual acknowledgment with date and evidence. Service to an agent uses a current representation authority for that action.

**Fixture N2.** Six electors A–F must receive notice and an observer G must receive one too. A and B have accepted email service. C needs a printed letter. D and E share one address but the governing rule requires individual notice. F uses acknowledged hand delivery. G receives an observer packet. Seven intended service entries result, including two individually addressed copies for D and E. A public Telegram post is an additional announcement. It contributes zero individual service results. If B's email bounces before the deadline, the clerk tries a permitted alternative and attaches its evidence to B's same entitlement. If the service is late, B remains late even if they attend.

## Late business, postponement and waivers

Classify a post-issue edit as clerical, substantive business, changed time, changed venue, cancellation or adjournment to a linked sitting. Compare the old and new packs and apply the governing rule to each affected class. A spelling correction need not restart time under a rule defining it as clerical. Changing a venue from accessible Hall A to upstairs Hall B changes attendance arrangements and triggers a new service assessment. A late resolution requiring special notice must be deferred or referred to the authorized procedure. A chair's informal approval cannot confer a waiver unless the governing instrument permits it.

In N1, on 16 June the trustees propose disposing of property for the first time. The policy requires five clear days' notice of the transaction, and the annual meeting is still on 20 June. The proposed disposition fails this meeting's test. Retain it as deferred business, issue a new call for a properly dated sitting, and keep the earlier agenda intact. If the governing rule permits unanimous written waiver by entitled persons, record each ratifying person, authority, item, scope and instant. A missing, invalid or withdrawn signature prevents waiver completion. Never treat attendance or an unsigned click-through as blanket waiver.

## Exit tests

- Replay a dispatch confirmation twice. The same underlying service entry acquires the evidence once; recipient count and issue number stay fixed.
- A meeting moved from the 20th to the 27th retains both dated notices. Obsolete reminders stop and ballots for the old sitting cannot be issued under the new sitting by accident.
- A guest checking the public notice sees venue and time, lacks access to private papers, and sees no exposed names in failed-service or waiver reports.
- An officer reviewing validity six months later can reproduce business-day calculations, see any ruling in force when the chair opened and distinguish it from evidence received subsequently.
