# Confirming minutes, correcting the record and hearing a review

The clerk distinguishes an error in a transcript from a change in the body's decision. Keep the meeting evidence, signed minute edition, resolution, implementation action, spending authority, recipient extract and appellate ruling individually identifiable. The [minute book](../../governance/minutes-and-resolutions.md) and [review register](../../governance/annual-cycle-and-policy.md) own those records. A material correction may also affect an [accountant’s previously acknowledged handoff](../../finance/accounting-exchange.md).

## Prepare and confirm

1. Compile actual chair, secretary, meeting times, attendance, apologies, quorum checks, agenda versions, motions, decisions, declared interests and permitted annexes. Secret ballots supply certified aggregates. The draft does not include confidential election traces or case notes merely because the clerk can view them.
2. Mark each item by audience and choose any separately controlled annex. Select the evidence required for claims about attendance or expenditure. The draft records its edition number, prior edition, source revisions, compiler and compilation instant. Human reviewers see changed wording in context.
3. Send the approved review copy only to authorized recipients. A signed extract uses reviewed text and an immutable source. Confirmation authority may rest with a subsequent meeting whose membership differs from the earlier sitting. Verify office and authority at the time of attestation.
4. Bind each confirmation to the same digest of body and annex manifest. A returned draft records reviewer concerns. Changing text or replacing an annex requires the approvals defined for the new digest. Publish only the authorized issued edition, and return a stable reference to that edition and the current-correction status.

## Four resolutions that demand different treatment

| What happened | Clerk's action | Downstream effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Person's surname is misspelled in an unsigned draft | Correct a new draft revision and name the source | Refresh review copies; no issued record has changed. |
| Verified count sheet says 10,000 but signed minutes transcribed 100,000 | Record a correction proposal, require competent approval, issue a linked erratum or superseding edition | Notify holders of prior extracts; review the authorization actually used to execute an action. |
| Signed minutes accurately authorize 10,000, and leaders now want 12,000 | Put a new motion by the competent body | Link the new decision, amount, fund and effective date; issue a separately approved funding request. |
| A petition challenges an eligible voter on the original decision | Open a procedural review with standing, dispute period, evidence custody and independent decision maker | Retain the as-issued decision unless an authorized stay or ruling changes its operation. |

**Fixture M1.** On 3 May the body approves 10,000 for repairs. The secretary certifies minutes on 12 May but types 100,000. An extract is issued on 13 May and finance commits 6,000 on 14 May. The signed vote sheet supports 10,000. On 17 May a clerk flags the discrepancy and an officer with authority freezes further releases pending review. On 22 May the authorized body adopts the correction. The first edition and extract remain historically authentic as records of what was issued; the status shows they have been superseded. The active authority is 10,000, expenditure committed remains 6,000 and residual authority is 4,000, subject to other budget checks. If commitment had been 13,000, report an exception of 3,000 for finance and leadership decision. Do not change the bank, purchase or contract by editing the minutes.

## Build a review dossier

Verify the requester's identity or authorized representative, standing under the procedure, disputed item, decision time, applicable filing deadline and any approved extension. Create an intake receipt visible to the requester. Preserve the exact challenged edition, original policy, quorum and decision fact set, index of exhibits, disclosure restrictions, received times and subsequent submissions. A late exhibit labels its actual date and reason. A party receives only the allowable extract. Where a response from another party is required, record the response window, inaccessible contact routes, substitutions and service proofs.

Assign a reviewer with jurisdiction and an independent decision maker when required. Record conflicts and replacements, who saw which evidence, whether execution is stayed, the hearing's applicable rules, outcome, reasons, effective date and route of further appeal. Possible rulings include dismiss for lack of standing, decline an untimely petition under the adopted rules, correct the transcription, direct a new meeting or overturn a decision. The original issuer cannot certify their own review when the rule requires independence. Notify affected action owners once, retain their acknowledgements and let each owning domain decide any financial, personnel or safeguarding consequences.

## Acceptance and access

- A signatory signs edition 2 while edition 3 is awaiting release. The signature attaches solely to edition 2; a missing current signature remains outstanding.
- A released copy of a general session omits a confidential annex. A shared link, document preview, search result or export cannot reveal the omitted title or download target.
- The deadline expires on a local holiday and the rule directs next-working-day filing. The clerk can reproduce the due instant under the holiday calendar in force at notice. If no holiday adjustment was adopted the system exposes that fact for the competent reviewer.
- A decision is rescinded after a service contract was partly performed. Pending authorizations route to review; completed obligations and prior certified minutes remain traceable. A rerun produces no second cancellation request.
