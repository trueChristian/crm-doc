# Accreditation, proxies and item-level quorum

**Decision.** Can the body take this particular action at this instant? Determine the entitled population, voting rights, in-person or remote presence, valid representation and item-specific exclusions. Use [dated terms](../../governance/bodies-and-terms.md) and the [frozen meeting roll](../../governance/meetings-and-notice.md). A regular service attendance total, an RSVP or a member login cannot establish deliberative quorum.

## Ordered calculation

1. Build candidate entitlements from the charter's effective cutoff and authorised evidence. For each candidate resolve the legal person, seat if applicable, representation class, vote weight, right to speak, right to vote and reason. Record lawful dual capacities as separate rights only if the constitution permits both to be exercised. Preserve vacancies separately from occupied eligible seats.
2. Resolve challenges before certification where possible. Freeze roll edition, rule edition and exact denominator basis. An appeal after freeze produces a ruling and a new version for affected decisions as permitted. Historical results keep the version used.
3. At time T include presence intervals that start at or before T and end after T or are open-ended. Treat joining and leaving at the same instant under the stated transition order. Separate attendance for observers. A proxy holder need not be treated as the principal physically attending.
4. For each entitlement determine the actor who may exercise it by item and time. Accept a valid unrevoked mandate, verify scope and holder cap, reject self-reference, chains and cycles, and apply the principal-return rule. Resolve duplicate or conflicting mandates by the configured formal rule. If the record cannot decide, suspend that specific right for a clerk ruling and show the potential impact.
5. Resolve item recusals and representative conflicts independently. Apply the rule controlling both the principal's and the actor's conflicts. Reduce local counts or the denominator only as the rule directs. Compute headcount, weight and representation categories separately, record their units and compare each with its own required threshold.
6. Freeze the inputs at opening, at changes that the procedures require, and before each substantive vote. A failed quorum marks affected business ineligible to proceed. After a voter departs the old opening test remains valid solely for the instant it measured.

## Fixture Q1, personal presence and a visiting proxy principal

Assume 12 eligible voting seats, all occupied, four people required in person or permitted live attendance, no proxy counted toward quorum, non-voting observers excluded. One holder may exercise at most one other right. A principal attending personally displaces their proxy prospectively. Timestamps use the meeting zone.

| Time | Facts | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 18:55 | A, B, C present; observer O arrives; B holds absent E's verified proxy | Headcount 3, represented rights 4, quorum short one. No substantive motion. |
| 19:01 | D checks in | Headcount 4. Five rights are exercisable on unrestricted items, four personally and E through B. |
| 19:08 | F also offers B a proxy | B already holds E's right. Reject F's pending assignment or resolve a permitted named substitution before issue. The count remains five rights. |
| 19:12 | E arrives in person | Headcount 5. B no longer exercises E. Five rights remain exercisable. If E's ballot was already cast under a valid prior mandate, the same round right remains consumed. |
| 19:16 | C leaves; D has a conflict on the current item. This rule excludes a recused person from item quorum but keeps the fixed four-person threshold | Five minus one departure minus one recusal equals three. Substantive decision pauses. Earlier decisions stay on the ledger of decisions. |
| 19:19 | The item is deferred and D can participate in the next item | A, B, D, E supply four present. Next item is quorate. The deferred item needs a new check when recalled. |

**Delegation variant.** An assembly has a threshold of three congregations represented and eight personal participants. Nine present from two congregations satisfy attendance and fail representation. Adding one eligible delegate from the third satisfies both. One observer from the third satisfies neither condition for a voting delegation. A report must show each independent result.

## Evidence and exception tests

- A delegate loses connectivity at 19:20, rejoins at 19:24 and grants no proxy. Under a two-minute grace rule authenticated loss is recorded at 19:20, grace expires 19:22, and a vote at 19:23 excludes the delegate. Preserve the observed interruption and rule-derived interval separately.
- The clerk discovers two people shared a mailing address. The roll still retains their two eligible rights. An actual duplicate person is routed for independent adjudication; an identity merge cannot silently erase a historically cast ballot.
- A verified quorum correction entered the next day states that C left three minutes earlier. Preserve both the as-certified and restated checks, identify potentially affected decisions, notify the authorized authority for ruling, and prevent automatic rescission or retroactive ballot editing.
- An observer views the public decision announcement. Only authorized clerks and adjudicators can inspect identity proof, proxy instructions, each entitlement's status and private recusal evidence.
