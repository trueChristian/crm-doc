# Purchasing and matching desk

## Separate five balances

A request reserves authority if the book enables reservations. A purchase order commits a quantity and value. A receipt establishes custody or confirms completion. A supplier invoice establishes a documented charge. A disbursement settles a liability. These events can occur in different periods. Maintain independent quantity, inventory value, receipt accrual, outstanding payable and unspent budget. A screen that marks an entire order “paid” after the first installment would conceal remaining delivery commitments.

The requester enters needed-by date, usage, ministry, fund, account, expected quantity/unit and acceptable substitute. The approver sees original request, likely vendor, competitive quotes, total exposure including delivery and nonrecoverable tax, related requests and authority. A buyer revises an order through a versioned change. A receiver enters observed quantities and condition with a dated service certificate or delivery evidence. An accounts clerk matches against approved order, accepted receipt, actual invoice and any credit. A treasury officer releases the exact authorized payment destination and amount.

## Tolerance policy and comparison grain

Set permitted tolerances per book, procurement class, supplier risk and period. Compare converted units and currency before checking tolerance. Store absolute amount and percentage, denominator, whether both must pass, rounding precision, treatment of tax/freight and the aggregate check across the order. A zero-priced item cannot use a percentage denominator; route for explicit authorization if a financial charge appears. A tolerance permits a specified difference after checking other facts; it cannot turn rejected goods into accepted receipt or override an exhausted donor restriction.

| Condition | Matching outcome | Follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quantity billed ≤ accepted unbilled quantity, price and tax valid | Match eligible portion and allow approval | Retain source quantities, conversion factors and remaining commitment. |
| Quantity billed exceeds accepted balance | Hold the excess or whole bill under the configured split policy | Request proof of another receipt, corrected invoice or authorized exception. Unaccepted quantities remain visible. |
| Price increase is within permitted absolute **and** percentage limits | Attribute difference according to price variance or inventory policy | Inform the budget owner when commitments and projected spend change. |
| Exact duplicate vendor document or same payable already settled | Hold duplicate before computing available payable | Verify supplier, book, currency and underlying obligation; link an authorized legitimate repeated number. |
| Wrong destination, missing supplier verification or conflict of interest | Block payment release even when invoice matching succeeds | Verify through the established independent route and seek fresh approval. |
| Credit or return exceeds remaining charge/quantity | Refuse excess application, preserve unapplied supplier credit if genuine | Request original invoice/receipt support and retain disputed amount separately. |

## Fixture: teaching materials ordered for a camp

The campus has an approved purchase authority of 1,200. Order 100 units at 10 each. The procurement budget measures acquisition commitments and posted bill costs; these are independent of the income statement if goods enter inventory. Assume no tax, no freight, all units held until after settlement and purchase discounts reduce inventory cost. The church recognizes receipt accruals at approved order price. Price tolerance requires both ≤2% of selected order unit price and ≤10 total per matched line. No billed quantity may exceed accepted units without another receipt.

1. Order 100 units at 10. Authorized order value is 1,000, open order 1,000 and unreserved purchase authority 200. No AP, inventory or cash posting yet.
2. Receive 60 in good condition. Debit inventory 600, credit receipts awaiting invoice 600. The outstanding order-to-receive is 40 units/400. A budget tracker can show 600 received/unbilled plus 400 ordered/unreceived as components of the 1,000 already committed. Leave total reserved authority unchanged.
3. The supplier bills 62 at 10.10 = 626.20. The unit variance 0.10 is 1%, but two units lack receipt. Under this fixture's whole-bill hold, current AP is zero, receipt accrual 600, and the invoice queue explains two unsupported units. Splitting the matched 60 and holding 2 is a different selectable policy and must never recognize the 2 as available inventory.
4. Obtain a corrected invoice for 60 at 10.10 = 606. The price difference of 6 meets the 2% and 10 limits. Debit receipt accrual 600, debit inventory price adjustment 6 and credit supplier AP 606. Stock 60 costs 606. Receipt accrual is zero and AP 606. Remaining purchase authority = 1,200 − 606 posted − 400 open commitment = 194.
5. Five unopened defective units go back under a nonreplacement agreement. Their credited cost is 50.50; link the dispatch, supplier credit and original receipt. Debit AP 50.50 and credit inventory 50.50 once the authorized credit is recognized. Remaining 55 units cost 555.50, AP 555.50. The original remaining 40 units are unchanged. Mark the five as returned/closed so they cannot be billed or reordered automatically. On the fixture's procurement budget this restores 50.50 to available authority.
6. Cancel the outstanding 40 with evidence and release 400 commitment. Pay the remaining invoice within an approved 2% discount period. Discount 11.11 and bank payment 544.39 settle AP 555.50. Debit AP 555.50, credit bank 544.39 and credit inventory cost 11.11. Fifty-five units now cost 544.39 in total. Any subsequent unit costing must preserve precision and exact remaining layer value. Available acquisition authority is 655.61 if this policy applies discounts to budget actuals.
7. Trace the supplier statement: invoice 606 less credit 50.50, discount 11.11 and payment 544.39 = zero. AP aging, receipt accrual balance, accepted unbilled quantity and open commitment all equal zero. Vendor liability and bank transaction show the same settlement reference at their distinct dates.

## Exception variants

If the supplier invoice is dated before year end but evidence shows the church acquired control after year end, apply the selected cutoff terms and show billed-but-not-received separately, or recognize a documented prepayment. If goods were shipped on terms transferring ownership before physical delivery, record owned inventory in transit at the supported date. Receiving it into the local store transfers custody without another acquisition. Preserve proof of shipment, title policy, date, destination, quantity, arrival and any loss claim. Supplier invoice date alone cannot establish transfer of control.

For goods received after purchase price rises above tolerance, the screen shows accepted quantity and disputed price as separate decisions. A supervisor approving the variance must specify whether it adjusts inventory, expense, a variance account or a specific grant's recoverable amount. On items partly sold before a late freight invoice, allocate the supported inventory cost across remaining stock and goods already sold. A supplier credit received after payment creates an open debit/credit balance on the supplier account that may be applied to a later bill or received as cash; it cannot silently erase the prior bank payment.

For a service milestone, use acceptance evidence and percentage delivered in place of warehouse quantity. An actual service certificate for 40% of a 2,000 agreement supports 800. Deduct previous accepted billings, approved retention and any advances from payable amount. Track retention separately with release conditions. A catch-up invoice for 1,200 cannot be matched simply because the order ceiling is 2,000; at 40% cumulative acceptance the unsupported amount is 400 before considering prior billings.

Test duplicate invoices across book boundaries, a returned transfer, fragmented invoices attempting to evade a tolerance, a claim from the approving officer, expired tax exemption, changed supplier routing, currency mismatch and supplier credit applied concurrently by two clerks. Report per order the ordered, cancelled, received, returned, accepted, invoiced, disputed, paid and outstanding quantities/amounts.
