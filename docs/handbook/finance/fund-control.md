# Restricted-fund decisions and close

## Establish the authority behind a balance

The secretary may record a board resolution designating money for a building; a donor may restrict a gift to building work; a grantor may pay money in advance, refundable until milestones are accepted. Each case can use the same bank account and project name. They produce different obligations and approval routes. The treasurer needs the governing instrument, effective date, permissible costs, expiry or release condition, person authorized to interpret terms and required evidence. A transfer between two bank accounts changes custody and liquidity. By itself it supplies no evidence of satisfying a donor restriction.

| Question on intake | Accounting and operating consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Is the church collecting solely for another accountable body? | Under a documented agency policy recognize the amount owed to that body, retain remittance evidence, expose overdue distributions and keep it out of the church's giving income. |
| Does the agreement create a barrier and a right of refund or release from obligation under the church's policy? | Determine whether cash remains an advance liability until the qualifying event occurs. Keep gross cash and unearned obligation separately visible. |
| Has the donor specified time, beneficiary or permitted use? | Classify recognized resources under the applicable restriction, track spending eligibility and require release evidence. Gift receipt is not automatic fulfilment of a future-purpose restriction. |
| Has a board internally reserved otherwise available resources? | Keep designation and authority to redesignate. Preserve original unrestricted status for reports that classify by donor restriction. |
| Is this capital required to be held or returned? | Separate protected principal, expendable income, spending limits and actual cash. An investment loss can make the fund underwater and require trustee review. |
| Does the classification or permitted use remain unclear? | Route for documented clarification before spending or issuing an inappropriate acknowledgement. Preserve the received instrument, uncertainty and review deadline. |

## Worked two-fund balance sheet

Use accrual accounting, one pooled bank and separate balanced fund books. General opens with 300 cash and 300 net assets. Restricted Outreach opens with 200 cash and 200 net assets, all available for qualified outreach work. The church holds no obligations. In this example expenses appear in General and supported releases transfer resources and cash from Outreach when conditions are met. An implementation using expense-by-fund can map to the same presentation with an explicit, balanced reporting bridge. Use one release route consistently.

| Event | General fund | Restricted Outreach fund | Combined implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Receive a new unconditionally recognized outreach gift of 80 | No entry | Debit allocated bank 80, credit restricted contributions 80 | New cash and income 80. Fund bank 280. |
| Receive a separate cash advance of 120, refundable until grantor accepts a milestone | No entry | Debit allocated bank 120, credit refundable grant advance 120 | Cash rises to 700 combined. Recognized resource remains 580 combined before expenditure. |
| Pay qualifying supplies of 90 initially from General | Debit outreach expense 90, credit allocated bank 90 | No entry yet | Combined bank 610. General cash 210, total expenses 90. |
| Reviewer accepts eligibility of the supplies against the unconditional outreach restriction, independent of the advance | Debit allocated bank 90, credit releases in 90 | Debit releases out 90, credit allocated bank 90 | Releases net to zero in combined presentation. Pooled bank stays 610. |

At the first cutoff General has bank and net assets 300. Restricted Outreach has bank 310, advance liability 120 and restricted net assets 190. Combined assets 610 minus liabilities 120 equal net assets 490. The bridge starts at 500 opening net assets, adds 80 recognized income, deducts 90 supplies and ends at 490. Column totals show releases in and out 90; organization-wide external giving remains 80. A public report can disclose the outreach purpose and totals while concealing the subscriber or beneficiary identity.

In the next period the grantor formally accepts an independently satisfied milestone for 60 and the church recognizes that portion as restricted grant income under the instrument. Debit advance liability 60 and credit restricted grant income 60. Bank remains 610, remaining liability 60 and net assets 550. If the grantor requires the unearned remainder back, authorize and pay 60 by debiting refundable advance and crediting allocated bank. Bank then falls to 550, liability zero, with no expense and no additional impact on net assets. If the same invoice previously supported another draw, the claim screen must show the remaining eligible amount independently of other restriction releases.

## Eligibility and release queue

Classify each proposed expenditure by fund, spend category, time window, beneficiary or grant location and source amount. Determine whether indirect costs, staff time, capital costs, shared costs or prior-period invoices are allowed. A 100 cost split between two grants can support 60 and 40 when permitted; allocation of 60 and 50 would exceed the source by 10. The reviewer chooses an adjusted split or obtains separate eligible evidence. A single cost might satisfy a general purpose and a reporting obligation without permitting payment twice. Preserve which relationship is an entitlement to reimbursement versus evidence of an outcome.

A time restriction ending on 30 June may release resources into available funds at that date if the agreement permits. An unspent purpose restriction with an administrative reporting deadline does not automatically expire on 30 June. At project closure produce a destination proposal for each balance: continue for the same purpose, carry pending costs, obtain donor-authorized redirection, return unearned/unused money, or retain capital and distribute only eligible income. The authorized party, evidence, effective date, bank account and remaining linked installments must be recorded. The approval button should state which balance will move and what financial report will change.

## Fund conservation and deficit handling

For a balanced-fund book at every cutoff, allocated assets minus allocated liabilities equal the net assets for each fund. Pooled bank summed over all fund allocations must equal the bank control. If General pays an invoice temporarily on behalf of a restricted fund and a release is not appropriate, capture reciprocal due-from and due-to entries. For a 40 eligible invoice paid by General and expensed directly to Outreach, record General debit due from Outreach 40, credit allocated cash 40; Outreach debit outreach expense 40, credit due to General 40. Settlement moves the allocated cash in the opposite direction and clears both reciprocal accounts. Consolidated external amounts show expense 40 once and paid cash 40 once.

Close preparations list active funds with negative available balances, unpaid obligations, unsupported releases, outstanding grants, unbanked balances, aged interfund claims and overdue donor reports. A reserve with negative cash but positive receivables may be solvent yet unable to fund a payment now. A donor-funded project with a positive bank amount and an equal refundable advance has no recognized expendable net assets from that advance under this fixture. Show cash, recognized resources and permitted spending authority as three distinct values.

Acceptance requires dated roll-forwards, disclosure by resource class, baseline plus adjustment history and the ability to drill from any release to the source instrument and paid/earned evidence. Disabling a ministry, changing a fund name or moving campuses preserves earlier terms and approved reports.
