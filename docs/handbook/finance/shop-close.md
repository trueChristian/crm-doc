# Order-to-ledger exceptions

## Determine who supplied what

One checkout can contain church-owned inventory, a consigned item, a time-limited digital subscription, a donation, a ticket to a future retreat and a refundable key deposit. Decide at line level who is principal, what the purchaser receives, which tax evidence applies, who owns the gift allocation, how the party paid and when each promise is performed. Name the seller of record and the owning book before selection of clearing and tax accounts. A single payment instrument can settle several lines; it still has exactly one capture and one cash/clearing debit.

A discount may apply only to eligible products or to an entire package. Specify whether allocation uses standalone selling prices, quantity, a documented fixed component or another approved basis. Preserve the allocation used at checkout. Percentage and fixed discounts can be mutually exclusive or sequential. A gift or a refundable deposit is excluded from this fixture's commercial discounts. A shipping credit can have its own treatment and tax base. Check that the sum of discounts excludes any excess beyond the eligible subtotal and that tax is computed on the approved pre- or post-discount basis.

## Fixture: two printed books and quarterly access

The store offers two copies of a book priced at 30 each and three months of online access priced at 40. A package discount of 10 applies proportionally to the 100 standalone total. An additional 15 donation goes to the general ministry fund. Books carry an illustrative 10% tax on the discounted amount; the access is outside this fixture's tax base. Both goods transfer on collection, access revenue is recognized evenly over three full months, and tax on books becomes due on delivery, contemporaneous with capture. Processor fee is 2.40. The two serial-tracked copies cost 11 and 13. These are the only opening assets, with 24 opening net assets.

| Segment | Gross before discount | Discount | Net commercial price | Tax | Capture portion |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Two books | 60 | 6 | 54 | 5.40 | 59.40 |
| Three months' access | 40 | 4 | 36 | 0 | 36 |
| Separate donation | 15 | 0 | 15 | 0 | 15 |
| **Total** | **115** | **10** | **105** | **5.40** | **110.40** |

At collection and capture, debit provider clearing 110.40; credit merchandise revenue 54, sales tax liability 5.40, deferred access revenue 36 and giving income 15. The gift document binds to the 15 allocation, and its imported notice can only acknowledge the existing journal portion. Debit cost of sales 24 and credit inventory 24 against the specific two copies. When the processor pays 108, debit bank 108 and fees 2.40 and credit clearing 110.40. Clearing returns to zero; the member may view only their personal invoice and any separately eligible giving receipt.

At each month end when a full access period has been supplied under the fixture, debit deferred access and credit media revenue 12. After month one, liability 24 and earned access 12. After month two, liability 12 and earned access 24. After month three, liability zero and media revenue 36. A reader's failure to watch the sermon does not undo elapsed stand-ready access under the stated policy. If access was never supplied because the grant failed, hold recognition pending verified remediation or contractual determination. An irreversible download or a perpetual licence may have another recognition policy that must be evidenced separately.

## Return with identifiable cost and damaged stock

During the third month the customer returns the copy originally costed at 13. The full discounted unit price is 27; tax on that unit was 2.70. Approve a 29.70 refund by debiting merchandise returns 27 and tax payable 2.70 and crediting refund payable 29.70. On provider execution debit refund payable and credit clearing 29.70. On bank recovery debit clearing and credit bank 29.70. Total net bank receipts become 78.30. A provider refund fee, if actually charged, is a separate event. Digital access, donation and the other physical copy stay intact.

Restore the original 13 cost by debiting inventory and crediting cost of sales 13 when the return transfers control. Inspection establishes a supported recoverable value of 10. Debit write-down expense 3 and credit inventory or its valuation allowance 3. Remaining net physical cost of sales is 11, write-down 3 and returned stock 10. The checkout cannot credit inventory at the 27 retail price, nor at average cost if the governing policy uses specific identification. Capture inspection status separately from availability for resale.

After the three months, net recognized commercial and giving income is 27 merchandise + 36 media + 15 giving = 78. Expenses are cost of sales 11, write-down 3 and processor fees 2.40. Change in net assets = 61.60. Closing bank 78.30 + stock 10 − sales tax owed 2.70 = net assets 85.60. This equals opening net assets 24 plus 61.60. One journal drill-down can explain the 15 gift without exposing it in a volunteer's pick list or sermon access administration.

## Tax, split tender and chargeback variants

If an item has two taxes, preserve their order and each taxable base and rounding. For 100 net with 10% first tax and 5% on net plus first tax, original components are 10 and 5.50. A 40% accepted credit recovers 4 and 2.20 from the same original applied components; remaining comparison is 60 base, 6 first tax, 3.30 second tax. If a rate changed after the original invoice, display original and current policy and route any legally required adjustment separately. Cross-document totals for document-rounded tax may differ from independently rounding each line; the last credit restores exactly the remaining permitted component amount.

For a 110 purchase paid 60 from a previously purchased voucher and 50 by card, debit stored-value liability 60 and clearing 50 against the eligible invoice plus tax. An external payment retry cannot debit the voucher twice. On a partial return, follow the published tender allocation rule and restore the permitted voucher portion and card portion with distinct confirmations. If an online authorization fails after voucher reservation but before any capture, release the voucher reservation; the original liability remains available. Free promotional coupons can reduce price under the marketing policy and are distinct from purchased value.

For a bank or processor dispute, distinguish open claim, temporary withdrawal, final outcome and any prior refund. If a disputed 50 has already been refunded but the provider withdraws another 50, finance records the real cash movement and a claim/exception against the provider under approved policy. Do not reverse the same original sale twice. Evidence includes order, consent, delivery, grant/refund times, prior credit and provider case. Winning returns the temporary withdrawal, losing resolves the recognized receivable or expense according to the claim's accounting. Only the provider's fee is a separate fee line where applicable.

Require allocation of every capture/credit across tenders, original line references, book, donor and tax sources. A shop clerk sees fulfilment and return amounts; the donor officer sees only gift authority; finance has independently authorized access to the reconciliation.
