# From a sealed offering to a donor statement

## Count and verify an offering

Open a batch for the receiving church, campus, service or other collection, local date and currency. Record the bag identifier, original seal, collector and handoff time. Let an unknown expected amount remain empty. If the bag arrives unsealed, record its condition at receipt, identify the receiving witness and send the exception to the designated supervisor. A bag awaiting investigation must remain traceable to its current custodian.

Print or issue individually numbered collection sheets when the count takes place offline. Capture the round, tender, counter, witness where required, location and time. Enter the same sheet identifier when connectivity returns. Have the second counter record an independent round under their own identity. Use the church's authorized alternate procedure for a small congregation, including who will independently review the record and when. A correction to a signed count requires a new round with the original still readable.

Church North uses two counters. Both record the following contents for bag S10 in one currency.

| Item | Declared | Verified | Instruction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Envelope A | 120 cash | 120 cash | Identified donor asks for 90 General and 30 Youth, with no public naming. |
| Envelope B | 80 cheque | 80 cheque | Identified donor gives 80 to Missions. |
| Envelope C | 50 cash | 47 cash | No confirmed donor or designation. The church has an approved policy for an unmarked gift to General. |
| **Total** | **250** | **247** | **167 cash and 80 cheque. General 137, Youth 30, Missions 80.** |

Compare the two rounds, then compare the accepted round to envelope instructions and tender totals. The two matching rounds support one count of 247. The supervisor records Envelope C's declared shortfall of 3, approves actual cash of 47 and identifies the selected round. An actual loss after the count would require a separate custody exception. Route an empty pledge card to stewardship and a prayer request to the private care channel. Neither contributes to the count. Separate currencies into their own control totals and record any cheque or note the recipient cannot accept for investigation.

## Resolve attribution privately

The giving clerk can resolve an envelope against a verified donor reference. A payment account name, common surname or shared address may suggest a candidate. Seek evidence or contact the donor through an authorized channel before making a disputed assignment. A person may be a visitor, a representative of an organization, a surviving spouse or a household member acting only for themselves. Store what supports the attribution and which recipient can receive correspondence. Keep identifiable notes out of count sheets, courier manifests and shared ministry reports.

A's donor can receive a personal statement and choose anonymity in public communications. C initially has no identified giving party eligible for personal correspondence. If someone claims C after verification, corroborate the envelope reference and instruction without revealing the bag's other gifts. Record the evidence, reviewer and change time. Relinking the existing 47 updates the person's giving history and correspondence eligibility. Its source gift, received date, purpose and batch totals remain the same. A competing claim stays under restricted review. Never generate competing donor statements for the same gift while ownership is unsettled.

Partners at the same address may jointly give for one purpose and separately for others. Apply explicit joint authority only to its covered gifts and period. Preserve separate deliveries and identity verification for the others. If the purpose on an identified envelope cannot be accepted, retain the donor's instruction, assign an owner to seek clarification and hold designation-dependent actions. Record donor authorization for an alternative or refer a return to the responsible payment authority. A volunteer cannot silently reassign the amount.

## Hand off the collection

1. Create a handoff from the accepted round. Record the tender subtotals, handing and receiving custodians, seal, departure, actual receipt and any discrepancy. A receiver confirms under their own authority. If the recipient counts 244, preserve the verified 247 and both observations, assign an owner and follow the three-unit difference separately from the earlier envelope discrepancy.
2. Verify the designated gift facts and request the configured exchange. An API delivery gets a source identity per logical change. A file carries stable row identities and currency totals. The treasurer with no configured accounting exchange can close the local custody work and retrieve the count evidence. A disabled connection produces no claim of external acceptance.
3. Follow each item to a business acknowledgement. A successful upload, a deposit slip and a response that bytes were received answer different questions. Record a rejection with the failed row, reason and authorized recovery. Reuse the same logical identity when retrying an unchanged event, including after a lost reply.
4. Keep accounting and donor information separated by role. An accountant can get the receiving church, effective date, currency, gift or batch reference, amount and purpose under the agreed mapping. Include identifying details only where the receiving role and agreement authorize them. A courier receives custody information. A youth leader sees the disclosure-approved summary. A relationship manager cannot infer a balance available to spend from the 30 allocated to Youth.

## Handle a return and donor correspondence

Suppose all three gifts were verified and the cheque for B later returns in full. Obtain an authenticated source notice identifying B and the affected amount. A notice containing only a bank charge cannot identify a returned gift. Record a dated correction for 80 against B's Missions allocation, retain the verified count, and route any accounting handoff under its own identity. The current giving report now shows General 137, Youth 30 and Missions 0, for a total of 167. The original as-of report still shows 247. A separate processing fee of 5 reported by the bank belongs to the accounting service. The gift report remains 167.

Use the approved giving-date, correction and correspondence policy when constructing a statement. Preview the giving party, authorized contact, covered gifts, currency, adjustment dates and inclusion rules. Have an authorized officer issue the edition. Freeze its recipient, line items, policy and wording, issue identity and date. If the original showed B's 80, link a corrected edition under the configured policy and record the reason. Keep delivery status private. A verified resend of a prior edition reuses that edition. A returned email or print item opens a delivery exception without changing gift history. Where an external service issues the formal tax receipt, retain its issuer and reference, and label CRM correspondence as a giving acknowledgement.

At shift change, hand unresolved envelopes, open bags, outstanding receipts, ambiguous donor claims, held designations, unidentified returns and failed deliveries to named owners. Each handover includes the last verified fact, required evidence, authorized next action and review date. A shared worklist may show a restricted case identifier while the underlying identity and attachments require donor-desk permissions.
