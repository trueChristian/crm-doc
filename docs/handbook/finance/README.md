# Giving desk handbook

On a Sunday morning, the counting team can confirm how much came out of a sealed bag. Later, a donor clerk can establish who gave an envelope and who may receive an acknowledgement. A treasurer can confirm whether the external accounting service accepted the approved facts. Each person needs a different view of the same offering.

| When a worker needs to | Procedure | Evidence they leave |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receive a bag, compare independent counts and explain a shortage | [Count and verify an offering](offering-room.md#count-and-verify-an-offering) | Seal and custody history, count rounds, accepted round, actual amount and reason for a variance |
| Respect a designation, anonymous gift or joint instruction | [Resolve attribution privately](offering-room.md#resolve-attribution-privately) | Allocation and authority for attribution, correspondence and publication |
| Track delivery to the cashier or an external partner | [Hand off the collection](offering-room.md#hand-off-the-collection) | Handover acknowledgement, original gift identities and exchange status |
| Correct a returned instrument or resend a statement | [Handle a return and donor correspondence](offering-room.md#handle-a-return-and-donor-correspondence) | Linked correction, dated report and statement editions, delivery history |
| Deal with interrupted delivery, a rejected purpose code or gifts arriving from another system | [Accounting exchange contract](../../finance/accounting-exchange.md) | Source and destination identities, acknowledged items and assigned exceptions |

Use the [giving records](../../finance/giving.md) to interpret the amounts and permissions. Independently verify the desk using the [offering and exchange cases](../../acceptance/finance/README.md). Responsibilities for bookkeeping and integrations are defined in the [church giving specification](../../finance/README.md).
