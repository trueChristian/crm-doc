# Finance implementation handbook

Use this handbook to decide what the treasurer sees and what the books contain after an operation succeeds, fails or crosses a period boundary. The [finance specification](../../finance/README.md) defines the enduring records. These worked cases specify how to process those records. All numbers are independent acceptance fixtures unless the chapter explicitly carries a balance forward. Rates, deadlines and thresholds in a fixture are inputs. A church must be able to substitute its approved policies.

| Operating question | Case and expected outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Which event may post and what happens when authorization, cash and delivery arrive out of order? | [Posting decisions and replay](posting-decisions.md) follows a single event through validation, acknowledgement, correction, policy change and reporting. |
| How does one receipt reach the correct donor, campaign, restriction and bank deposit? | [Offering room to donor statement](offering-room.md) follows mixed tender, a short count, later identification and a returned cheque without rewriting history. |
| What money can a ministry spend, and how does a restriction end? | [Restricted-fund decisions and close](fund-control.md) builds a two-fund balance sheet, records a grant advance and preserves both gross funding and release presentation. |
| Which part of an order can be received, invoiced, paid or released back to budget? | [Purchasing and matching desk](purchasing-desk.md) reconciles quantities, tolerance decisions, goods in transit, supplier credits and payment controls. |
| How can a parish pay a worker in one period for work spanning two? | [Payroll calendar and correction](pay-calendar.md) covers pay periods, local working calendars, gross-to-net, liability settlement, leave, off-cycle corrections and confidential access. |
| How do gifts, tangible stock, digital access and taxes coexist on one order? | [Order-to-ledger exceptions](shop-close.md) allocates discounts and taxes, times separate revenue obligations and proves payment, refunded tax and COGS. |
| How are outstanding deposits, stale cheques and foreign currency reconciled? | [Bank cutoff and FX desk](bank-cutoff.md) separates bank timing from ledger errors, values monetary items and resolves returned transfers. |
| What happens between board budget approval and the next management pack? | [Planning, allocation and liquidity](budget-desk.md) phases and revises a budget, traces a cost allocation and distinguishes spendable funds from cash. |
| How can supervisors issue, correct and compare financial statements? | [Closing desk and reporting bridge](closing-desk.md) resolves cutoff evidence, estimates, inter-entity differences, reissuance and authority. |
| How do two very different churches prove readiness before first entry? | [Onboarding and acceptance profiles](readiness.md) tests a small receipts-and-payments church and a multicampus accrual entity against operational and financial control totals. |

A preparer should be able to state the legal entity, source and affected period before recognizing any amount. A reviewer should be able to identify the approval, rule snapshot and either the matching settlement or an outstanding balance. An operator investigating a mismatch should see the document, amount, reason, permitted next action and the person responsible; a page showing only a red status does not satisfy these cases. The report reader can distinguish activity awaiting review from posted results and retrieve the approved version as originally issued.
