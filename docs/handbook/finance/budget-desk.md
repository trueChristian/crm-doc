# Budget, allocation and liquidity desk

## Resolve the governing budget

A ministry can submit different scenarios for the same year. A governing body approves a named version against a fiscal calendar and may later amend it. A forecast changes expected performance and does not confer spending authority. Every report names its comparison version, current approval, period, amount basis, inclusion of commitments and signed variance convention. A local church may plan on a cash basis while its governing financial statements use accruals: show the bridge rather than comparing a cash budget directly to depreciation or an unpaid invoice without explanation.

A ministry's annual expense budget is 1,200 phased by quarter: 120, 240, 360 and 480. At the end of Q2 the phased budget is 360. Actual posted expense of 320 creates a favorable 40 variance against the phased plan. Full-year unspent budget is 880 before commitments. One binding order of 150 brings uncommitted authority to 730. Entering a second estimate for the same 150 in a forecast must identify the underlying order so forecast does not count it twice.

| Event | Approved annual | Actual expenses | Open commitments | Uncommitted authority |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Initial Q2 view | 1,200 | 320 | 150 | 730 |
| Accept and book 90 expense from the existing order | 1,200 | 410 | 60 | 730 |
| Supplier and buyer cancel remaining 60 | 1,200 | 410 | 0 | 790 |
| Board transfers 100 from another unrestricted ministry to this responsibility | 1,300 | 410 | 0 | 890 |
| Donor promises another 250 but it is neither approved spending authority nor earned cash | 1,300 | 410 | 0 | 890 |

The 100 transfer increases this ministry and reduces the source ministry's annual authority by the same amount. The board report can still show original 1,200 and revised 1,300. Which quarter receives the transfer depends on the voted effective date and phasing; a later revision should not rewrite the as-issued Q2 comparison. A transfer request against a restricted reserve must first satisfy its instrument even if an unrestricted budget line has ample apparent room.

## Shared costs and rounding

An electricity payment of 101.01 serves three ministries. Direct allocation is inappropriate because one meter serves all. The approved driver is measured occupied floor area in weights 4:3:2. Total driver units are nine. Raw shares are 44.893333…, 33.67 and 22.446666…. Under a largest-remainder method with two-decimal precision, floor the amounts to 44.89, 33.67 and 22.44, then assign the remaining cent to the third row. Final charges 44.89 + 33.67 + 22.45 = 101.01. Document the deterministic tie rule, such as ascending stable dimension code, if residuals tie. A manually adjusted split needs rationale and independent approval and still totals exactly 101.01.

The original invoice debits a shared expense pool and credits payable or bank 101.01. The allocation entry debits the three ministry dimensions for the stated shares and credits the shared expense dimension 101.01. Overall expenses stay 101.01. If the pool contains an item such as an employer benefit or grant-ineligible expense, filter those lines before applying the driver and retain the excluded amount with an explanatory source. When a closed period's area measurement later changes, preserve the original approved allocation; route a delta or later adjustment under the budget and close policies.

Define denominator boundaries before collecting data. Headcount can be people at a date or full-time equivalent during the period. Room usage can be booked time or actual occupied time. Projects with waived hire charges may or may not consume a shared allocation according to the approved policy. If all driver quantities are zero, hold the allocation with the reason and permit only an explicit authorized substitute method. When an original expense is reversed, identify which allocations consumed it and propose the corresponding reversal, preventing an expense disappearing centrally while remaining charged to the ministries.

## Liquidity stress example

The church has bank 1,000. Of this, 300 belongs to a restricted project and 100 is a refundable deposit to a tenant. Available general cash is 600. The board's designated contingency reserve of 80 is part of the 600. Under this fixture's operating policy only 520 is usable routinely unless the board releases the reserve. A positive fund balance is insufficient if the cash is earmarked, pledged but uncollected, tied up in equipment or required to settle an obligation imminently.

| Week | Signed expected general cashflows | Routine available cash at week end |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Opening | General liquid 600 less protected internal contingency 80 | 520 |
| 1 | Collections 170 and payroll payments 240 | 450 |
| 2 | Collections 130 and suppliers 210 | 370 |
| 3 | Collections 100 and scheduled maintenance deposit 300 | 170 |
| 4 | Collections 80 and wages/taxes 200 | 50 |

A downside scenario reduces each week's expected collections by 20%, with costs fixed. Weekly general available cash becomes 416, 310, 90 and −46, respectively. The downside shortfall is 46 in week four. This is a planning warning, not permission to spend the 300 restricted cash or the refundable 100. A proposed board release of 80 would raise week four to 34 only if authorized. Grant reimbursement due in week four could address liquidity only to the extent it is confirmed, unrestricted in purpose, not already counted and actually timely. Publish collection certainty and timing assumptions alongside the forecast.

## Review workbench

The budget holder selects a responsibility and sees actual, committed, approved/revised and forecast by period; drill-down respects donor, payroll and vendor privacy. The finance officer sees transfers crossing dimensions, overspend requests, duplicate encumbrances, grant cap risk, negative lines, overdue deliverables and zero-driver pools. Allow clear override authority for a truly urgent roof repair, require purpose and effective amount, and distinguish the exception from a standing budget increase.

Track capital commitments separately from operating expenses. A 600 projector purchase may reduce project acquisition authority by 600 immediately, while accrual expenses include only relevant depreciation later. A user comparing actual spend must know which basis their dashboard uses. For multi-year grants, distinguish donor award life, budget year and budget revisions with different periods. Carry forward unspent budgets only through approved authority; never treat that action as new gift income. Verify operating, capital and financing cash together reconcile to projected bank and that each subledger obligation enters the forecast once.
