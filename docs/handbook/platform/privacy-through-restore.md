# Preserve privacy choices through correction, disposal and restore

Use the [privacy records](../../platform/privacy-records.md) to prove what someone agreed to, who could disclose it and why a retained record still exists. Restoration reestablishes an earlier technical state. The reopening decision must separately reestablish the church's current obligations. The privacy officer owns the decision about continued processing. The recovery team produces evidence that it reached every controlled copy.

## Evidence and checkpoints

Record the purpose, person or controlled record, field or use, edition shown, actor and representation evidence, scope, effective time and recorded time of each decision. A paper preference received last week but entered today has two distinct dates. Where historic permission cannot be reconstructed, mark it unknown and route any proposed optional use to a reviewer. Record copy classes that need an action rather than repeating the sensitive values in logs.

| Copy class | Owner and expected response to a restriction | Evidence of completion |
|---|---|---|
| Current profile or field | Register steward applies restriction or approved disposal while preserving unrelated facts. | Active edition and change receipt under the person key. |
| Search, aliases, suggestions, thumbnails and learned representations | Relevant projection owner suppresses immediately at read time and clears stale derivatives. | Suppression revision, purge outcome and negative retrieval test. |
| Prepared job or export | Job owner rechecks authority and removes the subject or protected field, or withdraws the artifact. | Prior package tombstone, replacement digest and recipient status. |
| Restricted minutes, journal or held case | Custodian evaluates the approved retention basis and visibility. Preserve the lawful original, issue a redacted rendition or append a correction. | Reason, authority, expiry or review date and approved rendition. |
| Issued print or external copy | Distribution owner requests correction, return or destruction under the applicable agreement. | Per-recipient notification, acknowledgement or unresolved exception. |
| Offline pack, replica or backup | Operations retains a minimal suppression/deletion marker for replay, expires local copies and verifies restoration in quarantine. | Reachable replica confirmations, expiry of outstanding devices and tested restore manifest. |

## Worked sequence

At 08:00 on Thursday a backup contains Dona's surname, contact address and public directory photograph. At 09:05 Dona withdraws permission for public display of that photograph, while keeping a private profile photo. At 09:12 the directory produces a scheduled export. At 10:30 a preservation hold covers specific incident evidence containing the photograph. At 11:20 a steward corrects the private address. At 12:00 a service interruption requires restoration from the 08:00 backup.

1. Record the withdrawal with effective time 09:05 and its separate private-use decision. A directory read after 09:05 produces no photograph. The 09:12 export either omits it under a fresh edition or becomes unissuable. A public thumbnail and a search preview follow the same result.
2. Store the hold scope, legal or policy authority, evidence classes and approved readers. The hold protects its named original evidence. Its existence does not restore public distribution. Staff maintaining the directory cannot see that the person is subject to a hold.
3. Restore the backup into a closed environment. Suspend scheduled sends and suppress all public endpoints. Establish the backed-up privacy watermark, last independently safeguarded obligations watermark and known gap. Replay the withdrawal, hold and address correction in order. A correction with unavailable source evidence remains in restricted review.
4. Test separately as the public visitor, Dona, an ordinary secretary and the designated case custodian. The public visitor sees no image. Dona and authorized private-profile staff see only the permitted private version. The custodian can retrieve only the assigned preserved evidence. An unauthorized user searching by old address receives no private result.
5. Compare the stable ID of the 09:12 export with independent delivery receipts. A delivered package is not sent again during recovery. A content mismatch, unconfirmed provider outcome, unavailable purge acknowledgement or missing obligations segment prevents the affected channel reopening. Unaffected services can resume once independent checks pass.
6. The custodian authorizes reopening after checking dry-run outputs, portal view, search, offline issue, file retrieval, scheduled audience and outstanding external copies. Preserve a limited incident report describing exceptions and owners. No log contains the address or image.

## Disposal decisions with interacting clocks

Store retention triggers as actual events. A case closed on an unknown date cannot be disposed merely because a guessed expiry has passed. If the retention period is expressed in years, calculate the anniversary in the applicable calendar rather than assuming a fixed number of days. Policies specify treatment of leap dates, changed retention editions, reopened cases and partial records. Re-evaluate the current holds at the final disposal boundary. When two holds overlap, releasing only one cannot authorize removal.

Choose the smallest disposition compatible with the ongoing duty. A withdrawal of marketing consent may leave a limited suppression identifier necessary to prevent resubscription on the next import. An invoice correction may require a linked credit or amendment. A donor disclosure request may need redaction of another family member before release. A dismissed complaint can retain its approved disposition while a separate optional directory entry disappears. Record reasons, key references and responsible owners without creating a permanent duplicate of erased content.

If a backup cannot support individual deletion, isolate it from ordinary processing, set and monitor its expiry, and require replay of deletion obligations after restoration. Maintain a recoverable, separately restricted copy of the minimum obligations evidence beyond the oldest still-restorable backup, adjusted for active holds and approved retention. A destroyed key or backup must not strand another church's independently owned evidence. A change of storage policy needs a tested restoration exercise before adoption.

## Acceptance evidence

- Supply data, backup and obligations watermarks plus the count of unmatched obligations. Any unaccounted gap blocks release of the affected scope.
- With two active holds and one released, verify the original is preserved but all declaratively withdrawn public derivatives are unavailable.
- Re-run the withdrawal and disposal receipts twice; the same protections and counts apply, with no new release, notice or deletion of unrelated records.
- A lost offline device never acknowledges an erase command. Mark its outcome unconfirmed, expose its maximum expiry and route unresolved exposure for review. Do not record an unobserved wipe as complete.
- A disclosure package sent to a verified representative contains only fields and dates permitted by that mandate. Revocation before download makes the package unavailable. Delivery evidence distinguishes creation, retrieval and confirmed destruction.
