# Operating the shared church register

Start with the decision someone has to make. A church commissioning a new installation can walk these exercises with fictitious records and record its actual policy values alongside the example results. A numerical target in a worked example describes that exercise; the accountable church selects its own operating thresholds, approval rules, dates, languages and recovery commitments.

| Decision | Manual | Evidence that the task completed |
|---|---|---|
| May this person, representative, worker or public visitor perform this action now? | [Access decisions](access-decisions.md) | A reproducible decision for a specific record, field, scope, purpose, time and real actor. |
| Will a withdrawal or disposal survive restoring yesterday's backup? | [Privacy through restore](privacy-through-restore.md) | Obligations reconciled to a verified watermark, private derivatives unavailable and required evidence still protected. |
| What should staff do with two devices that disagree after a service? | [Offline reconciliation](offline-reconciliation.md) | Canonical identities, accountable observations, conflict queue and safe custody outcomes. |
| Can the secretary and treasurer sign off an import with duplicates and errors? | [Migration rehearsal](migration-rehearsal.md) | Counts by disposition, surviving contact restrictions and gift allocations by designation and currency. |
| How can a church app, outside provider and an MCP assistant safely act on the register? | [App and integration contracts](../../platform/church-apps-and-integrations.md) | Complete a member task, accept an inbound proposal and trace its actual actor, scope, revision and result. |
| Can a delayed or duplicated message create a second real-world effect? | [Command and event replay](command-event-replay.md) | One intended effect, original receipt, explicit remedy for late payment and reproducible replay. |
| Can members use the entire service with their language, device and method of access? | [Accessible member journeys](accessible-member-journeys.md) | Verified tasks, localized errors and receipts, measurable usability targets and an assisted route. |
| Is the optional assistant reliable enough for this audience and language? | [Assistance evaluation](assistance-evaluation.md) | Claim-level evidence, abstention results, privacy tests and independent human release decision. |
| Can independent churches hold a joint event and remove access afterward? | [Tenant sharing drill](tenant-sharing-drill.md) | Per-party evidence of permitted transfer, withdrawal, expiry, separate accounts and isolated recovery. |
| Can the duty officer safely reopen after an outage? | [Service recovery drill](service-recovery-drill.md) | Verified loss and recovery measurements and independent custodian outcomes for real transactions. |

The [worked decision cases](../../decisions/platform/README.md) provide concrete input and outcome combinations for seven of these operating exercises. The [platform specification](../../platform/README.md) supplies the logical entities and shared services. Each manual names business owners, concrete inputs, failure states and acceptance evidence. To verify a proposed change, run the nearest ordinary example and its failure/recovery path. Record the policy edition, the observed result and any discrepancy against the owning domain.
