# Run a joint event without merging church registers

Two churches can share an event without sharing all their members, finances, controls or accounts. The [organization lifecycle](../../platform/organization-lifecycle.md) owns workspace controls and exchange agreements. The [organization directory](../../foundation/organization.md) identifies congregations, campuses and affiliations. This exercise tests sources of authority, delivery receipts, correction and exit from a cooperative activity.

## Establish the parties

Pine congregation belongs to Organization West. River congregation belongs to independent Organization East. Both rent the same community hall and call it “Central Campus.” Each uses its own person 1001 and group 7. A volunteer serves both organizations using separate local accounts. A network office receives aggregate participation figures; its role carries no authority to inspect either local roster.

West hosts a joint music workshop and requires attendee display name, event assignment, a verified emergency contact route where authorized and limited instructions essential for that event. East remains custodian of its register. Both parties agree named purpose, exact fields, destination staff, event window, responsibility for emergency changes, consent or other local authority, expiry, jurisdiction/configuration, expected response to withdrawal and treatment of reports. No shared data is released until both named signatories authorize the edition.

## Map the exchange

| Data or activity | Authorized view and owner | Regression evidence |
|---|---|---|
| East person 1001 and West person 1001 | Local IDs remain distinct. Use a scoped transfer subject key for East's attendee. The host may store an event guest reference with provenance under the agreement. | An import and a support search cannot match either person by the bare number. |
| Volunteer opens East workspace while West roster is already open | Re-establish account, role, organization, filters, work queue, download permission and draft context. | An old tab or saved link for West cannot perform actions under the East token/context. |
| Shared hall on calendar | Each church can publish its permitted event view. A venue custodian sees room reservations and agreed times, subject to local restrictions. | Room identity, occupancy evidence or same street address never creates a church-to-church role grant. |
| Event authorization initially valid at 09:00 and withdrawn at 10:00 | East sends a specifically scoped correction; West acknowledges processing at the event and refreshes live views and packs as appropriate. | West's workshop administrator sees the neutral action, not East's private reason. |
| Network requests aggregate results | Each church verifies the measure, period, eligible population, deduplication basis and small-group protection. | A mutually participating person cannot be described as a unique network individual without an independently authorized deduplication method. |
| West later invites the guest to its own church | Use a separately volunteered identity and purpose, following normal intake. | A host attendance receipt cannot subscribe the guest to newsletters or establish formal membership. |

## Resolve corrections

Use the sending organization's subject key, authorized scope, event edition and receipt. A superseding correction references the original transfer, fields, effective time and affected recipient. The recipient validates current agreement and its authority to receive the correction. A roster correction that removes an obsolete telephone can still be necessary after ordinary sharing expires. Define that narrowly permitted remediation route in the agreement, with no ability to submit new persons or fields after expiry.

If the sending church knows a safety restriction is active but cannot legally disclose the underlying case, send only the operational instruction allowed by policy and refer to a named private supervisor. An acknowledged transmission means the host received the correction. A staff acknowledgement or a roster reconciliation establishes the different fact that the corrected operational disposition was applied. During a network outage, stop any action requiring live authority and use the pre-agreed supervised route.

A receiving church correcting its own private record must not overwrite the sender's register or change an independent profile with a shared phone or name. Send a discrepancy with the minimum evidence to the local custodian and accept their confirmed response or leave it unresolved. Withdraw chart numbers or recalculate aggregates only under the report's named original/restated policy.

## End of event and exit

1. Generate a scoped inventory of issued packs, printed material, pending notices, held evidence, unfinished actions, approved reporting needs and financial obligations. Staff with access to the sharing agreement do not thereby receive access to both churches' books.
2. At the agreed boundary, cease ordinary access and terminate future publication or new exports. Return or destroy issued copies under the recorded custody terms. Track unconfirmed external or offline destruction honestly. Retain required evidence under a specific class and review date.
3. Provide an aggregate report including the count of transferred records, accepted records, denied disclosures and outstanding discrepancies under agreed privacy thresholds. If denominators count participating congregations separately, publish them accordingly.
4. Exercise the recovery of West only from a backup taken during the event. East's current decisions, gift references, roster and authorized corrections stay intact. Reapply cross-church withdrawals and prevent replay of old messages. A failed partition proof blocks release of the affected scope.
5. On actual organization closure, verify exit authority and ongoing obligations. Export that organization's schema, codes, stable keys, joins, evidence digests, restrictions and approved records under a manifest. The receiving custodian reconstructs a sample person, household, payment and minutes. Keep an explicit disposition for material that cannot lawfully transfer.

## Completion evidence

Run the same input through public search, a member session, network dashboard, integration subscription, offline pack, report scheduler and restored workspace. Each surface must return the expected scoped result for both local person 1001 records. A failed test identifies the responsible owner and field class without exposing either church's data to the other. Sign-off includes the completed withdrawal, retirement of the agreement, remaining responsibilities and one successful isolated restore.
