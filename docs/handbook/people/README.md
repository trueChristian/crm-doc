# Working with people and sensitive decisions

Use these chapters when implementing an operation or rehearsing it with staff. Each begins with a specific church situation, identifies the authoritative role and ends with observable results across records and audiences. Dates, amounts, thresholds and names are fictional examples; decisions described as local policy must use the church's approved version in a real installation.

| If you need to… | Read | Work with |
|---|---|---|
| Record a one-name guest, calculate an age from incomplete information, or unwind a mistaken merge | [Intake and identity](intake-and-identity.md) | Welcome desk, registrar, identity steward |
| Represent a blended family or decide who may collect a child today | [Family authority at work](family-authority-at-work.md) | Registrar, guardian verifier, check-out supervisor |
| Certify a meeting roll and explain a late admission, transfer or difference from last year | [Certifying the roll](certifying-the-roll.md) | Secretary, authorizing body, returning officer |
| Transcribe a register and issue a limited extract without revealing adjacent families | [Register book and extract](register-book-and-extract.md) | Minister, archivist, registrar |
| Coordinate a visit, prayer request or meal while honoring exactly what may be shared | [Consent, care and handover](consent-care-and-handover.md) | Care lead, volunteer, communications editor |
| Receive a concern, assign an independent lead, disclose evidence or review a measure | [Handling a protected concern](handling-a-protected-concern.md) | Safeguarding lead, independent reviewer |
| Assess assistance, reconcile a vendor return and decide a partial-award appeal | [Aid, payment and appeal](aid-payment-and-appeal.md) | Intake worker, welfare assessor, treasurer |
| Triage wrongdoing and retaliation, resolve a grievance or remit part of a decision | [Independent review and remedy](independent-review-and-remedy.md) | Independent custodian, investigator, adjudicator |
| Reconcile people during an activation, match later recoveries or verify a preventive action | [Activation, accountability and claims](activation-accounting-and-claims.md) | Continuity coordinator, insurance officer, trustee |

The [people specifications](../../people/README.md) and [assurance specifications](../../assurance/README.md) define the records behind these procedures. Use the [shared record conventions](../../foundation/record-conventions.md) for time, amounts, permissions, versions and evidence. A family tie, consent to a message, personal account, membership period and a delegated action each follows its own decision. Read the corresponding chapter before using one fact to decide a different question.
