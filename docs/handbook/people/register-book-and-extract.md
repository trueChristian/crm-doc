# From a handwritten register to an authorized extract

An archivist receives a volume containing faded entries, changed names and incomplete dates. Years later someone requests an extract of a baptism. The office must be able to trace the exact source and evidence behind the extract, distinguish a local rite from an account received elsewhere, and avoid exposing another family's details from the same page. Use [Rite registers](../../people/rites-and-milestones.md) and [Archives](../../administration/archives.md).

## Accession and transcription

1. Inventory the volume as received. Give it a stable reference, originating church and custodian. Record its physical or digital condition, range of dates as stated, missing pages, numbering scheme, transfer authority, restrictions and acceptance date. Preserve the source object separately from its searchable transcription.
2. Capture the source locator for each transcription: volume, page or folio, original entry number where present and location within the page. A separate current series can provide a unique logical register reference. Preserve a historic duplicate or gap as source evidence; never alter the written number merely to satisfy a modern sequencing rule.
3. Transcribe wording and scripts as witnessed. Label indistinct, omitted and supplied values. A pencil annotation, later marginal note and first written entry can have distinct authors or unknown origins. Distinguish the original record, literal transcription and searchable normalized spelling. Retain a confidence/verification decision with responsible reviewer whenever normalization changes meaning.
4. Check the ceremony definition, principals, witness roles and date precision. An outside officiant, marriage witness, sponsor or historic guardian can be recorded by name without opening a current member profile. Link a principal to a person only after review when the evidence supports it.
5. Review a sample of low-risk fields and require a second authorized review for uncertain identity, contested corrections, transferred or otherwise restricted material as local policy requires. Publish permitted search fields and restrict the source image; a redacted extract may be the only suitable output.

| Uncertainty | What the registrar saves | What may be said on an extract |
|---|---|---|
| Date says “May 1941” | Month and year; no manufactured day | State month and year with the source reference if permitted. |
| Source reads “Jon” and an applicant now uses “John” | Preserve recorded name. Link current name only if authority and evidence support it. | Use recorded wording; a separately approved explanatory note can identify the current name. |
| Page is missing between 17 and 19 | Record a physical gap and review owner | Report inability to confirm from the surviving record. Never issue a negative certificate claiming no event took place. |
| Two children of the same name appear in neighboring homes | Keep distinct source entries and unresolved identity links | Disclose no family hints while asking the requester for independent particulars via a safe route. |
| The rite was performed by another church | Preserve origin and provenance of the received account | Identify local custody of the attestation; do not certify the event as conducted locally. |

## A request and correction across three dates

On 8 April Sami requests a copy covering a grandparent. Record who requested it, their return route, stated purpose, the authorized decision maker and limits for disclosure. Kinship asserted in a message does not by itself authorize the full page. The reviewer obtains the approved authorization or authorizes a narrower extract under local archive policy. An unresolved application receives a neutral status; it must not reveal another person's name, standing or confidential family circumstance.

On 11 April the registrar locates the entry, verifies the principal, and issues an extract containing the approved fields, original book locator, issue number, issuer, time, template revision and qualification about the month-only date. A scan also shows the unrelated family in the next entry. The office exports the approved extract rather than attaching the whole page. If a second identical copy of the same issued edition is delivered, record that delivery; a changed certified wording requires a replacement issue.

On 16 April a better preserved copy establishes a transcription error. An independent reviewer compares the images, appends the correction referencing the original entry, records the old/new reading and why the new source was judged better, and authorizes a replacement extract. Preserve both issues and indicate the superseded status of the earlier one during authorized verification. The book's original sequence, preserved source image, previously issued wording and actual issue dates stay recoverable. Where the underlying source itself is disputed, record the dispute; correcting a transcription cannot determine a contested historic fact.

## Reconciliation tests

A page with two baptisms and five visible participants produces two counted local baptisms, not five principals or five new members. A batch with 47 accepted entries and three unresolved entries yields 47 durable successes and a review list of three; a retry cannot renumber the accepted entries. Before archival transfer reconcile total entries, voids, amendments, attached media, withheld items, rights/holds and the receiving custodian's acknowledgment. A later search by an authorized former name must not make that name publicly discoverable.
