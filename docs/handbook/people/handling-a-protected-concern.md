# Receive, route and review a protected concern

Anyone should be able to contact an appointed safeguarding recipient without a church login or approval from the person named in the concern. The local policy edition identifies the human lead, independent alternate, urgent response instructions, external referral authorities, required timelines, communication restrictions and retention rules. The procedure below describes the product decisions needed to administer that policy. See [Safeguarding records](../../people/safeguarding-and-disclosure.md).

## The first account

A youth volunteer receives a disclosure after an activity. Staff can record what was said in the speaker's own words, separately label direct observations, note whether someone else supplied information, and distinguish incident time, receipt time and later recording time. Permit unknown or approximate details. Preserve the original account and later signed addenda. The workflow must not require a clinical conclusion, a determination of blame, a full identity or corroborating evidence before accepting the concern.

The intake screen asks whether the proposed reply route is safe. The reporter may decline an email receipt, identify a conflict, or use an independent route. Do not claim absolute secrecy; communicate the applicable human-controlled sharing process. An urgent situation uses the locally approved immediate response instructions alongside the case intake. The software records actions and acknowledgments; it cannot substitute for emergency services or a trained human lead.

## Decision and minimum disclosure

| Event | Decision maker and control | Information passed outward |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary recipient is implicated | Independent designated recipient verifies conflict and assigns a non-conflicted lead; withhold normal recipient notification. | Neutral receipt via approved safe route. No title, person or allegation in general office queues. |
| Lead receives a third-party allegation | Lead records the original account, its source, uncertainties and basis for any referral. | Only the fields approved for the receiving authority and purpose. |
| Temporary activity restriction needed | Authorized person records subject, action, scope, effect, evidence authority, start, review date and operational recipient. | Current neutral allow/refer/deny decision at use, never allegation narrative. |
| Referred externally | Approver verifies recipient identity, agency scope, approved basis and safe delivery. Human acknowledgment tracked separately from delivery. | Selected reviewed extract and custody reference; no default bulk case export. |
| Guardian requests details | Lead determines authority, other parties' privacy and safety under applicable policy. | A reviewed reply through a verified safe channel; family status alone cannot release evidence. |
| Retention review arrives | Authorized custodian checks hold, applicable schedule, linked investigations and approved disposition. | Operational services receive only updated restriction status when relevant. |

For every substantive sharing decision, record the requested purpose, authority, recipient, actual fields or edition, authorizer, time, transfer method and acknowledgment; where information is withheld, record the reason and whether an alternate means of response was offered. An exceptional access decision has a bounded purpose and duration, detailed read audit and independent retrospective review. General system administration and board office alone confer no routine read access.

## Six dated events

On 3 November at 16:20 a visitor submits a concern through an independent intake route. The usual recipient is involved. The independent lead acknowledges personal responsibility at 16:46. The initial automatic receipt at 16:21 is not treated as case acceptance. If no human acknowledgment arrives by the locally configured deadline, alert the alternate without including the case subject.

On 4 November the lead records the report, one direct observation and a separate disputed account. Each entry retains its source and recording time. An external reviewer requests a photograph and the whole attendance roster. The authorized lead identifies the minimum material and approves a redacted extract as applicable; preserve the original protected evidence, approved derivative, custody transfers and delivery acknowledgment. People absent from the extract stay absent from filenames, thumbnails and metadata.

On 5 November a scoped activity restriction is approved and synchronized to the service responsible for release decisions. On 6 November a volunteer checks the activity roster; they see only a neutral instruction. A disallowed lookup from the general register returns the same kind of result as it would for someone without a case, avoiding a case-existence disclosure.

On 12 November an account contains a corrected date. Append the signed amendment and its source, retain the original, and ask the human owner whether a previously authorized referral must be updated. On 18 November a lead closes the internal review but the external referral has no acknowledgment and a protective measure is due for review. Record outstanding owner, deadlines and independent disposition; a closed summary cannot silently revoke the measure or represent external acknowledgment.

## Verification at the boundaries

Revoke access from the departing caseworker and repeat direct URL, export, search, calendar and notification checks. An authorized successor retains the chronology and must acknowledge the handover. Reproduce one past release decision against the authorities in force at that time. A correction today does not erase the action staff actually took then. A board dashboard of small case populations can say that a protected measure is monitored or suppress the cell according to policy; it must not permit identifying inference through repeated filters. Any assistive drafting tool requires permission for this data class, human verification and the same disclosure boundary; credibility or reportability never comes from an automated score.
