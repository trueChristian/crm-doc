# An independent review from first disclosure to remedy

A volunteer responsible for the hall reports that maintenance requests were suppressed. She also believes her service assignments were removed after she complained. One submission describes two issues, several potential owners and a conflict involving the usual intake officer. The church needs evidence-based decisions, confidentiality, an independent appeal and a means of checking that promised remedies actually happened. See [Complaints and appeals](../../assurance/complaints-and-grievances.md) and [Incident review](../../assurance/incidents-and-near-misses.md).

## Route the substance separately

| Information in the submission | Destination and decision owner | Minimum shared information |
|---|---|---|
| The entrance has remained unsafe | Facilities and incident coordinator | Condition, location, timing and work request. Share witness identity only if justified and authorized. |
| Earlier reports were suppressed | Independent wrongdoing docket | Reporter-approved safe route, original account, evidence and confirmed authority to investigate. |
| A supervisor allegedly removed assignments after the report | Independently restricted retaliation docket and appropriate workforce/volunteer owner | Relevant assignment change and decision under review. The supervisor cannot oversee intake or appeal. |
| A separate fact suggests a child may be at risk | Designated safeguarding lead via protected handoff | Minimum referral determined under applicable policy and confirmation of ownership. Avoid repeating sensitive questioning for the general docket. |
| A financial loss may be covered | Insurance officer | Asset, occurrence date, value or estimated exposure and filing deadline. No automatic release of witness statements or attendance lists. |

The intake custodian gives a safe receipt and a confidential means of exchanging further information. An anonymous person may retain a private reply credential. A tracking number printed on a public board cannot grant access. If the reporter selects “do not telephone,” a roster worker cannot override that choice by calling a household number. A conflicted manager, ordinary administrator or board member must not see that a specific person reported, even through dashboard counts, notification previews or a calendar title.

## Set scope and collect the record

On 5 February the independent recipient acknowledges responsibility. Staff preserve two statements and a maintenance ticket without editing their original wording. On 6 February a reviewer checks conflicts, documents the ordinary investigator's recusal and appoints someone outside the contested decision. Scope each issue in neutral terms, record which questions are referred elsewhere, the authority under which evidence is gathered, due dates, the opportunity to respond and available representation. An assignment disappears from a rota; record that fact separately from an allegation about the reason.

On 12 February two additional witnesses report the same physical defect. Preserve separate reports, then refer them to one incident occurrence. Their count proves neither motive nor culpability. A reviewer evaluates each issue and records observations, evidence, uncertainties and disagreements. A respondent receives a reviewed account sufficient for the applicable procedure; third-party identities are protected according to authorized disclosure. A new, material allegation follows a recorded scope decision, with suitable opportunity to respond.

## Issue decisions and deal with an appeal

On 20 February the reviewer accepts that a repair was delayed and finds the evidence about retaliation indeterminate. Keep the two findings distinct. The facilities remedy assigns an owner, completion evidence and a verification date. Communicate a version of the decision appropriate for the reporter and another appropriate for the respondent. Trustees receive necessary aggregate oversight. If volunteers need rota amendments, the authorized scheduling owner implements them independently. Docket closure does not directly alter pay, clergy standing or membership.

On 24 February the volunteer appeals the scope of the second issue and supplies a dated message. Check timeliness using the published locally configured calendar and the decision's safe notification date. An extension or late submission receives an explicit decision; expiry must not silently erase the appeal. An uninvolved reviewer with authority may affirm, vary or remit specific issues. Here, the reviewer remits retaliation for further evidence. The uncontested repair finding remains final under the example policy unless the appeal authority expressly orders otherwise. Retain the original decisions and amended editions.

On 14 March an independent reviewer confirms the repair and documents verification. On 21 March a separate reviewer completes the remitted issue. Notify only entitled parties, record the implementation owner and a safe follow-up date, and reevaluate any outstanding temporary measures. If retaliation is first reported after the original resolution, open a separately controlled review and link it without exposing the original reporter to the implicated chain.

## Acceptance walkthrough

A reviewer discovers that the new investigator is a witness. Remove their case access, retain an access trail, appoint an independent alternate and identify any decisions requiring fresh review. An anonymous complainant loses their reply credential: a public lookup must not reveal the case, while the independent recipient can continue the investigation as authorized. A person withdraws their service complaint while a policy-mandated referral remains outstanding: close or limit the withdrawn issue and show the other owner's obligation separately. A board pack prepared for a department of two people suppresses inferential cross-filters, even when the underlying issue count can be individually authorized for a different reader.
