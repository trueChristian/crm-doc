# Certify a roll and explain every change

At the annual meeting the secretary needs a certified list of eligible voters and an annual membership return. The two lists can use different classes and dates. Keep the approved policy version, congregation, reference date, knowledge cutoff and certifier with each edition. See [Membership and certified rolls](../../people/membership-and-rolls.md) and [Annual returns](../../administration/annual-returns.md).

## Six decisions before producing the list

| Decision | Secretary records | What goes wrong if omitted |
|---|---|---|
| Counted unit and scope | Individual formal members of this congregation, defined classes, campus aggregation and primary-affiliation rule | Household relatives, regular guests or members counted twice across campuses can inflate the total. |
| Reference date | Civil date for effective standing, with inclusive start and exclusive end | Someone whose membership ended on 1 July incorrectly appears on the 1 July roll. |
| Knowledge cutoff | Instant until which decisions had been entered and approved | A backdated correction silently changes the edition used by a past meeting. |
| Voting rule | Qualifying standing/classes, effective dates, verified eligibility and any exception authority | An official membership count is mistaken for the voting population. |
| Identity treatment | Merge resolution, unresolved disputes and exception list owner | Duplicate names are either double-counted or silently collapsed. |
| Distribution | Audience, allowed columns, approved print/download and expiry arrangements | A voting list discloses confidential reason codes, home addresses or private notes. |

Receive applications against an individual, including someone already in a member household. Check whether a qualifying admission exists before opening another. A minister may recommend admission, a committee decide it and a clerk enter it; store the distinct actors and dates when policy requires these roles. Approval writes the decision and first period once. Simultaneous approvals must see one admission. While an application is under review the applicant remains a guest or regular attender as applicable.

## Worked reconciliation

The 1 January opening formal roll contains 80 people. Over the six-month period, the clerk records four admissions, two acknowledged external transfers in and one restoration of someone outside the opening count. Three people resign, one transfers out and two die. A standing change moves one additional person from a counted to an uncounted class. No boundary changes or prior-period adjustments apply. The closing count is 80 + 4 + 2 + 1 − 3 − 1 − 2 − 1 = 80. Explain each movement by a dated decision; “no net change” conceals fourteen movements. A move between campuses inside this congregation can appear as a campus movement with zero effect on the congregation total.

A visitor attends 19 services and stays with two members; the visitor appears in attendance reporting and possibly a directory edition, but contributes zero to this reconciliation. For a person affiliated to two congregations, local rolls can each include the person if local policy permits it. A federation-wide unique-person total must declare its deduplication or primary-affiliation rule rather than adding local totals blindly.

## Late knowledge and transfer acknowledgment

On 24 June trustees approve admission for Nia effective 25 June. The public meeting's list is certified on 28 June with the information then entered. The clerk records the approved decision on 2 July. A query for effective membership on 30 June using 2 July knowledge includes Nia; a reproduction of the original certified edition retains its original rows. A revised edition, if authorized, receives a new version with reference to the superseded edition. The actual meeting and its recorded voting result always identify the edition used. Display separate “as issued” and “restated” movements and show the approver of a revision.

On 5 July Nia asks for transfer to another church. The clerk verifies purpose and disclosure scope; the outgoing package includes only the particulars authorized for a transfer. On 8 July the church issues the letter. The destination church's receipt on 12 July acknowledges delivery, while its acceptance decision might occur on 20 July. Transition at the locally authorized effective decision(s), not at the moment an email was opened. If no acknowledgment arrives, remind the responsible clerks, then review without blindly ending membership. If a cancellation arrived first, a late callback goes to reconciliation and never completes the transfer automatically.

## Closing the review

Before certification, run an exclusive-period overlap check, duplicate-person check, unapproved-decision check, roll-number uniqueness check, opening-to-closing reconciliation, issued-letter exceptions and print field review. Unknown eligibility produces a named exception for an authorized reviewer; the chair may receive a count of unresolved cases, not confidential reasons. Approve an immutable edition, record who received it, and set a recheck when new evidence arrives. A clerk correcting an erroneous death verifies the change, restores eligible future statuses through authorized workflows and reviews any messages or duties already cancelled separately.
