# Registering a person when the details are uncertain

The secretary receives one visitor card for “Eli” and an import with two people using the same telephone number. The desired outcome is a reachable person when follow-up was requested, a date that says only what is known and an identity decision another steward can explain. Use the [personal register](../../people/personal-register.md) and [identity operations](../../platform/migration-data-quality.md).

## At the desk

1. Ask what the person wants recorded or done. For headcount alone, increment the appropriate occurrence count. For a named visitor card, record the supplied name, source, date, congregation and requested purpose. Do not require a surname or a birthday just to request a call.
2. Search within the clerk's permitted scope before creating a person. Show the least detail needed to distinguish candidates. A shared surname and mobile number can describe two relatives. A confidential former name cannot appear as a suggestion, match explanation or increased match count to a volunteer.
3. Accept “around 2012,” “September 2012,” “3 September,” “3 September 2012” and “I prefer not to say” without silently converting them into the same precision. Where a decision depends on disclosure status, record withheld versus unknown in the permitted evidence; do not add personal history to a wide-access note.
4. Ask for separate channel/purpose permission if the person requested a response. A contact confirmed as reachable can still be unsafe for a particular message. A person can provide correcting information through a clerk and retain a profile with no login.
5. Before verifying account ownership, check independent evidence. Bind the account link and subsequent personal record to the verified person. A sibling accepting a household email invitation must not gain access to the other sibling's record.

## Date precision at a cut-off

For a bounded, non-estimated date, derive the earliest and latest possible valid civil birthdays. Youngest possible age is completed years from the latest birthday to the cut-off; oldest uses the earliest birthday. If the cut-off predates the possible birth, show inapplicable/unknown as appropriate, not a negative age. Exact birthday without a year supports an anniversary, never an age calculation. “Around 2012” without a stated uncertainty interval cannot sustain a reliable upper/lower eligibility bound.

| Entered fact | As of 1 September 2026 | Outcome for a minimum age of 14 on that date |
|---|---|---|
| 31 August 2012 | Exactly 14 | Meets the age criterion if the recording standard is sufficient. Other entry conditions still apply. |
| 30 September 2012 | Exactly 13 | Does not meet on 1 September; re-evaluate at the actual later event date if relevant. |
| September 2012 | 13–14 | Request verification or authorized exception. On 1 October all September birthdays have occurred. |
| 2012, exact year only | 13–14 | No automatic approval; the cut-off straddles the range. |
| 3 September, year omitted | Unknown age | May show anniversary only to an audience allowed to see it. |
| Born 29 February 2012 | Leap-year civil date | For a non-leap-year threshold, select the church's applicable configured rule and show its version. |

## A disputed duplicate across two Sundays

On 4 October the office imports Eli's card under a durable source key. Re-import on 5 October returns the first intake result. On 11 October a steward notices an existing “E. M.” with a shared telephone, same birth year, a different name spelling and a protected household. Display the ordinary clerk only a request for authorized review. A data steward can compare eligible claims; the protected-domain owner may return a conflict or clearance without sharing the confidential address. “Distinct” closes the candidate pair in either order and permits a later evidence-based reopening.

On 13 October independent evidence establishes that two transposed versions of *one other* visitor's name belong to one person. Preview selection of survivor, field-by-field contact authority, overlapping membership periods, outstanding invitations, assigned tasks, finance references and restricted evidence responses. A reviewer cannot copy a suppressed address onto the public record or replace a refusal with an allow. Competing active accounts stop automatic relinking. Ask the identity owner to resolve the claim. Preserve immutable earlier names on certificates and statements.

On 15 October the steward discovers a mistaken merge on a different pair. Freeze disputed privileged access, retain a single correction case and obtain a compensating merge decision referencing the original. Restore pre-merge references to each person when provenance determines ownership; route later entries with ambiguous ownership to their own authorized domain. A certificate issued after the mistake is corrected through the registrar's issuance process. A payment posted under the mistaken identity is reviewed by finance. Replaying the correction cannot move either artifact twice.

## Completion checks

Run a same-name search as a welcome worker and a steward; compare only authorized differences. Verify that shared guardians, phone numbers, birthdays and household membership alone never establish an identity. Rehearse a merge with a finance acknowledgment followed by a timeout: the retry completes pending acknowledgments and preserves one ledger reference. Confirm that “appears on a guest list,” “qualifies by age,” “may contact by telephone,” and “may sign in” can each have different answers for the same person.
