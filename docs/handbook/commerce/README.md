# Church resource and publication desks

| Operator question | Procedure | Compare afterward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May a family attend after a concession, delayed fee response or cancellation? | [Church resource and external payment handoffs](../../commerce/desk-handoffs.md) | Places held, waived or confirmed; one surviving ticket per confirmed place; adjustment separately identified |
| Can a site visitor find a private prayer after a replay is edited or withdrawn? | [Release and withdraw media](publication-and-withdrawal.md) | Exact revision, cleared uses, live and archive permissions, channel removal acknowledgments |
| Who holds the hall key, the borrowed copy or undelivered relief packs? | [Facilities, lending and outreach rehearsals](../../acceptance/commerce/README.md) | Physical custody, witnessed condition and next accountable desk |

Assign a named coordinator to each outstanding exception. A church volunteer can resolve a physical quantity or access problem within their role. Authority to approve a public media release, disclose a donor’s identity, waive an event fee or confirm a provider adjustment comes from the applicable separate permission. When several jobs belong to the same person, preserve the action and capacity in which they acted and route any required independent review.
