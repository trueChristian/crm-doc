# Settle licensed media proceeds with a rights holder

An outside speaker permits paid distribution of an approved audio course under an agreement. The bookstore can charge purchasers and issue grants, but its sales report cannot also act as the rights-holder settlement statement. The rights reviewer determines which formats, territories, periods and commercial uses are authorized. The licensing officer applies the agreed earnings formula. Finance approves vendor, balances, payment and adjustments. A rights holder can receive the amounts they earned without seeing who purchased, attended church or played the course.

## Freeze the agreement before the first paid use

Record the contracting church and the verified holder or authorized representative, the exact MediaRights evidence and edition or approved release scope, effective/expiry instants, territorial and audience scope, allowed uses, currencies and approved translation policy. Specify whether the counterparty earns per licensed sale, paid renewal, included bundle component, authorized rental, verified usage or a fixed guarantee. Capture the calculation base, exclusions, tier order, refunds, timing, minimum guarantee, recoupable advance, payment threshold and due dates. A download and stream of the same purchase cannot earn twice unless the agreement explicitly pays for two independent uses. The rights evidence provides authorization, while the contract and posting approvals govern settlement.

For sales-based agreements, freeze the source OrderLine, bundle allocation basis, accepted discount, tax, currency, granted use, issue or fulfilment evidence and posting cutoff. For usage-based agreements, define the specific qualifying observation and a minimal expiry-limited correlation key that prevents repeated playback retries from creating royalty claims. A payer's ability to view an invoice never authorizes access to a viewer's history. Exclude activity unsupported by an authorized event instead of guessing from popularity statistics.

## Settlement with a cumulative adjustment

A licensed study originally sells eight units at 49.90 each. Each received a fixed discount of 4.90. Two units are refunded in full during the same open period. Taxes, gifts and processing fees are excluded under the signed terms. Royalty rate is 12.5% of qualifying net sale proceeds. There is an outstanding recoupable advance of 20.00. Amounts below use one two-decimal currency, with half-up rounding once per cumulative statement.

| Calculation | Amount | What it represents |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Eight gross sales | 399.20 | 8 × 49.90 |
| Discounts allocated to those eight units | 39.20 | 8 × 4.90 |
| Two units fully refunded at net selling price | 90.00 | 2 × 45.00 |
| Qualifying net | 270.00 | Six retained units at 45.00 |
| Accrued royalty | 33.75 | 12.5% × 270.00 |
| Advance applied | 20.00 | Remaining prepaid balance becomes zero |
| Amount payable now | 13.75 | 33.75 earned less advance applied |

If the previously issued advance was an authorized recoverable asset, an illustrative recognition entry debits royalty expense 33.75 and credits the advance asset 20.00 and royalties payable 13.75. Paying the balance debits payable 13.75 and credits bank 13.75. The earlier payment of the 20.00 advance has its own proof. A nonrecoupable guarantee follows the independently approved accounting policy.

After the statement has been paid, a further unit is returned. The cumulative qualifying consideration becomes 225.00 and the recalculated cumulative royalty becomes 28.13 (28.125 rounded half up). The correction is 5.62, the difference from the previous rounded 33.75, even though rounding the single unit's royalty in isolation would produce 5.63. Do not alter the prior statement. Create a dated change referring to the actual returned unit, its original rights scope and the settled statement, and route the 5.62 to authorized recovery, future offset or write-off under the agreement. If the rights holder is never responsible for a post-settlement refund, retain the customer refund as a sale adjustment and record why there is zero licensor recovery.

## Owners and evidence

| Boundary | Required fields | Failure response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rights reviewer → channel owner | Approved use, exact edition/release, territory/audience, effective interval, agreement revision and payee authority | Block or pause the affected sale if permission is unknown. A past royalty settlement never reauthorizes publication. |
| Commerce → licensing officer | Sale unit identity, accepted gross/discount/net, eligible use and grant, return/chargeback disposition, source currency/precision, period and related correction | Ambiguous chargeback with no identified unit enters investigation. Never remove the same refund in both the closed original and adjustment statement. |
| Licensing officer → finance | SupplierAccount, period, contract/currency/edition scope, source line set, inclusive/exclusive tax treatment, earned amount, advance opening/applied/closing, approved payable/credit, reviewed evidence | Hold discrepancies. No cash movement occurs on circulation of a draft statement. |
| Finance → rights holder | Approved reference, safe title if authorized, licence period, permitted aggregate units, allowed deductions, advance and net remittance, currency/rate, dispute deadline | A dispute reopens review. Share itemized evidence only under authorized scope. Keep household, individual purchase timing and playback details out of the cover document. |
| Finance → review team | Settlement reference, confirmed payment amount/date, bank reconciliation and any blocked balance | Mark settled only for the amount actually paid or contractually offset. A sub-threshold payable can carry forward with statement-level opening/closing balances. |

## Acceptance

An order includes an 8.00 gift and a separate paid licence. The gift never enters the royalty basis. A publisher removes the edition halfway through a term: new use is stopped, past permitted earnings remain assessable, affected paid promises enter customer-remedy review, and payout history stays intact. A royalty agreement covering only audio cannot generate earnings on an unrelated printed workbook or authorize translation. A bundle's nominal standalone price cannot override the sale allocation frozen at checkout.

Two agents prepare the same month-end statement concurrently. One approved period/scope/revision and source set can settle only once. A late source correction creates an amendment and never causes the first payable to be paid again. If the licensing officer records rights holder A but the bank mandate names B, halt transfer and refer for independent payee verification.
