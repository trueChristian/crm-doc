# Administer a conditional grant

## Terms before fundraising

A foundation invites a proposal for a food programme. The church requests 12,000 and proposes a 3,000 church match. The sponsor offers a maximum award of 8,000, financing 80% of eligible verified project cost, with the church financing the remaining 20%. The agreement permits a 4,000 initial cash advance, recoverable if eligibility conditions are unmet. Finance approves a conditional-advance model for this fixture. Eligible documented costs and sponsor acceptance create income under the agreed terms. The award has a reporting deadline, a separate cost-incurrence window and a separate claim-submission deadline. Enter the actual dates and their consequences as different fields or milestones.

The grant coordinator and authorized signatory review the reduced scope, amended matching budget and programme feasibility before accepting. At the 8,000 ceiling the programme needs 10,000 of eligible cost and 2,000 of matching finance. The original proposed 3,000 is retained in the proposal edition. It does not automatically remain a separate payable or externally restricted balance.

A cost may be eligible but partly contributed by the church. The 2,000 church-funded total above comprises 1,050 agreed eligible match and 950 excluded costs. The public statement “all our expenses were eligible” would be wrong. If the sponsor instead approves an offset against a later draw, link the offset and pay no duplicate refund. Estimates and actuals, original and corrected reports, and receipt date versus recognition date remain visible.

## Close, reopen and test

Close the application after acceptance, the narrative report when the sponsor meets its acceptance conditions, and the financial award only after outstanding advances, costs, receivables, restriction releases, disallowances, refunds and audits are settled. A sponsor may permit retention of equipment but prohibit sale for a further year. Preserve that obligation beyond financial close and link the disposition approval. A right to use participant data may end even while accounting evidence is retained.

Test split costs with one underlying 600 eligible invoice claimed 400 here and 250 elsewhere. Only a combined 600 can consume the eligible base absent a legitimate distinct cost allocation. Test an award extension agreed before period end versus an unsigned email suggesting one. Only the documented and authorized edition can authorize new costs after the old deadline. Test withdrawing a photograph included in a submitted pack: remove it from future releases or issue a corrected evidence pack under agreement, retain the restricted audit trail, and leave aggregate cost recognition intact where eligibility is unchanged.

When a closed sponsor report is reopened, retain the dated original 4,400 earned figure and issue a corrected 4,200 edition. Board users can see an aggregate 200 adjustment and its period. Only assigned grant, finance and privacy workers can access the disputed beneficiary or supplier documentation. Replaying the 200 correction changes no balance.
