# Classify support and recognition

A church raises money for a training project through a dinner sponsor, a giving foundation and a matching employer. The development lead wants a public update. The treasurer must prove it from actual receipts. The project lead needs the usable project amount. The three inquiries use the same underlying transactions with different groupings and intentionally different totals.

## Trace the evening

Assume one currency, no tax or fees in this fixture, an approved benefit valuation, and all stated gifts eligible for the project. The project accepts designated gifts for training. Donors' agreed terms govern any surplus. The appeal public goal counts verified net cash contributions allocated to this project, excluding trading income and noncash goods.

| Event | Treasury cash | Finance classification | Appeal cash goal |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Sponsor pays 1,200 under an agreement exchanging promotion valued at 300 plus a documented eligible gift | 1,200 | 300 exchange consideration and 900 contribution under approved treatment. Defer the exchange portion if benefits are outstanding. | 900 |
| A foundation remits 500 on advice of two individuals | 500 | One gift 500 from the verified actual GivingParty. Advisors' permitted recognition records carry zero additional actual cash. | 500 |
| Employer promises one-half of eligible third-party project gifts up to 250. The sponsor's exchange and gift are excluded by its agreement. The foundation's 500 qualifies. | 0 | Matching position 250 earned under agreement, 0 received. Recognition or receivable accounting is decided separately by finance. | 0 |
| Employer remits an identified 180 instalment | 180 | One actual employer gift 180 with match linkage. Remaining eligible unpaid promise 70. | 180 |
| Program receives 60 workbooks valued at 2 each | 0 | A separately accepted and valued in-kind contribution of 120 under local policy. | 0 on the cash-only goal |
| Church pays 90 supper costs | −90 | Expense 90 with event and programme dimensions. | No reduction to the explicitly gross contribution goal. |

Gross cash collected is 1,880. Cash contributions are 1,580; exchange receipts are 300. After paying the dinner cost, net cash movement from these events is 1,790. Project cash contribution goal progress is 1,580. The separate in-kind contribution is 120, and 70 remains to be collected under the match. A public statement of 1,880 in donations would misclassify the exchange benefit. Adding 250 earned match to 180 actually collected would count part of the same promise twice. Aggregate the split finance allocations, not all influence records joined to them.

| Appeal manager | FundraisingAppeal, linked finance GivingCampaign, AppealChannel, source code, separately qualified goal, AppealAllocationCredit with exact GiftAllocation amount | One finance allocation can contribute only up to its eligible amount across reporting portions. Preserve donor designation when a campaign code disagrees. |
| Match coordinator | MatchOffer and finance MatchAgreement, rate, cap, cutoff, excluded remitters, qualifying allocations, evidence, amounts actually settled | Each MatchQualification links its authoritative finance match attribution, source allocation and sponsor contribution where received. Advance, earned and paid remain distinguishable. |
| Donor steward | Actual GivingParty, GiftAttribution, documented DevelopmentCreditInstruction, permitted recognition name/amount visibility, audience and effective dates | Source gifts drive official statements. Soft recognition cannot generate an additional receipt, confer membership or expose who advised a foundation. |

| Publisher | Dated AppealSnapshot, supporting fund totals, approved anonymization thresholds, source cutoff and publication approval | Once released, retain the exact snapshot. Corrections become a dated replacement with reason. |

## A return after the announcement

The foundation later receives a 100 authorized return. Its eligible source contribution falls from 500 to 400. At 50% match this supports 200. Since the employer has paid 180, expected outstanding falls from 70 to 20 under the example agreement. Current cash contributions become 1,480. The previous published 1,580 snapshot remains dated and reproducible. If the employer had already paid the full 250, 50 would exceed recomputed eligibility. Open a sponsor decision for permitted redirection, return or documented waiver. Never promise the 50 to a new project before the sponsor and finance authorize it.

## Rights and operational outcomes

An adviser wants their name in a private supporter report and refuses publication on the church website. Provide authorized label in that single release. Do not print the foundation's giving amount beside their name or issue a receipt to the adviser. If the foundation requires confidentiality, the absence of the sponsor or adviser's name in one audience does not disclose their identity through totals in another. Apply disclosure thresholds for a small cohort, particularly when a single gift could be inferred from exact amounts.

If only one of two advertising placements is delivered, BenefitDelivery shows the shortfall and an approved replacement or adjustment. Finance reviews any required credit or revenue timing independently. A paid placement does not permit sharing a dinner guest list. Any offered lead sharing needs its own valid scope and participant permissions.

| Acceptance probe | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| One transfer arrives with one remitter and three recognition names | One verified receipt, three permitted recognition relationships at most, no multiplicative joins in the funds-raised measure. |
| A salesperson moves a sponsor's logo after its licence expires | Publication is held and a new placement or renewed consent is required. Original delivery evidence remains intact. |
| Two qualifying gifts simultaneously exhaust a match cap | Approved ordering/allocation policy distributes only the remaining cap. Replaying them leaves the allocation unchanged. |
| A staff member attempts to select bereaved families or assistance recipients for a sponsorship ask | The sensitive basis is unavailable for solicitation. The authorized relationship steward can record only permitted purpose and contact eligibility. |
| A project photo is withdrawn after the dinner | Amend controlled publications and notify any permitted partner recipients, track takedown acknowledgements, preserve substantiated aggregate outcomes independently. |
