# Realize legacy and in-kind support

## A confidential intention becomes a distribution

A supporter privately tells a legacy officer that they hope to leave 80,000 to church education. They ask for no contact with their family and decline public recognition. Record a LegacyNotice with the minimum agreed description, stage, responsible officer and next permitted review. Under this example, the nonbinding intention is memorandum only. Finance uses a linked PlannedGift if an authorized review requires a financial planning record. It does not post a gift, pledge receivable, donor statement, unrestricted cash or publicly reported appeal progress from the initial conversation.

Later, the authorized estate representative supplies evidence of a 60,000 distribution for education. The reviewer verifies authority, applicable restriction, remitter identity, consent limits and whether the church can honor the terms. Record the accepted instrument edition, finance FundRestriction where applicable and allowed cash handling. On actual receipt under the illustrated policy, debit bank 60,000 and credit contribution income 60,000 in the appropriate restricted dimension. If recognition took place earlier due to different independently approved evidence, receipt settles the receivable instead. Never post both a new contribution and settlement for the same distribution.

| Measure | At intention | Upon verified actual receipt under the fixture |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Confidential historical expression of intent | 80,000 | Prior estimate of 80,000 remains restricted to authorized history |
| Currently unfulfilled legacy forecast | 80,000 as estimate if policy permits, with confidence disclosed | 0 if closing evidence confirms this is final |
| Actual gift received | 0 | 60,000 once |
| Unrestricted amount available simply because cash entered bank | 0 | 0 for this restricted purpose |
| Public named attribution | None | None without affirmative authority |

If only 40,000 arrives initially and another distribution is possible, record only 40,000 actual. Keep pending administration as an unresolved scope with evidence and no automatic promise of the difference. A different household member cannot access the record simply by logging in or sharing the same surname. A memorial notification, family contact and named annual report acknowledgement each require the appropriate instruction and authority.

## Donated books and their later sale

Under an authorized policy the church accepts 100 new books, with a documented carrying valuation of 4.00 each when ownership transfers. The donor permits 60 books for free distribution and 40 to sell for the programme. The configured terms require tracking these intended quantities separately. Acceptance, receipt, valuation, testing for sale/distribution restrictions and physical ownership each need evidence before there is a saleable or recognized balance.

| Event | Quantity and custody | Financial effect under this fixture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accept and receive confirmed delivery | 60 for distribution, 40 sellable. Each lot or restriction segment carries line and origin evidence. | Debit inventory 400, credit in-kind contributions 400. Donor acknowledgement describes the goods per approved wording. It cannot promise the church's valuation is the donor's tax value. |
| Distribute 50 to the programme | Distribution stock remaining 10. Commerce saleable quantity remains 40. | Debit programme supplies expense 200, credit inventory 200. Link the authorized ProjectSupply/AssistanceIssue proof without exposing recipients to donor services. |
| Sell 25 from saleable stock at 6.00 each, no tax in this example | Physical sold 25, saleable remaining 15. | Debit bank or processor clearing 150, credit trading revenue 150. Debit cost of goods 100, credit inventory 100. Sale creates no additional donor receipt. |
| Five saleable books suffer water damage and have zero recovery value under approved inspection | Quarantine and approved write-off reduce saleable inventory from 15 to 10. | Debit inventory loss 20, credit inventory 20. Preserve inspection evidence and independent approval. |
| Count at close | 10 distribution and 10 saleable remain. | Inventory 80. Opening 400 = program issues 200 + sale cost 100 + loss 20 + ending 80. Sales revenue 150 and in-kind gift 400 are separately identified. |

A public appeal defining cash-only goals may include the 150 net-of-returns trading proceeds only if the separately declared goal expressly counts trading proceeds. It must never call these proceeds another donor's 150 gift. Endowment or restricted purpose requirements on proceeds can persist through sale. Oversight can see the allowed purpose, available quantity and valuation without exposing the identity of beneficiaries.

## Field and evidence transfers

| Origin → destination | Required transfer | Exception route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Donor services → acceptance panel | Offer, donor identity/anonymous status, restrictions and return conditions, title/evidence of ownership, condition, quantity/unit, confidentiality instruction, conflicts, costs and safety concerns | Decline or hold with custody instructions when conditions cannot be met. Goods physically at the church may remain segregated as held for owner pending decision. |
| Panel → logistics and finance | Accepted quantity/terms version, donor giving party, InKindGift link, receiving location, restriction scopes, valuation method/date, authority, lot identifiers and goods receipt | A disputed valuation does not authorize a new donor-facing amount. Incomplete rights or owner evidence excludes saleable allocation. |
| Programme → finance/stock | Distribution event, actual units, approved purpose, relevant supply or grant, stock movement key, private recipient evidence accessible only to assigned team | Receipt of fifty items is not proof that fifty distinct children were served. Retain only the public measurement supported. |
| Shop → finance | Actual order units, donated inventory cost layer, tender and net sale amount, return/credit evidence, beneficial owner and proceeds restrictions | Missing cost/rule creates review queue while preserving actual collection. Distinguish consigned items that remain a third party's property. |
| Disposition → donor/finance | NonCashDisposition, actual sale/donation/loss, authority and date, linked proceeds and cost, required reporting deadline, permitted recipient and purpose | Any required notice uses the applicable policy without exposing buyer or programme beneficiaries. |
| Estate officer → treasury | Authorized estate/custodian identity, instruction, restriction and financial receipt evidence, currency, amount, date, remitter and previously recognized balance if any | Unidentified funds remain under finance control pending evidence; no new named donor or legacy reporting automatically. |

## Edge decisions to rehearse

- A donor offers five musical instruments on condition they can never leave a particular campus. No secure storage is available. The church can decline, seek a revised instrument or accept with a funded storage plan. A polite thank-you and physical delivery to the vestibule do not establish acceptance.
- A volunteer donates an afternoon. The volunteer roster can recognize time, while the gift register records a monetary value only when the governing policy and evidence support recognition. Never generate a cash receipt from hours worked.
- A bequest is denominated in a second currency. Preserve instrument currency and amount, functional-currency measurement dates/rates, fees, realized differences and bank receipt amount. The donor's historical estimate cannot act as the exchange rate.
- A documented return of a restricted item after acceptance reverses its original custody and carrying amount up to the value still held, and prompts review of the giving document and any already-disposed portion. It cannot silently subtract stock from an unrelated purchased lot.
- A household asks to make a public donation in tribute to the decedent. This is a fresh gift by its own GivingParty with a separate recognition instruction. It neither updates the estate gift's actual donor nor reveals the initial private intention.
