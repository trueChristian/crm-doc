# Release and withdraw media

## One service, multiple lawful audiences

A 54-minute service contains a sermon delivered by a visiting speaker, an eight-minute musical performance and a six-minute community prayer naming a person. The church has permission for live transmission of the full service to its defined audience. It has separate approval to publish the 40-minute sermon as a public audio replay, captioned replay and transcript after editorial review. The musician permits the live use only. The prayer is restricted to the live congregation. Use the fictional facts as the church's recorded permissions, not as universal assumptions about the rights conferred by a church event.

A producer can finish the live service without publishing the master. A media librarian can produce a public edit without exposing the song, private name or internal master title. If a separately sold course includes the public sermon plus licensed teaching materials, the course entitlement changes access only to the items and uses covered by those rights.

| Object or derivative | Initial scope | Publication gate | Expected on withdrawal of public replay permission |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Live service player and schedule | Defined live audience | Active LiveSession and allowed live MediaRights, valid audience throughout | An ended session stays ended. Replay access follows separately recorded release, not the earlier live URL. |
| Full 54-minute source master | Authorized editors/preservation only, where recording is separately permitted | Ingest, custody, retention and source integrity check | Restricted source may remain only as permitted for preservation/evidence. Public replacement must never reveal it. |
| 40-minute sermon edit and audio-only derivative | Public at the approved launch time | Edition and source revision, cleared segment ranges, approved credits, accessible rendition checks | Block future public retrieval of affected uses. A safe replacement requires its own clearance. |
| Captions, translations, transcript, chapters and search snippets | Same or narrower audience as their source release | Map timing and text to the approved edit, record language/review and publication state | Withdraw affected derivatives. Search suggestions, previews, translated spelling and cached snippets are each disposal targets. |
| Syndicated feed and sample excerpt | Separately approved use | Independent permission for syndication/preview and actual expiry | Cease future controlled feed delivery and request updates where distribution is external. Record acknowledged vs unreachable copies. |
| Paid course bundle | Named or eligible purchasers | Active grant, cleared edition/use and territory, course promise in accepted terms | Rights failure overrides access. Identify affected purchasers and open replacement/credit/refund queue without revealing unrelated private metadata. |

## Editing sequence and verifying scope

The master covers seconds 0–480 music, 480–2880 sermon and 2880–3240 prayer. The public derivative contains source interval [480, 2880), now mapped to output [0, 2400). A source caption starting at 500 seconds appears at 20 seconds in the authorized derivative. Cues crossing either cut must be trimmed and reviewed; a caption beginning at source second 2879 and continuing into prayer cannot leak the prayer text in its final output second. A title card, speaker biography, poster or filename can itself disclose a protected identity. Inspect them alongside audio and image content.

A second reviewer signs the exact edition revision, rights scope and accessibility results. Editing the cut, soundtrack, transcript, title attribution, rights holder or territory after review invalidates the affected sign-offs. Never replace bytes behind a supposedly immutable public edition without recording a new version. A transcript corrected for spelling can follow a lighter approved path; removal of a sensitive name must also find text excerpts and index entries derived from the old wording. Before release test keyboard access, caption visibility, transcript reading order, accessible player labels and low-bandwidth audio, under a user with the minimum intended permissions.

## Withdrawal clock

Use a 10:00 effective withdrawal and a configured maximum five-minute recheck interval for previously authorized online sessions in this rehearsal. Immediately block new playback, previews, downloads and protected snippets at the authoritative source. End or recheck already active sessions within the promised five minutes. Assets issued for authorized offline use may have a different known expiry or may already be permanently downloaded. Report controlled prevention, outstanding cache purge, external takedown request and unrecoverable pre-existing possession independently. Never claim to erase a file from a personal device based solely on a website withdrawal.

A template for the purchaser's notice contains the affected privately recognizable purchase, practical availability, remedy, deadline and contact route. At-risk or confidential audiences receive no revealing push title. If the replacement requires different accessibility accommodations, territory or language, describe the changed promise and ask for the appropriate approval. Never force a more restricted or inferior edition while claiming full fulfilment.

## Acceptance probes

| Persona and action | Expected result |
| --- | --- |
| Unauthenticated visitor asks for a private edition title by ID, asks for a transcript phrase, or compares category totals | No private title, filename, count difference, related recommendation or diagnostic detail that confirms its existence. Controlled objections return the same public presentation as an unavailable item. |
| Eligible course purchaser follows an old link at 10:02 | Newly authorized retrieval is denied for the withdrawn use. A separately authorized release may still be allowed if rights actually cover it. No private reason leaks in the response. |
| Public transcript search queries the removed prayer name after completion of withdrawal confirmations | Zero matches across primary listing, suggestions, accessible text and syndicated copies under church control. External responses are tracked as reported rather than presumed. |
| Worker retries publication of an older pre-withdrawal revision | Version check holds or rejects the stale publication. A late success callback cannot reopen the release. |
| Rights holder later restores permission for audio only | Fresh approved edition/use becomes available for authorized windows. Video, translations and public downloads remain unavailable until separately cleared. |
| Subscriber reports lost accessibility | Staff can identify cleared captions/transcript and assess remedy without inspecting unrelated prayer text or personal viewing history. |
