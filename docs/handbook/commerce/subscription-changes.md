# Price and deliver paid access

A church may make sermons freely available while charging for an optional study archive, interpretation or course package. The accepted offer defines the promises, exact access window, included editions, pricing, renewal authority, cancellation and remedies. A seat purchased for a learner belongs to the approved beneficiary. The payer can manage renewal without inspecting that learner's playback history.

## Clock and money fixture

Use a 30-day term from 1 April 00:00 through 1 May 00:00 in a zone with a fixed offset during the fixture. The end is exclusive. The approved policy prorates by whole service days with changes taking effect at local midnight. Basic costs 30.00 per 30-day fixture. Premium costs 45.00. There is no tax in this fixture. Full access is a stand-ready service recognized ratably for the example's stated policy. A single-event licence delivered at a point in time can use a different recognition policy.

| Change | Derivation | Customer balance and service effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 April. Customer pays Basic | 30.00 collected, 30 days Basic | Customer advances 30.00. Basic active until 1 May. Finance recognizes 1.00 per earned day. |
| 11 April. Upgrade after ten days | Basic earned 10.00. Unused Basic 20.00. Price of remaining 20 days Premium = 45 × 20/30 = 30.00 | Apply 20.00 existing unearned value to Premium. Collect 10.00 extra upon accepted change. Remaining service liability 30.00, Premium service 1.50 per day. |
| 21 April. End Premium immediately with a contractual refund | Another ten days Premium earned = 15.00. Remaining ten days Premium worth 15.00. | End 21 April at the effective instant. Credit/refund remaining 15.00 to permitted original tender(s). Total retained 30 + 10 − 15 = 25.00, matching 10.00 Basic and 15.00 Premium earned. |
| Alternative. Cancel renewal only on 21 April | Keep the accepted service term and unearned 15.00. | Access through 30 April inclusive. No next-term charge. At 1 May term expires and total earned = 40.00. Do not also issue the immediate-cancellation refund. |

If policy allocates the 15 refund across original cash captures, record the agreed method and per-capture ceilings. For example, return 10 to the upgrade capture and 5 to the original capture where original-method limits allow that allocation. A bank delay shows a pending payout. The customer can see the refund decision and committed access end without waiting for a card statement. A cancellation before the charge is confirmed follows the pending rather than paid-change flow.

## Field handoffs

| Sender → owner | Minimum data | Acceptance condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shop → subscription owner | Buyer/beneficiary or seat assignment, AccessPlan and approved version, permitted uses, included Release or series policy, price, currency, billing zone, anchor, start basis, renewal authority, terms evidence | Do not activate an unknown beneficiary, expired audience right, or mismatched future offer without a documented remedy. One source unit grants only its authorized seats. |
| Change request → billing | Source subscription/term, old and new versions, incoming change identity, effective service boundaries, calculation convention including day/second basis and time zone, quoted debit and credit, existing unpaid balance | Show both the unused-service credit and new charge. Record customer acceptance. A previous unpaid invoice cannot silently generate a cash refund for service never paid. |
| Billing → grant owner | Confirmed amount or approved credit terms, effective time, original grant, replacement scope, source action and any future renew/cancel flag | Close old scope and begin new scope with neither overlap nor gap beyond the contract. Financial retry cannot reset access start. |
| Subscription → finance | Paid term, earned-service schedule, plan-change credit/debit, original invoice references, tax basis where applicable, obligation and effective date | RecognitionSchedule and RecognitionInstallment apply approved timing. Cap total recognition at promised consideration net of accepted credits. |
| Rights → customer service | Affected MediaRelease, grants/terms impacted, blocked use, affected interval, alternative edition, replacement/refund decision rights | Unavailability is traceable to a controlled event. Reveal reason to staff according to scope. The customer need not learn a speaker's private withdrawal reason. |

## Renewal and grace truth table

| Observation | What the member sees | Billing and grant response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before end, future renewal authorized | Current paid access and next scheduled renewal price/date | Prepare one scheduled term and if required send an advance notice. No current-term duplication. |
| Next term capture verified | Renewal confirmed and the promised uninterrupted access | Resolve the existing renewal order. Create one paid term and one applicable grant despite repeated callbacks. |
| Charge declines, agreed three-day grace | Clearly labelled grace through a specific instant and an opportunity to update payment | Record failed attempt and configured retries. Grace access is an explicit grant basis. Finance follows policy for unearned/unpaid terms. |
| Customer cancels during grace | Effective cancellation and what remains available | Suppress future collection where required by terms. A later success for a previously submitted attempt requires review against cancellation and any refund due. |
| End of grace, charge still unpaid | Restricted paid item no longer plays, independent free releases still work | End only the grace/renewal basis. A separately paid pass, scholarship or institutional seat remains evaluable. |
| Renewal notice not delivered | Member can still review terms, account status and support channel while signed in | Retry using the permitted notice purpose. Where notice is a precondition, hold renewal until the policy requirement is satisfied. |

## Rental and seat edges

A separately purchased 48-hour rental starts at the first successful eligible playback with an activation deadline of 20 June 18:00. A rejected preview on 18 June cannot start it. Successful playback on 19 June 10:00 starts a 48-hour interval ending 21 June 10:00 if the deadline governs activation only. An offer whose deadline also caps expiry must state that rule before sale. A network failure after activation retries the original expiry. Issuing a second access credential or switching to captions cannot create another rental. If a rights window ends earlier, honor the disclosed cap or open a remedy case for the undeliverable promise.

A five-seat group pass is billed to one organization. It assigns four named learners plus one open seat. Removing one learner leaves two free seats and four economic facts remain distinct: purchase, assignment, current ability to play, and actual usage. A returned seat does not automatically reduce invoice value unless the terms authorize that credit. Staff can review assignment history and current capacity while the sponsoring payer sees only permitted enrolment and billing information.

## Failure and reversal tests

Attempt simultaneous upgrades from two devices. One accepted service revision becomes binding. The other must quote again against that revision and cannot charge twice. Replay a failed entitlement notification and observe unchanged start/end dates. After an upgrade capture succeeds but the new release becomes unavailable, route to customer service with the captured amount, original permissions and remedy options. Reissuing Basic without buyer approval cannot silently consume the Premium price.

Repeat a month-end anchor for a subscription beginning on the 31st. A configured last-day policy maps to the final date of shorter months and returns to the 31st where it exists. A different configured rule must display its actual next date before checkout. Repeat an elapsed-hour rental through a daylight-saving transition: 48 elapsed hours and two local calendar days can end at different wall times. The stored basis and promised end must explain which one governs.
