# Annual return workpapers and certification

The clerk needs three answers before signing a return. Which people or units were inside the perimeter, which dates govern inclusion, and what evidence explains the difference from the previous return? A report renderer must reproduce the answers from the certified revision even after the live congregation changes. This chapter gives a fixture for a church that uses civil dates, one primary formal membership per person within a congregation, and a calendar reporting year.

## Distinguish six measures

| Measure | Inclusion event | Distinct identity and grain | Permitted aggregation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Active formal roll | A qualifying MembershipPeriod covers the as-of date | Person and congregation at the agreed cutoff | Recompute distinct people at a wider perimeter where permitted. |
| Household connection | A HouseholdPerson relationship covers the date | Person and household | A person may appear in more than one household. Publish household and unique-person counts separately. |
| Attendance visits | A valid attendance observation names a person at an occurrence | Person and occurrence | Sum visits across occurrences. Mark anonymous headcounts separately. |
| Unique attendees | At least one valid attendance in the interval | Person within perimeter | Distinct across authorized occurrence records; cannot be recovered from anonymous totals. |
| Admissions during year | Qualifying admission or transfer-in took effect in the year | Approved movement identity | Count qualifying movements. A person may have more than one movement in a year. |
| Rites entered | A verified register entry satisfies the reporting definition | Rite occurrence or principal depending on the line definition | Distinguish one ceremony with two principals from two ceremonies. |

## Dated fixture

The return covers 1 January through 31 December. The end of a membership period is exclusive. At opening 40 people are on the formal roll. No person appears twice in the opening count.

| Effective date | Recorded date | Event | Movement in the original return | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 12 February | 12 February | One guest child joins a member's household | 0 | Household connection with no admission. |
| 5 March | 7 March | Three approved formal admissions | +3 | Use effective date. |
| 20 June | 20 June | One transfer out | −1 | Its membership period ends on 20 June. |
| 1 July | 1 July | A member moves from campus A to B within the same congregation | 0 | Campus view changes; organization roll does not. |
| 14 August | 16 August | One death closes a membership | −1 | Preserve historical presence before the date. |
| 1 October | 1 October | Family of four attends a service; one person is already on roll | 0 | Add attendance; no automatic admission. |
| 31 December | 15 January next year | One transfer-in accepted with verified effective date | 0 at 10 January freeze | The original extract did not yet contain that movement. |
| 1 January next year | 4 January next year | One admission for the following year | 0 | Future effective date. |

The certified balance at the 10 January data cutoff is 41: opening 40, plus 3, less 1 and less 1. The transfer entered on 15 January produces a restated year-end roll of 42 once an authorized revision admits the late evidence. Do not revise the previously signed balance from 41 in place. The new bridge includes the one additional transfer-in, the registrar's evidence and the independent authorization for amendment.

## Preparation and exception handling

1. Fix the definition version, civil as-of date, data cutoff instant, scope and reporting currency if finance lines are present. For a financial line obtain the approved external report edition, currency, entity and reporting basis. A missing or disputed figure remains unresolved until the accountable office supplies evidence. Record the expected submitting congregations, including units that closed during the year.
2. Build a private inclusion workpaper keyed by allowed identities. Store only the proof needed for the line. Gift values, pastoral notes and relationship narratives do not belong on an ordinary membership workpaper.
3. Compare movement totals with actual qualifying periods. A difference has an owner and one or more causes: unknown movement class, duplicate correction, conflicting dates, absent opening population, unresolved transfer or changed perimeter.
4. Run exception queries in both directions. A movement with no affected period and an unexplained new period with no authorized decision are separate exceptions. A parallel group membership or directory entry satisfies neither.
5. Provide an explanatory note for a material policy change even if the arithmetic balances. An amended definition may widen inclusion without implying that the additional people joined this year.
6. Prepare a signed aggregate return and a separately protected workpaper. Check that printed copies, spreadsheet exports and network transfers do not leak people behind suppressed cells. Track certified copies sent.

## Independently check the perimeter

Congregation A reports 34 unique participants; B reports 27. Six identities are shared inside an organization permitted to deduplicate. The authorized organization-wide figure is 55, while the sum of congregational participations is 61. If they are independent churches sharing totals only, report 61 participations and mark the number of unique people across churches unavailable. An incomplete return from B yields a known contribution from A with a coverage note. Reporting a total of 34 as the complete network count is a failed acceptance test.

For an aggregate with small-cell protection, verify the visible totals and subtotals together. Hiding a single small line while publishing a grand total and all other component lines still identifies it by subtraction. Apply complementary suppression or choose a coarser authorized breakdown. Test print, download, drill-through and assistant summaries against the same disclosure decision.

## Signing and undoing mistakes

The preparer can edit an explanatory note in draft. An independent reviewer can return a specific line with a question. A certified revision is read-only. A subsequent correction references the original revision, identifies exactly which lines changed, states the effective and recording dates, and marks any earlier issued copy as superseded where recall is possible. A former clerk with no current grant remains identified on an old attestation without retaining viewing privileges.
