# Close a room, move a service and reopen the site

On Friday a steward finds water in the church hall. Sunday's children's group and a public service use different partitions. The community choir hires the whole hall on Monday. A volunteer has the side-door key, the projector belongs to a pooled ministry booking, and the child's collection desk has a printed list. The facilities officer needs to establish who must act today and who can authorize reopening.

| Step | Decision and responsible desk | Evidence the next person needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Record and isolate | The steward identifies the space, affected partitions and start of the closure. Create one FacilityClosure linked to a ResourceWorkOrder. If an incident report is warranted, the assurance officer opens it separately. | Booking staff see the unavailable space, expected review and a safe public label. Witness identities stay in the incident file. An unknown end date blocks new commitments until reviewed. |
| 2. Identify commitments | Compare closure time with occupied intervals, including setup and reset. Expand recurring reservations over the affected horizon and check whole-hall conflicts, outside hires, equipment collections and transport drop-offs. | Queue one review per affected occurrence. Each has an owner, latest decision time and the contact responsible for that booking. Later unaffected dates retain their own reservations. |
| 3. Decide where people can go | A replacement room seats 30. The combined public registration is 36. The organizer must secure safe capacity, offer a second session or obtain cancellations. Check the accessible route, volunteer clearances, check-in stations and travel time independently. | Preserve original places while options are reviewed. Record each confirmed change or voluntary release, plus people still awaiting individual contact. Keep sibling and guardian restrictions in the check-in desk. |
| 4. Contact the right people | Issue a versioned correction against the affected occurrences. Reception follows up undelivered notices. The hirer receives the decision under its own agreement. A public service notice gives a location and time. | Each approved audience has its permitted notice, dispatch result and paper/telephone fallback. The correction excludes children's names, witness statements and private access instructions. |
| 5. Move equipment and access | The custodian signs the projector transfer. Record each issued key against its recipient. Reconcile the old printed list and replace it with a purpose-limited issue for the new desk. | A missing key goes to the access steward. A stale list cannot authorize collection; a child remains supervised when current instructions cannot be verified. |
| 6. Complete and reopen | The steward logs repair evidence. The designated reviewer records inspection and ends the closure. Separately, an authorized officer sends approved supplier or rental adjustments to accounting and tracks the response. | Future booking availability follows the recorded reopening time. End the temporary access grants, recover keys and list copies, and reconcile the attendance and unresolved actions. Repair completion alone cannot return a deposit. |

## Acceptance at the desk

Assume the two Sunday occurrences and Monday hire already have confirmations. Closing the parent space must produce three booking reviews even if only one partition's steward filed the report. Two staff members processing the same closure must see the same work order and the same affected bookings. A Wednesday booking after an explicitly confirmed Tuesday reopening remains in place.

Move only the children's group first. Its carers receive the approved service correction on the permitted guardian routes; the adult service and choir still show unresolved actions. Check that a general bulletin subscriber receives only the public announcement if published. Confirm a separate private reach-back for a carer whose notice failed. Compare the revised check-in point to the actual supervised roster at collection.

If the accounting connection goes offline, the service move, child collection reconciliation and physical repair can proceed. The office retains the pending fee or deposit query, records the external confirmation when available and never treats the building's reopened status as evidence of money returned. Close the case when every operational review has a disposition and each outstanding external matter has an identified owner and due date.
