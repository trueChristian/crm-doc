# From a family enquiry to a certified entry

Use one case identifier to coordinate the church office, officiant, room coordinator and record custodian. Give each role the information it needs. Holding a date for a funeral does not verify a death, an interment, an entitlement to a particular burial space, permission to publish an obituary, or receipt of a fee.

## Intake and scheduling contract

| Command | Required evidence or payload | Accepted effect | Exception or correction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open enquiry | Safe contact route or an agreed anonymous enquiry reference, service type, preferred timing and accountable office | One CeremonyRequest. Expose public directions and the responsible office's contact. | Two relatives contacting the church can refer to the same case. Verify authorization before revealing what the other relative supplied. |
| Hold date and space | Named occurrence, availability at that instant, hold expiry, room/grounds custodian, access needs | One time-limited reservation. Display the expiry and the person's confirmation options. | A stale availability page cannot displace a confirmed booking. On expiry release only this hold. |
| Verify documents | Requirement edition, inspected evidence, authorized reviewer, date and minimal outcome | A completed or returned CeremonyRequirement. Other staff see the operational result. | An authorized exception binds to the specific unmet condition. The uploaded document alone cannot waive it. |
| Confirm arrangements | Verified authority, officiant availability, approved location, outstanding requirements, public-information decision | Confirm the agreed ceremony bookings and release approved notices. | Changing the family contact reevaluates who may approve a publication. A prior contact remains visible only to permitted staff. |
| Record completed service | Observed occurrence, officiant, principal names as recorded, location and completed requirements | The registrar reviews one RiteRecord and creates or links the designated register entry. | A cancelled rehearsal or memorial event cannot create an interment or certify a civil status. |
| Issue extract | Verified requestor authority, approved register entry, audience and issue purpose | One authorized serial-bound release using the archive and rite issue records for the same issue. | A repeat print is a rendition. A materially corrected issue creates a replacement and preserves the old serial's status. |

If the family withdraws the public notice, stop unpublished announcements and update publicly controlled pages. Notify owners of already printed copies. Any permitted internal record, room reservation and decision about fees have their own lifecycle.

## Burial capacity with existing rights

Count physical occupancy once. Count unused, effective reserved rights once. A reservation consumed by an interment must leave the remaining rights available to their proper holders. Store both before and after balances and the single operation key.

A space has capacity four. One unit is occupied by a historical interment, unrelated to current reservations. Family H holds two unconsumed units. One unit remains available to new reservations. H uses one reserved unit: occupancy rises to two, H has one reservation left, and the unrestricted available quantity remains one. A walk-in cannot claim H's remaining unit by noting that the plot still has two physically empty positions. If the final unrestricted unit is issued, further open allocation must wait for a different space or authorized change.

| Situation | Registrar decision | Postcondition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The holder presents a verified active right covering this ground | Check unused units, physical capacity and any required authorizations. | Consume authorized units once. Link the interment to the right and the verified location. |
| Two desks try to use the final unit concurrently | Compare the current occupancy and reservation revisions at commit. | Accept once. The other desk receives an availability exception without creating a second burial entry. |
| The memorial location is approximate in an imported historic book | Record the original description, precision, mapping evidence and reviewer. | A provisional map location cannot authorize a new conflicting reservation. |
| The family wants a memorial but no burial here has been verified | Record the ceremony or tribute and publication conditions. | Create no occupancy movement and issue no location certificate. |
| A correction changes a plot reference | Verify evidence and applicable authority, record the linked correction, and reconcile both affected locations. | Preserve the historical wording, current corrected location and issued-copy review. |
| A physical relocation is proposed | Route to the authorized local process. Record separate instruction, completion evidence and updated custody. | A clerical correction cannot by itself mark physical relocation complete. |

## Permission and release rehearsal

Use two unrelated families with the same surname, an external officiant, a private memorial, an approved public tribute and a retired graveyard section. A volunteer can direct arrivals to the approved entrance. A registrar can inspect authority for the specific rite. The grounds coordinator sees the authorized location and service timing. The finance clerk sees the fee reference, payer and settlement. Only the permitted release includes the contributor's name or a photograph.

Verify the internal register search, public memorial listing, exported contact sheet, cached preview and a printed certificate separately. Withdraw a photo halfway through scheduled publication and confirm that the original case remains locatable to the authorized custodian. Review existing downloads and physical copies as issued evidence rather than promising their remote destruction.
