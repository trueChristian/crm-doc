# Property insurance, other cover and claims

A trustee can ask which property or activity was covered on the date of a loss, on what terms, whether notice was acknowledged and what remains unrecovered. Coverage descriptions and amounts are recorded as written in approved policy documents; coverage and liability decisions belong to authorized people and the insurer. The finance asset register, supplier directory, property bookings and general ledger remain authoritative for the resources they manage.

## Operating sequence

1. Record the known loss, safety handoff if applicable, affected property and owner. Preserve relevant evidence, valuations and policy wording under restricted custody. A claim may reference a restricted incident through an opaque ID; the insurer does not receive a copy of case records by default.
2. Check the policy, endorsements, event and discovery dates, reporting window, legal entity, interests and limits. Where wording or dates are unclear, flag human review with a due time. Keep denied or uncertain coverage visible as such. No automated declaration that a claim is payable.
3. Prepare notice under organizational authority and record recipient, dispatch, proof, acknowledgment, missing evidence, review and dispute deadlines. Reassign outstanding matters before a trustee or broker leaves. Record emergency spending under procurement controls, linked but separate.
4. A suitably delegated person may accept or dispute a settlement. The external accounting office records actual receipts, vendor payments, deductibles, impairment and repairs under its own accounting policy. A claim estimate is not automatically a receivable or cash receipt.
5. Reconcile accepted offer, recoveries, associated costs and outstanding amounts before closure. Track reopened claims and supplemental recoveries separately. The board can see totals by policy, exposure, open deadline and outstanding value; access to names and incident evidence is limited.

## Worked cases

| Case | Expected result |
|---|---|
| A roof leak causes assessed damage of 12,000 in the policy currency. The insurer allows 10,000 with a 1,000 deductible | Net offer is 9,000 before other adjustments. Received installments of 4,000 and 5,000 settle the offer; the 3,000 difference from the reported damage is separately explainable. |
| A donated instrument carries a book value of 800 and insured value of 3,000 | Both amounts retain their dates and bases; neither overwrites the other. |
| An incident occurs before renewal but the insurer learns of it after | The system presents relevant periods, notice rules and endorsements for human review. It does not silently select the newest policy. |
| Two scopes claim the same loss under overlapping policies | Link the related claims, flag possible double recovery and require a coordinated decision. Separate payouts stay visible without double counting the underlying loss in reports. |
| A receipt callback arrives twice | One matched recovery references the external accounting reference; claim status and totals stay unchanged on replay. |
