# Assurance, risk and independent review

A trustee checks risk acceptance, an insurance officer reconciles claims, and a designated reviewer handles complaints independently. Most staff need a safe action or a due date rather than the underlying case material. Start with the workflow and follow the linked record owner.

## Authoritative boundaries

| Other record owner | What assurance exchanges |
|---|---|
| [People and pastoral records](../people/README.md) | Refer protected concerns to the designated safeguarding lead. Consume only action-specific authority and privacy-safe status for operational decisions. Confidential pastoral narratives remain with their owners. |
| [Workforce](../administration/workforce.md) | Route approved employment remedies and grievance results to the restricted personnel case. The independent docket controls investigation, conflicts and appeal. Volunteer cases can exist without an employment record. |
| [Ministry and resources](../ministry/README.md) | Refer to actual event, building, equipment, responsible team and child check-out receipt. Maintenance, reservations and handover remain in those registers. |
| [Accounting exchange](../finance/accounting-exchange.md) | Link the church property or equipment, insurer’s terms and permitted external claim-payment references. The accounting provider owns carrying values, financial corrections and reconciliation. |
| [Operational recovery](../platform/operations-recovery.md) | Refer to service capability and restoration status during a disruption. Human continuity, accountability and handover remain in the active continuity record. |
| [Shared record conventions](../foundation/record-conventions.md) | Reuse organization scope, typed identifiers, dates, evidence controls, immutable history and idempotent operations. |

## End-to-end example

A near miss at a community meal identifies a recurring floor hazard. The coordinator records the occurrence and the three witness submissions without counting three incidents. Facilities receives a repair request; the risk owner schedules a test to see whether the new inspection works. If a later loss occurs, the insurance officer links a separate claim and documents the policy term. Finance records any actual repair and recovery. A worker alleging prior pressure to conceal the hazard uses a protected route; the risk register may receive a general preventive action, with no link accessible to an ordinary steward that would reveal the reporter.

## Reports and adoption

The board can review risk acceptance expiring next quarter, controls lacking current evidence, unacknowledged incident handoffs, pending claim notice dates, confidential case service levels and exercise findings awaiting verification. Each measure declares its as-of date, population and source owner. Privileged case readers have explicit, time-bound grants. A trustee's reporting role alone cannot disclose personal evidence or reporter identity. Before activation publish local decision authorities, alternates, notification calendars, accessible routes, privacy and retention schedules, and thresholds for independent review.
