# Giving and accounting exchange records

Each record below belongs to the church or records its exchange with an authorized outside service. The business chapter gives the decision sequence. Open the record for field definitions and an acceptance decision.

## Offerings, gifts and donor correspondence

| Record | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [GivingParty](GivingParty.md) | The giving identity that a donor-services officer may use for gifts and correspondence. |
| [CollectionBatch](CollectionBatch.md) | A collection of physical tenders received into the church’s custody. |
| [CollectionCount](CollectionCount.md) | A tender line in a separately attributable count or recount. |
| [CustodyTransfer](CustodyTransfer.md) | A witnessed release and receipt of a collection or sealed bag. |
| [Gift](Gift.md) | One monetary contribution received, with a persistent source key and a separate verification decision. |
| [GiftAllocation](GiftAllocation.md) | One portion of a gift directed to a church purpose. |
| [GiftAdjustment](GiftAdjustment.md) | A confirmed correction to one original allocation, preserving its initial value and subsequent effects. |
| [GiftAttribution](GiftAttribution.md) | Credit for a person or organization associated with a gift, separate from the payer and giver. |
| [Pledge](Pledge.md) | A donor’s accepted promise to support a purpose on an agreed schedule. |
| [PledgeInstallment](PledgeInstallment.md) | One due amount and date in an accepted giving schedule. |
| [DonationMandate](DonationMandate.md) | Evidence of the donor’s permission for scheduled collections by an external payment service. |
| [GivingDocument](GivingDocument.md) | A donor-facing acknowledgement or statement edition, or a reference to one issued by an authorized external system. |
| [GivingDocumentItem](GivingDocumentItem.md) | A fixed, reproducible inclusion in a reviewed donor document. |

## Noncash, memorial, matching and planned gifts

| Record | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [InKindGift](InKindGift.md) | The church’s receipt of property, goods, services or use of space under agreed terms. |

## Designations, restrictions and grant obligations

| Record | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [Fund](Fund.md) | A church giving designation or collection purpose available to a donor or clerk. |
| [FundRestriction](FundRestriction.md) | Evidence of terms governing what the church agreed to do with a designation or gift. |
| [GrantAward](GrantAward.md) | Administrative record of signed incoming or outgoing mission support or grant terms. |
| [AwardMilestone](AwardMilestone.md) | A delivery, report, approval or activity that the church agreed to evidence to a sponsor. |

## Accounting exchange and source ownership

| Record | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [FinanceCodeMapping](FinanceCodeMapping.md) | A scoped interpretation of a church designation or activity at an external accounting destination. |
| [FinanceExchangeBatch](FinanceExchangeBatch.md) | A sealed manifest of financial handoff items, transport-neutral and scoped to one connection. |
| [FinanceExchangeItem](FinanceExchangeItem.md) | A source event with durable business identity and its latest destination disposition. |
