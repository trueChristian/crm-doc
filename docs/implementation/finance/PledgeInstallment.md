## Record invariant

Identity, audit fields, ownership and effective-date notation follow the [shared record envelope](../../foundation/record-conventions.md). A business key or parent-child uniqueness rule below remains enforceable even when an implementation also assigns an opaque identifier.

Derive paid amount from allocations net of returns. Unmapped or excess cash remains unapplied, applies to another installment with consent, or becomes a new gift.

## Input, storage and exchange cases

Each section targets one field on an otherwise admissible record. The first probe tests its declared representation, not permission to commit the transaction. The owner invariant above can reject an otherwise valid value. A change to a projection is tested at its source.

### pledge_id

The target is [Pledge](Pledge.md). Resolve the identifier in the approved scope before using its fields.

| Probe | Input or observation | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admissible representation | Pledge·A01 | Resolve stable identity in the permitted scope. |
| Malformed or unmapped | Pledge·MISSING | Return a type mismatch without changing the link. |
| Unresolved target | Pledge·MISSING | Queue the missing Pledge mapping or reject the command before linking. |
| Wrong scope | Pledge·B01 | Do not attach another church’s Pledge without a supported exchange and local authority. |
| Target rename | Rename Pledge·A01 after linking | The existing pledge_id keeps the same identity and historic documents retain their original wording. |
| Incomplete record | No pledge_id at the step where the rule requires it | Identify the missing business fact before considering the record complete. |

### due_on

Use civil calendar date for `PledgeInstallment.due_on`. Keep the date without converting it into a time or shifting it across zones.

| Probe | Input or observation | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admissible representation | 2024-02-29 | Keep the date without converting it into a time or shifting it across zones. |
| Malformed or unmapped | 2025-02-29 | Reject the impossible civil date. |
| 2026-04-31 | 31 April 2026 | Reject instead of advancing into May. |
| Midnight in a different time zone | 23:30 on previous UTC day at a separate site | A civil date remains the entered date. |
| Date lies on an effective-period boundary | effective date equals the stated interval endpoint | Apply the owning record’s explicit endpoint convention. |
| Incomplete record | No due_on at the step where the rule requires it | Identify the missing business fact before considering the record complete. |
