# OperationsIncident

[Operational readiness, interruptions and review](../../ministry/readiness.md) · [Department records](README.md) · [Value conventions](../value-conventions.md)

## Record invariant

Identity, audit fields, ownership and effective-date notation follow the [shared record envelope](../../foundation/record-conventions.md). A business key or parent-child uniqueness rule below remains enforceable even when an implementation also assigns an opaque identifier.

Log a facilities issue, interruption, welfare concern, near miss or drill. Separate factual observation, staff actions and later conclusions. Protect reporter identity and location according to role.
