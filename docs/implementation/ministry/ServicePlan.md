## Record invariant

Identity, audit fields, ownership and effective-date notation follow the [shared record envelope](../../foundation/record-conventions.md). A business key or parent-child uniqueness rule below remains enforceable even when an implementation also assigns an opaque identifier.

One occurrence may have several tracks, for example different venues or languages. One approved active revision per track. Cross-track dependencies are explicit.

Each section targets one field on an otherwise admissible record. The first probe tests its declared representation, not permission to commit the transaction. The owner invariant above can reject an otherwise valid value. A change to a projection is tested at its source.

### revision

Use revision number or opaque revision token for `ServicePlan.revision`. Compare under the declared revision scheme. Require the expected version for conflicting work.

| Probe | Input or observation | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admissible representation | the current revision returned by the owner | Compare under the declared revision scheme. Require the expected version for conflicting work. |
| Malformed or unmapped | superseded version presented for a new approval | Reject or reopen review against the current contents. |
