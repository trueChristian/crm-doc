# HouseholdPerson

[Households, kinship and delegated authority](../../people/households-and-authority.md) · [Department records](README.md) · [Value conventions](../value-conventions.md)

## Record invariant

Identity, audit fields, ownership and effective-date notation follow the [shared record envelope](../../foundation/record-conventions.md). A business key or parent-child uniqueness rule below remains enforceable even when an implementation also assigns an opaque identifier.

Many people per household and many households per person. Within one household the same person's residence episodes cannot overlap. At most one primary residence per person for a given date where the church uses that designation. An adult relative in another home can be linked solely through kinship.
