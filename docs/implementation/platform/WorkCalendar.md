## Input, storage and exchange cases

Each section targets one field on an otherwise admissible record. The first probe tests its declared representation, not permission to commit the transaction. The owner invariant above can reject an otherwise valid value. A change to a projection is tested at its source.

### label

Use human-readable text for `WorkCalendar.label`. Preserve the meaningful spelling, script and structure; derive separate search forms.

| Probe | Input or observation | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admissible representation | entered label in the original script | Preserve the meaningful spelling, script and structure; derive separate search forms. |
| Malformed or unmapped | invalid text encoding | Return a field-level validation issue without silently changing meaning. |
| Accented and non-Latin characters | Zoë and ǂNū-khoen in the original script | Preserve and display their original spelling. |
| Label changes after formal issue | approved edition before a spelling correction | Keep the authorized original rendering in the issued edition. |
| Bidirectional or multiline input | mixed-direction or multiline entered text | Render in the field’s declared context and respect accessibility and disclosure rules. |
| Incomplete record | No label at the step where the rule requires it | Identify the missing business fact before considering the record complete. |

### effective_at

Use civil calendar date for `WorkCalendar.effective_at`. Keep the date without converting it into a time or shifting it across zones.

| Probe | Input or observation | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admissible representation | 2024-02-29 | Keep the date without converting it into a time or shifting it across zones. |
| Malformed or unmapped | 2025-02-29 | Reject the impossible civil date. |
| 2026-04-31 | 31 April 2026 | Reject instead of advancing into May. |
| Midnight in a different time zone | 23:30 on previous UTC day at a separate site | A civil date remains the entered date. |
| Date lies on an effective-period boundary | effective date equals the stated interval endpoint | Apply the owning record’s explicit endpoint convention. |
| Incomplete record | No effective_at at the step where the rule requires it | Identify the missing business fact before considering the record complete. |

### version

Use revision number or opaque revision token for `WorkCalendar.version`. Compare under the declared revision scheme. Require the expected version for conflicting work.

| Probe | Input or observation | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admissible representation | the current revision returned by the owner | Compare under the declared revision scheme. Require the expected version for conflicting work. |
| Malformed or unmapped | superseded version presented for a new approval | Reject or reopen review against the current contents. |
| Two operators begin at revision seven | two edits both cite baseline 7 | Commit the first change once and re-evaluate the second; never overwrite an unreviewed difference. |
| Re-read an already approved revision | re-open issued revision 4 after revision 6 | Return the frozen value and evidence for that edition. |
| Replay of an earlier result | same business operation observed at two revisions | Correlate by the business operation identity; a newer version does not create a second effect. |
| Incomplete record | No version at the step where the rule requires it | Identify the missing business fact before considering the record complete. |
