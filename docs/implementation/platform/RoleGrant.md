# RoleGrant

[Accounts, representation and authorization](../../platform/identity-access.md) · [Department records](README.md) · [Value conventions](../value-conventions.md)

## Record invariant

Identity, audit fields, ownership and effective-date notation follow the [shared record envelope](../../foundation/record-conventions.md). A business key or parent-child uniqueness rule below remains enforceable even when an implementation also assigns an opaque identifier.

Many roles per account, many accounts per role. An employment-derived grant identifies its originating engagement or appointment. Independently authorized roles retain their own grants. Scope can be organization, congregation, campus, group, case or explicitly owned resource.
