## Record invariant

Identity, audit fields, ownership and effective-date notation follow the [shared record envelope](../../foundation/record-conventions.md). A business key or parent-child uniqueness rule below remains enforceable even when an implementation also assigns an opaque identifier.

Unique organization/destination/source/action/generation. Include the stable order-line unit or action reference when a line has multiple recipients, seats or partial adjustments. Admissions consumes each Ministry AdmissionSaleUnit through its RegistrationPlace and records receipt or exception. Finance/giving issue official finance documents.
