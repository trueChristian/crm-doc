# Meetings, notice, eligibility and quorum

The secretary selects a body and meeting class, chooses a date and local time zone, proposes a chair and venue and checks the notice deadline. The chair reviews the draft agenda and authorizes calling the meeting according to the active charter. The secretary can see exactly which voters were entitled to notice, which copies were issued and which exceptions need attention.

## From draft to sitting

1. Preview the local deadline, including holiday treatment and the exact meaning of clear days. Preserve both the intended local time and resolved instant. An ambiguous clock-change hour requires an explicit offset. Changing the venue, date or business after issue opens a revision assessment and may restart the notice period.
2. Certify the voting and notice roll as of the configured cutoff. Recalculate a draft as people join, transfer or resign. A certified roll changes through a dated correction that records who authorized eligibility and why. A guest or invited expert may attend while holding zero votes.
3. Dispatch separate notices to the eligible audiences. Record returned mail, bounced email and manual service. The clerk can document a waiver only when the adopted rules allow it, preserving signatory, scope and date. Flag a short notice period or an unserved class to the chair before opening.
4. Check presence and proxies at opening and at each decision when required. Record declarations of interest and temporary absences. Disconnecting from a remote meeting triggers the configured grace and review process. Losing quorum pauses substantive decisions, identifies pending business and records which adjournment options remain authorized.
5. A postponed or cancelled meeting retains the earlier notice and history. Communicate changes through a new dispatch, withdraw obsolete reminders and link the reconvened meeting. The active charter determines whether to recertify eligibility or reissue notice.

## Worked decision

With 24 eligible seats and an at-least-one-third requirement, the threshold is 8. Seven eligible voters are present and an observer is also present. Under a rule that excludes proxies from quorum the meeting is one short. A late eligible arrival brings it to 8. When that voter leaves before a motion at 20:12, a new check records 7. Historical opening and closing checks remain separate. If the charter counts represented principals, recalculate that case with the accepted, unexpired mandates, counting each entitlement once.

## Verification

- Call a meeting for the 18th requiring seven clear calendar days. Where both service day and meeting day are excluded, service on the 10th qualifies and service on the 11th needs a ruling.
- A principal with an accepted proxy appears in person. Apply the charter's replacement rule before ballot issue, invalidate the obsolete representation, and prevent two uses of the same entitlement.
- Replaying last month's roll gives the same result after a household moves or a member resigns. A roll appeal identifies the authorized ruling without revealing the member's pastoral history.
- A meeting cancellation keeps prior dispatch evidence. A member receives the change once and receives no reminder for the old sitting.
- The clerk can report notices without qualifying evidence, open appeals, turnout by class, proxies due for validation, and each moment when a decision lacked quorum.
