# Governance and official records

Start here when a secretary, board chair, returning officer or archivist needs to establish who may act, call a meeting, confirm a decision or release its record.

| Work at hand | Open | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Establish a board, office or delegation and its rules | [Bodies, terms and rules](bodies-and-terms.md) | An effective roster and a rule edition in force |
| Call, postpone or reconvene a meeting | [Meetings and notice](meetings-and-notice.md) | A dated notice, attendance intervals and reproducible quorum |
| Put a proposal, amendment, election or ballot to the body | [Deliberation and voting](deliberation-and-voting.md) | A decision on an exact text or a certified election |

A governing body carries formal authority granted by a church. A ministry group can furnish its roster, but appointing officers, publishing to members and authorizing expenditure are separate decisions. Congregations choose their own rule editions. A local policy decision must be recorded before a workflow depending on that decision is enabled.

## Working contracts

An entity name denotes a logical table. Each row has an immutable opaque primary key `id`. An `X ref` field points to exactly one X unless marked optional with `?`. The referring entity can have many rows per parent unless uniqueness narrows this. Each record belongs to one organization, directly or through its required parent. Every reference must resolve within that scope unless explicitly exchanged as a released extract. An instant denotes an absolute point in time. A date follows the governing body's named local time zone. Intervals include their start and exclude their end. State changes identify the accountable actor, time, prior revision and reason.

The secretary's workspace opens on forthcoming notices, readiness of meeting papers, minutes awaiting confirmation and overdue actions. Chairs can see quorum and conflicts on the item currently under discussion. Ordinary members can find released minutes by date, body, subject or resolution number. A denied record contributes neither a title nor a search-result count. Private attendance and voting weights never appear in a general directory.

## Cross-department handoffs

People supplies identity and dated membership evidence. Governance freezes an electoral roll and its rules for each proceeding. Communications returns dispatch outcomes for meeting notices. Congregational attendance belongs to ministry operations, while governance attendance supplies item-level deliberative quorum. Finance accepts an approved resolution as authority for a spending request and independently checks budget, delegation and posting. A passed motion authorizes the specified action only. The secretary cannot debit a fund by confirming the minutes.
