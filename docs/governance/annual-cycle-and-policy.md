# Annual assemblies, policies and review

A secretary planning an annual meeting can work backward from the meeting date to identify the membership cutoff, local notice deadline, nominations, committee reports, accounts, budget proposals, policy renewals and open actions. A regional assembly adds delegate credentials and reporting to constituent congregations. The church selects its reporting year, required instruments, signatories and external obligations for each operating context.

## Annual meeting preparation

Create the cycle, assign report owners, provide accessible submission formats and track which documents are due, approved or restricted. Finance releases a versioned statement for the chosen fund, extent of consolidation and period. Membership supplies as-of totals and movements, including the start and end population definitions. Ministry leaders supply aggregate participation, activities and outcomes. Resolve mismatched reporting periods before the report packet is approved. A late revision after circulation creates an updated packet and the governing body's required correction notice.

At registration, validate local voters separately from delegates. Identify represented seats, observer status, replacement credentials and whether a delegate can hold multiple rights. Freeze the electoral roll, seat allocation and voting rule at the configured cutoff. A church with three delegates and two alternates should contribute no more than its three rights when one delegate and both alternates arrive. Activate only duly substituted delegates.

After the meeting, return certified extracts, adopted budget references, election results and outstanding actions to the authorized constituent bodies. The report coordinator can see dispatch and acceptance evidence while donors, individual ballots and private case detail remain outside the packet. Track annual filing or policy renewal as a dated obligation with an actual responsible officer. Keep separate dates for board approval, dispatch to a recipient, receipt and acceptance of the filing.

## Policy change, petitions and review

Publishing a policy edition includes its effective date, affected roles, overview in plain language, accessible full text, acknowledgment requirement and review date. Assign staff and volunteer acknowledgements from a frozen population. New joiners receive the currently applicable edition upon appointment; a departing volunteer's future reminders stop. The record of an earlier acknowledgement remains available under retention policy. An emergency temporary policy needs a recorded authority, expiry and later review.

A member can lodge a petition with an authorized officer, receive a visible status and get a reasoned decision within church-defined deadlines. Verify the signers against the historically eligible class rather than counting a public petition page. On acceptance, add business to the appropriate agenda or start the formal calling process. An appeal routes to a permitted, conflict-free reviewer. A person whose decision is under review cannot self-certify its outcome. Restricted complaints and care concerns must never be distributed as ordinary petition text.

## Verification

- Restart a cycle after a missed deadline. Already approved returns keep their revisions, overdue owners receive one reminder and an exception report identifies the unresolved items.
- A policy is revised after 12 of 20 volunteers signed. Reports show 12 prior-edition acknowledgements, a new assignment cohort and the exact change. Receiving a reminder creates no acknowledgment.
- A petition needs five eligible signatures. Two signers share a phone, a third signs twice, and a guest signs once. Count the distinct eligible rights, document exclusions privately and publish only the permitted aggregate.
- An annual packet contains a corrected budget after approval. Its replacement issue pins the corrected finance release, retains link to the prior circulated version and gives members the notice required by the active rules.
- A review overturns a decision already partly executed. Mark the decision history, notify the action owner and request a financial review for any affected commitments. Preserve the historical execution records.
