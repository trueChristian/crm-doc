All money uses one currency. Expected opening net assets are 70 unrestricted and 30 restricted against a bank debit of 100; balances are tested both by fund and in total. A staged identity prevents its person-level mutations but does not invalidate an independently identified financial unit. Uploaded history sends zero messages even where future notices are permitted.
