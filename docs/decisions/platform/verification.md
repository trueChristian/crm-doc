# Independently checking the platform decision cases

The seven suites use finite commissioning profiles. They test the specified interactions and selected business outcomes independently of any implementation language. A church can run the profiles against an installation, add local operating values and keep evidence of actual differences.

| Suite | Input dimensions | Distinct cases | What must reconcile |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Authority | 6 staff roles × 4 operative campus-grant states × 6 child mandates × 4 directory choices × 4 staff job states | 2,304 | Personal rights, staff action, child rights, directory exposure and job outcome each follow their own evidence. |
| Replay | 6 domain histories × 4 initial consecutive checkpoints × all 120 permutations of five revisions | 2,880 | Each arrival has one receipt, every revision after the initial checkpoint applies exactly once and the highest consecutive applied revision ends at five. |
| Migration | 4 identity states × 4 register fact types × 4 contact decisions × 4 financial receipt classifications × 4 opening fund mappings | 1,024 | The disposition of an independent receipt totals 30. Debit and credit totals and changes against each expected fund must both agree before opening. |
| Offline | 4 sets of attendance evidence × 4 pack states × 4 contact revisions × 4 seat allocations × 3 custody observations × 3 guest-key states | 2,304 | Presence, proposed contact, room or seat capacity, supervised release and visitor follow-up are independent decisions. |
| Privacy | 4 public field classes × 4 directory choices × 4 hold histories × 4 artifact states × 4 recovery conditions × 3 retention clocks | 3,072 | Scope the outcome individually to the original, visible field, derived representation, issued copy and reopening boundary. |
| Recovery | 3 baseline transactions × 4 measured time profiles × 4 money results × 4 notice results × 4 privacy conditions × 4 custody states | 3,072 | Compare two measured clocks against the selected objective and evaluate four downstream gates independently. |
| Assistance | 3 capabilities × 4 claim-support results × 4 answerable-completion results × 3 abstention results × 4 critical-status fixtures × 4 draft events | 2,304 | Quality thresholds apply jointly, critical failures block rollout, and a local pilot verdict never substitutes for a draft reviewer. |

## Reproduce representative checks

**Event projection.** Start at checkpoint zero and receive revisions 5, 2, 1, 4, 3. Track the set of observed revisions. After 5 and 2 the largest complete prefix is empty. Receiving 1 extends it through 2, so revisions 1 and 2 apply. Receiving 4 still leaves a gap. Receiving 3 extends the complete prefix through 5, applying 3, 4 and 5. Peak deferred backlog is two. An intention in a rebuilt projection is never a receipt for a real-world payout or notice.

**Fund control.** With 100 bank debit, expected unrestricted opening net assets of 70 and restricted opening net assets of 30, a mapping of 100 and zero passes the ordinary debit-credit trial but fails both fund controls. The differences are plus 30 unrestricted and minus 30 restricted. Independently classified receipts must reconcile to gift plus trading plus unapplied equal to 30, with the original purpose still attached to unresolved money.

**Scope.** Choose a former campus-A staff member with a live guardian registration right, a withdrawn directory choice and a previously queued staff approval. The personal action still works, campus staff action ends, registration authority is evaluated only for the named child, the public derivative is invalidated and the staff job is cancelled. Changing only the directory choice must not revive the queued approval or affect child collection.

**Care and custody.** A verified supervised handover undergoes independent reconciliation even when the original device is expired. A newly proposed pickup from a stale roster can never acquire authorization by being uploaded. Combine this condition with a duplicate named attendance and a disputed contact to confirm record types stay independent.

**Scoring.** For an assistant capability with 23 supported claims out of 24, 18 correct completions out of 20 and 9 correct abstentions out of 10, the unrounded rates meet 95, 90 and 90 percent. One unauthorized source title changes the pilot decision to hold. It has no power to approve or reject a particular submitted translation on behalf of the human reviewer.

## Completeness checks

Every case ID must occur once and sequences must have no gaps. A distinct input tuple must occur once within each suite. Identical outcomes across different input tuples can be correct: expiring an unrelated permission must have no effect on a current member's independent right. For an independent implementation, compute the next consecutive prefix directly from a set of observed revisions, compare fund variances independently of trial balance, compare rates as exact proportions before formatting, and recalculate all scope and time limits from the inputs. The corpus was checked against these controls over all 16,960 cases.
