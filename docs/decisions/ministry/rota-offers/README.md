# Who should receive these two duty offers?

I is interpretation 09:00–10:00. W is welcome beginning at 10:00 plus the stated gap and lasting 45 minutes. Skills and recent workloads are in A/B/C order. A is available for either role until the displayed clearance end. B and C have current credentials and the displayed availability. Equality at the end of a fully covered shift is allowed.

“I”, “W” or “IW” means the role or list of roles. The church first maximizes covered roles, then prioritizes I if only one can be covered, then avoids assigning one person twice. It minimizes the sum of recent served-to-target ratios for the selected duties and breaks ties by person identity in I, W order. A double assignment requires the church to allow two, the worker to qualify for both, and the travel time to fit the gap. An offer is still unconfirmed.

A role-pair enumeration is checked against a second independent assignment by distributing subsets of the duties to the three named workers, filtering those distributions, then comparing coverage, scarce-role priority, repeated duty, recent burden and tie break in order. No declared or private protected characteristic, giving history or disciplinary reason enters rank.
