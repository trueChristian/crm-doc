# Prorate earned service against a paid plan change · cases 961–1200

[Policy, arithmetic and case ranges](README.md) · [All decision suites](../README.md)

A paid monthly term contains the stated number of local service days. The price change takes effect at midnight after the elapsed days. Historical earned service is rounded half up once. Unused original value equals original collected price less that earned amount. The new component is the remaining days at the new full-term price, rounded once. A positive difference is collected or invoiced and a negative difference is customer credit. A credited balance alone does not establish that cash was remitted.
