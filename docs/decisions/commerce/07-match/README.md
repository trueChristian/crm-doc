# Recalculate qualifying gifts sponsor capacity and cash shortfall

One appeal has one qualifying actual gift. A signed match pays the stated share of this eligible gift up to the remaining agreement cap. Only an actual sponsor payment enters the cash total. The donor then receives an approved partial return. The contract recalculates cumulative match on the net eligible base, rounding once. If the sponsor already remitted too much, route the excess to review for return, waiver or redirection. Never recast it as an extra gift without authority.

Calculate the qualifying earned match before and after the return. Independently verify outstanding minus excess = recalculated match minus independently received sponsor cash. Reconciled cash remains net donor gift plus actual sponsor remittance until cash is actually returned. Influencers cannot add another cash amount.

One gift 1,000, cap 400, match 50% gives 400 earned. Returning 300 leaves 700 eligible, thus 350 earned. If 380 from the sponsor is already in bank, the review excess is 30 and cash held is 1,080.
