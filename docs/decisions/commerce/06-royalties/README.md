# Apply a licensed sale royalty and recoverable advance

A licensed course edition is already sold under a valid paid-use permission. Contract terms pay a percentage of actual unit sale proceeds net of merchandise discounts and completed unit refunds, excluding tax, gifts, delivery and processor fees. An unrecouped advance offsets earned royalty before any new payable. Rounding is once on the cumulative settlement basis. The rights-holder report is separately authorized, with no purchaser names or viewing histories.

Independently compute period earning by summing a constant net price over retained units and by subtracting the original price and discount for refunded units. Apply the lesser of advance outstanding and cumulative earnings to the advance. New payable + advance recoupment must equal period earnings. Advance recoupment does not change the church sale amount.

Four copies sold at 1,000 less 100 per copy and one copy refunded leave 2,700 eligible. A 12.5% contracted royalty rounds 337.5 to 338. With 200 unrecouped, 200 offsets the advance and 138 is newly payable. A later return after settlement requires an authorized adjustment linked to the original accounting period.
