# Measure sponsor entitlement after a cost disallowance

The signed grant pays a specified percentage of eligible cost up to its ceiling. Cash actually drawn can be an advance. Ineligible costs remain expenses of the church. The sponsor later disallows part of the previously eligible base. Under this fixture all remaining conditions have been verified. Closeout requires payment of any remaining receivable or return/approved offset of excess cash. One row is one independent award.

Calculate entitlement from the revised eligible base and cap. Check a cash bridge: cash already drawn + receivable − refundable = revised earned. Compare old earned to new earned to obtain the correction. A reduction in unearned headroom alone creates no loss or payment. Historical sponsor submissions remain reproducible.

Cost 5,000, sponsor 80%, ceiling 5,000 and cash 4,000 gives 4,000 earned. Disallow 250 of cost: revised entitlement 3,800, refundable 200, no receivable. A signed waiver changes the evidence and should be another decision.
