# Refund specific units to their original tenders

All items in the basket are identical and the stated unit price is the accepted tax-inclusive gross. The voucher can pay for merchandise only. Apply it to ordered units in ordinal order. The card pays the rest plus the entire voluntary gift. Merchandise is returned from the last unit backwards. The gift remains designated and untouched. Each row is a new order and a single approved partial or full merchandise return.

For each row, build tender allocations per unit and sum only the returned units. Independently compute the result from the remaining unreturned merchandise capacity. Initial captures less their refunds must equal unreturned merchandise plus gift. Original-method and original-unit ceilings both apply.

Four units at 125 cost 500. A voucher of 300 covers 125, 125, 50 and 0 by ordinal. Returning the last two credits the voucher 50 and card 200. A gift of 19 stays on the card.
