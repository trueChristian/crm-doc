## Reconciliation

Adjusted bank equals statement less signed confirmed bank error plus outstanding deposits minus outstanding payments. Correct books by debiting the bank charge/crediting bank for fees and debiting bank/crediting interest for earnings. A signed bookkeeping discrepancy requires investigation even when only one cent. Here sign means the arithmetic gate passed; independent signoff remains required. Source dates or outstanding cases can still require evidence.
