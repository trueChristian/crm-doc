# Giving authority, designations and corrections

Each row is an independent decision in the same church and original currency. A giving clerk has intake permission and a separate reviewer has correction authority. The reporting policy counts verified receipts and applies confirmed corrections by their effective date.

| Starting fact | Evidence presented | Decision and observable result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unnamed cash of 18 has no designation | Approved default purpose is General | Verify 18 to General without a donor profile. Include it in the permitted aggregate. There is no recipient for personal correspondence. |
| Unidentified envelope of 18 says “widows' relief” | Purpose closed the previous week | Hold designation pending contact or approved disposition. A General default for unmarked gifts cannot overwrite a written direction. |
| Identified donor gave 40 for youth | Ministry leader asks for names to send a thank-you | Apply recognition and correspondence authority for this audience. An aggregate-only leader sees the permitted total and no name. A giving clerk can arrange privately authorized thanks. |
| A gives 30 and A and B jointly give 50 | Both authorize joint correspondence only on the 50 | Joint history is 50. A’s personal giving is 30. B’s access to past joint correspondence does not disclose A’s personal amount. |
| Gift 120 contains 90 General and 30 Youth | Finance confirms a 20 return on the Youth line | Net is 90 General plus 10 Youth. Preserve original 120 and linked return. A 20 return referencing an unknown line is held. |
| The same Youth line has 10 remaining | A second independent return for 12 arrives | Hold the over-return for explicit resolution. Do not take 2 from General. A duplicate of the first 20 return has no further effect. |
| A return for 20 was previously applied | The provider confirms reinstatement of 6 against that return | Reinstate 6 on that allocation. Outstanding loss from that return is 14. A new reinstatement greater than 14 is held. |
| Donor asks to move 15 from General to Missions | Original authority verified, sufficient remaining General, receiving purpose open | Preserve the original allocation and an approved movement of 15. General decreases and Missions increases by 15. Total net giving stays constant. |
| An organization gift has four authorized tribute names | An official receipt issuer supplies one valid organization receipt | Store one issuer reference. A permitted organization contact may receive it. Tributes remain recognition records. |
| A documented gift is corrected after a statement was issued | Reviewer approves the effective correction | Original edition remains available subject to authority. New edition references the original, preserving the observed old and new values. |
