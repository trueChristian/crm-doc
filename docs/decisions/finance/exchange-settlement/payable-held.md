## Reconciliation

Close carrying is an outstanding monetary item, without a new movement of foreign units. For a receivable lifetime gain is total cash collected minus original base; for a payable it is original base minus total cash paid. Sum both settlement gains, the cutoff valuation and its optional reversal to reach that same signed result.
