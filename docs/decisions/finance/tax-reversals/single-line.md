## Reconciliation

At issue debit receivable for invoice gross; credit net line revenues and each assessed tax liability. At approved credit debit sales adjustment and the original tax liabilities for the credited components and credit customer balance/refund payable. Gross after credit plus credit gross always equals original gross. A later credit uses remaining original amounts; a final full reversal absorbs any prior-cent residual.
