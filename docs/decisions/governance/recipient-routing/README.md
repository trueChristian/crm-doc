# Channel preference, opt-out and last-minute authority changes

[All decision profiles](../README.md) · [Clerk procedure](../../../handbook/governance/contact-routing.md)

These dispatches require a named individual. A channel subscriber with no verified direct-chat binding cannot receive a private Telegram message. A ready endpoint means already verified, purpose-authorized and within its delivery window. A shared endpoint has been separately assessed under the policy stated on the profile.

## Reproduce the cases

1. Check active recipient authority, then inspect the ordered eligible routes. A blocked, unverified, stale or declined endpoint is unavailable. A person following a channel has no verified direct binding solely for that reason.
2. Reevaluate before provider handoff after the stated change. Do not substitute a public channel announcement. If the purpose's authority ends, suppress every route.
3. Compare original and latest decisions. A newly permitted fallback becomes a separately tracked route. Pending privacy or delivery exceptions disclose only the reason the operator may see. Profiles with no permitted route do not generate sends.
