# Zero decisive or cast votes in a quorate meeting

[Decision family](README.md) · [Decision arithmetic](../../../handbook/governance/motions-and-weights.md)

In each independent case, 12 voters are eligible, eight are present and the fixed quorum is six. All have one vote. Four absent voters cast nothing. An abstention is explicitly cast; an unreturned vote from someone present is not cast. A zero denominator makes the threshold undefined under these profiles. The chair records an undetermined outcome and follows the adopted referral or new-round procedure. No auto-passage, declared defeat or fictitious percentage is allowed for that state.

| ZERO-0004 | 0 | 0 | 8 | 0 | At least three-quarters of all eligible rights | 12 | 9 | Lost |
| ZERO-0005 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 8 | Strict majority of cast votes including abstentions | 0 | undefined | Undetermined; refer for disposition |
| ZERO-0006 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 8 | Strict majority of all present | 8 | 5 | Lost |
| ZERO-0007 | 1 | 0 | 7 | 0 | Strict majority of decisive votes | 1 | 1 | Carried |
| ZERO-0008 | 0 | 1 | 7 | 0 | Strict majority of decisive votes | 1 | 1 | Lost |

For each row reconcile yes + no + abstain + present unreturned = 8. Select the denominator from decisive, cast, present or eligible before applying any threshold arithmetic. A nonzero denominator with insufficient affirmative votes produces a fully determined loss. Keep “undetermined” distinct from failure of quorum; every example here retains quorum. Reissuing an undetermined poll creates a separate round and preserves the original counts.
