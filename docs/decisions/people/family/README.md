# Household presentation and authority at point of use

[All people decision cases](../README.md)

All times are local in May 2028, in day hour:minute notation. The initial example grant covers the named activity in East and West from 1 May 09:00 inclusive until 20 May 18:00 exclusive. A reviewed revision is evaluated alongside that baseline. “Revoke on review” ends the grant at 10 May 15:00. “Restriction confirmed” denies from that instant. “Scope narrowed” limits West while East continues. “Restricted interval” denies only until 15 May 09:00. “Extension approved” extends the original until 25 May 18:00. “Verification pending” allows only after verification on 15 May 09:00. Household projection assumes the ordinary permitted viewer; safety overrides and specific field consent still apply. Guardian/kinship labels do not confer a grant.

Decision order is current scoped restriction, then verified applicable grant, then neutral referral. Both consent-to-attend and physical release can have valid independent checks. A second evaluator reconstructs the revised allow and deny intervals and checks the current result at every instant.
