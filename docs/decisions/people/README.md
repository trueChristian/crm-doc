## How to use a row

1. Open a family's policy and the relevant chapter or band. Set the church's test configuration to the example policy and use only the declared starting facts. The row's later correction is hidden from the original decision.
2. Act as the stated reader or reviewer on the specified date. Record the authority, policy version, evidence condition and expected outcome. Run any correction, return or review through its owning operation.
3. Check both original and corrected outcomes, including absence of data leakage and absence of duplicate fulfillment. Rows are independent and can be executed in any order. A correct headcount does not certify the identity, consent or protection status of an individual.
4. Compare calculated results with the business invariants. A chart, spoken announcement or narrative note cannot replace the relevant permission, receipt, edition or verified action.

## Verification of this edition

The seven families contain 17,873 distinct case identifiers across 99 case tables, each with at most 320 cases. The published decision cells were reread and independently checked. Eligibility uses the equivalent anniversary and possible-date calculations; historical roll counts reconcile with standing and signed movements. Scoped grants and restrictions resolve against their effective windows. Disclosures distinguish eligibility from treatment of earlier distribution. Award totals reconcile by amount and unit. Appeal cutoffs are checked by independently counting eligible days and comparing the filing instant; the displayed remedy preserves unrelated issues. Field visibility is verified against its expected display, search, print or retrieval behavior.
