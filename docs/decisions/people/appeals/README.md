# Appeal windows, recusal and issue-specific remedies

[All people decision cases](../README.md)

All example dates are in 2028 church local time. The start day is excluded; the last counted date is included until exactly 17:00. The five- and ten-service-day profiles count Monday–Friday excluding 1, 4 and 18 May in this fictional service calendar. The seven-day profile counts each civil day, including weekends/closures. An independently approved extension of two days means two additional eligible days under the same policy. Example dates are case service times, not jurisdictional deadlines. The original decision remains operative while timeliness or recusal is reviewed unless a separately authorized interim stay exists. Each appeal has one disputed issue and retains the unaffected issue.

The filing clock starts from the evidenced safe notice. No human decision follows solely from the calendar. A late filing moves to an independently authorized late-acceptance review, and a conflicted reviewer cannot resolve it. Independent enumeration and business-day stepping verify the due date; direct date and clock comparison checks the inclusive cutoff.
