# Purpose-specific care disclosures and withdrawals

[All people decision cases](../README.md)

The tested edition opens 4 May at 09:00, is withdrawn 7 May at 14:00 and would otherwise expire 9 May at noon, in one church time zone. “Earlier authorized” describes a prior distribution with its own valid permission, even when the current edition has not opened. Each named revision is a reviewed change to scope or content, never a silent expansion. A second identified person requires specific authority or a genuinely nonidentifying rewrite. An organization-wide restriction or unsafe endpoint overrides a content approval. Printed copies already lawfully distributed cannot be electronically recalled.

Implementations must evaluate the full accepted audience, purpose, channel, exact wording, third-party authority, route safety, protected-field policy and effective interval at send or retrieval. The displayed first reason is for an authorized reviewer; public responses remain neutral. Each result is validated against an independent all-conditions predicate.
