# Membership decisions on two time axes

[All people decision cases](../README.md)

All example dates are civil dates. Church A and B lie in one authorized federation. A full or associate affiliation counts formally; suspension keeps formal status and removes voting. Only F votes. A means associate, S means full with voting suspended, N means no qualifying membership at that church. A combined unique count includes a person once when either affiliation qualifies. A roll has one unit per person. All other eligibility rules are satisfied. Original and corrected information are known in their respective runs; the original certified edition remains unchanged. Numeric cells are this person’s contribution to the population, not a congregation total.

An independent ledger of plus/minus entries verifies the two point-in-time counts and their difference. Boundary selections cover dates immediately before and at both effective dates, the intervening period and cancellation. A real summary reconciles opening balance plus all applicable movements to the closing total.
