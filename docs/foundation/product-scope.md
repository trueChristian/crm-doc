# What the church manages and what it exchanges

Begin with the congregation's work. A ministry group, household, meeting, gift and booking each have an accountable owner. A role such as secretary may operate several desks in one church. It still matters whether the person is correcting a contact address, certifying the roll or asking a treasurer to investigate a missing receipt. An integration uses the authority of the desk responsible for the action.

## When systems meet

| Boundary | Church CRM decision | Other owner and evidence returned | If no connection is configured |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accounting and payroll | Approve a gift designation, event-fee allocation, aid authorization, spending ceiling or time record. Send permitted source facts and track discrepancies. | The church's accounting service owns journals, accounts, payroll calculation, taxes, financial statements and bank reconciliation. The CRM records the external references, statuses and approved report editions relevant to church work. | Use an authorized manual handoff or export. Show pending or unverified until an authorized acknowledgement is matched. The register and ministry desks continue to operate. |
| Formal learning | Schedule a church group, record attendance, request an external learning status when authorized and decide assignment eligibility under church policy. | The LMS owns curriculum, content, assessments, grades, academic enrolment, progress and academic awards. A verified attestation can supply evidence for a church decision. | Operate class meetings and rosters locally. Accept independently verified evidence where church policy permits. Missing evidence leaves the assignment pending. |
| Payment, shop or media service | Allocate specified amounts to places or gifts, record a fulfilment referral, and approve the audience and rights for a church publication. | The provider owns checkout, payment processing, commercial returns, stock valuation or playback infrastructure as applicable. A callback identifies the exact component or release affected. | Keep the church-side reservation, request or rights decision; use a manual route if available and distinguish its confirmation from the request. |
| Congregation website, mobile app and authorized MCP client | The same church record owner validates each command and grants limited reads. An app may submit an enquiry, request registration or fetch its user's rota. | The client presents the result and handles any pending operation, denied access, correction or expired delegation. | Reception or the church's own staff interface can submit the same action and record the actual operator. |
| Independent churches and regional bodies | Each church approves the permitted fields, recipient, purpose, time limit and correction route. | A receiving registrar makes a local admission decision. A district can acknowledge a certified statistical return without opening member records. | A signed extract can travel by an authorized route; staff log its custodian, issue and acceptance. |

A new requirement belongs here when a church officer can name the decision, its subject, the accountable record and an observable outcome. If the action changes an external system's record, document the request, acknowledgement, correction and outage route here. Put the provider's own operational detail in its documentation. See the [integration requirements](../platform/church-apps-and-integrations.md) and [business commands](../handbook/business-commands.md).
