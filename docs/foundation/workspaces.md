# Everyday workspaces and findability

Each workspace opens on the next piece of work for the signed-in person. An officeholder can switch roles or congregations. Show the active scope, reporting date, freshness and any outstanding approvals. Always provide a path back from a total to the authorized underlying rows or the explanation for suppression.

| Workspace | First screen and common actions | Visibility and exceptions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visitor, occasional attendee | Discover public service times, directions, accessibility and language, register interest, book a public event, ask for contact or decline it. | Support anonymous browsing and a guest route. A visitor form never publishes a household or enrolls someone as a member. |
| Member or household delegate | Review own record, contact preferences, next event, serving request, giving statement if authorized, and published notices or minutes. Request a change or nominate a delegate. | Clearly identify whose record is being viewed. One adult cannot edit another adult's confidential details by virtue of living at the same address. |
| Secretary or registrar | Work through possible duplicates, admissions, transfers, address changes and returned messages. Prepare a dated roll or certificate, print correspondence, and locate a decision by subject and date. | Show planned, effective and former statuses separately. Keep private care case content out of register lists. |
| Pastor or care coordinator | See assigned requests, safe contact routes, permission to share, appointment dates and overdue follow-ups. Reassign when someone is absent. | The minimum shared task can say “contact assigned coordinator” while the narrative remains sealed. |
| Ministry or worship leader | See next session, roster gaps, attendance sheet, room, substitutions and people awaiting a response. | Show only assigned groups and events. Credentials can appear as “eligible until” without the underlying evidence. |
| Child check-in or reception steward | Select occurrence, locate participant, validate pickup instructions, request duty lead help, reconcile people still checked in. | Display only operational details. Time-limit station access and expire printable codes. |
| Clerk, chair or board member | Prepare a notice or agenda pack, check voting eligibility, record conflicts, approve minutes and follow up actions. | Provide member and restricted versions of the same issued pack with explicit audience labels. |
| Giving steward, treasurer or independent reviewer | Check an offering count, review donor restrictions, resolve mismatched acknowledgements and attach an accountant-approved summary to the annual return. | Show the responsible reviewer and age of each pending handoff. Public reports disclose only the authorized released totals. |
| Administrator, auditor or support operator | Review grants, configuration changes, failed transfers, export requests and recovery status. | Administrative access and permission to read a pastoral note or individual gift require separate evaluation. |
| Bookstall, media and communications teams | Route an enquiry to the named provider, approve church media releases, preview a bulletin and see failed deliveries. | Contact for one event or order has a distinct purpose from a subscription. A public broadcast contains only approved material. |

## Interactions that every workspace supports

- Search by the terms people actually use, including former names, household names, ministry labels and decision numbers. Suggestions, recent items, snippets and result counts apply access checks before display. Distinguish a stale index from a genuinely empty result.
- Lists retain filters, date, scope, selected sort and accessible pagination. A batch action previews both the selected count and what will be excluded. Show the actual recipients or eligible rows only to people permitted to inspect them.
- Profile summaries disclose the source and date of each fact. Buttons name a concrete outcome, such as “request family correction”, “issue statement” or “approve minutes”. Sensitive or irreversible steps identify their subject and effect before confirmation.
- Printed and low-bandwidth routes support attendance, roll extracts, meeting packs, emergency contact sheets and receipts. Mark issue time, audience and copy status. Assign a custodian and collection or disposal date to restricted offline copies.
- Confirm success with a receipt or durable reference. Explain validation in the user's language, preserve valid entries and provide a path for a clerk to resolve a failed or duplicate submission. Staff assistance records the actual actor and the person they assisted.

## Usability acceptance

A first-time secretary given an applicant's former name can locate the correct person, read the dated membership history, prepare the roll at the last annual meeting and explain its count. A member using only a keyboard can decline one volunteer date and keep the rest. A screen reader announces the selected household subject, validation error and successful save. An intermittent connection at reception conveys “pending sync” until the attendance owner accepts the record. Test each journey with realistic language, names and permission differences.
