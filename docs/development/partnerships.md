# Partnerships, sponsorships and impact

A congregation may partner with a school to deliver meals, accept sponsorship of a community concert, host another church's project or share equipment. Begin with the existing MinistryPartner, MissionProject and ProjectPartnerRole. The relationship agreement defines what each party promises and what it may share. It does not give the partner access to the register, beneficiary names or private ministry notes.

## Operating rules

Plan measurable outputs with the programme lead before inviting sponsorship. Record expected activity, baseline, target, unit, collection method, period and evidence quality. Meals delivered, households reached and change in food security measure different things. For a shared project, mark joint attribution explicitly. Each partner may describe its role without the church summing duplicated outcomes. When a beneficiary cannot be identified safely, report contact occasions or quantities delivered and explain that a unique-person count is unavailable. Public progress information must avoid small-cell combinations that reveal a family, protected location or private donor amount.

For sponsorship containing both money and a supplied benefit, obtain the authorized split from the accounting office, link any external invoice reference, verify the gift portion with the giving desk and attach brand authorization. Governing decision-makers disclose and manage conflicts. Failure to deliver a promised benefit creates a remedy task, estimate and notification to finance, with an agreed credit, replacement or renegotiation. A pledged venue or hour of work can be evidenced operationally even when finance policy records no accounting contribution. A termination closes sharing permissions and future brand use, reconciles unfulfilled obligations and identifies records retained under the agreement.

## Acceptance cases

- A sponsor pays 1,000 for acknowledged advertising and separately designated support. Finance approves 300 as a sale and 700 as a contribution under the configured policy. The partner summary shows total support of 1,000 with its components. The sales provider issues its invoice and the designated issuer issues the permitted gift acknowledgement and the public appeal cash goal admits only its eligible gift allocation.
- A school and two churches deliver 240 meals to 60 households. Each partner sees its permitted joint project summary. Cross-project reporting shows one 240-meal observation and, where deduplication is supported, 60 households. No sponsor receives recipient contact details through the agreement.
- The church promises two logo placements and delivers one. The sponsor obligation remains partial until a second approved placement or an agreed alternate remedy. The finance reviewer receives any needed credit decision exactly once.
- A youth participant withdraws consent for a named photograph after publication. The publisher removes controlled uses, preserves a restricted record of the withdrawal and republishes an anonymized outcome narrative. The unchanged aggregate service count can remain where its evidence and privacy threshold still support it.
- A partner ends an agreement during a project. Existing delivery facts retain their original scope and dates. Future access, unfulfilled commitments, retained restricted funds, borrowed equipment and open sponsor reports each receive a named disposition owner.
