# Campaigns, audiences and content

A campaign brings related dispatches together, such as notices about a district conference. Each dispatch has a single purpose, permission scope, approved edition and delivery policy. A visit reminder can create a standalone dispatch without a campaign. Preview must show the exact audience criteria, qualifying time, exclusions, language variants, sample redacted messages, expected channels and estimated cost before release.

## Composition and review

Use structured content that supports headings, readable links, text alternatives and pronunciation-sensitive names without requiring a programming language. Field preview covers missing preferred names, multiple surnames, recipients without a title, right-to-left scripts, long dates, currency precision and alternate languages. For each required field choose a tested neutral fallback or route to review. A missing detail that changes meaning blocks sending. Protect account links and attachments to private records with current recipient access. A forwarded notification must not confer eligibility to open a directory, statement or restricted minute.

Distinguish a deduplicated person from a shared physical destination. If a recipient sings in the choir and serves as an elder, a single churchwide dispatch reaches that person once. If two adults share a telephone, each remains a distinct entitled party. Personal content requires approved addressing for each adult, with safe-channel checks. Only non-personal material may consolidate to one household copy where preferences and policy permit it. Delivery reports explain that consolidation without marking all family members as individual recipients.

Reviewers see an authorized sample and count by channel, expected SMS segments, translation gaps, inaccessible attachments, overlap with another campaign and required approval for targeted content. Permit an internal test to designated reviewers using fictional or minimised data, clearly labeled as a test, excluded from live delivery totals. On a material edit after approval, hold unissued recipients, preserve any issued edition, and prompt an explicit correction or withdrawal notice. Release can be staged by rate, time zone and spending limit without broadening the frozen audience.

## Checks for the communications lead

- Compose a newsletter for choir, youth leaders and a campus list; an overlap of 12 people is deduplicated and the reviewer can explain the final count.
- A translated service change lacks the corrected date. The configured reviewed fallback is used with an explicit language marker, or the send is held. An unreviewed generated translation cannot silently become an approved edition.
- An organizer attempts to target people by donation size with no giving access. Audience preview, the count and the send are denied without exposing whether any donors match.
- An editor changes a sentence after a reviewer signs. The earlier approval cannot authorize the new edition. Already emitted copies retain their original digest.
- For a confidential meeting, the SMS says a notice awaits in the member portal. The approved minute text remains accessible only after the recipient's current access is checked.
